Why an exam countdown helps you revise

Revision is easier to plan backwards from a number than from a date. "June 12" says very little in March; "83 days" tells you there are about twelve weeks, and how many topics each one has to cover. As the number drops, it also makes procrastination a little harder to ignore.

A countdown helps with the logistics too. With several exams in one season, seeing them in order with the days left for each makes it obvious which one needs your attention this week.

How to set up an exam countdown in Left

1. Add the exam

  1. Open Left and go to Ahead.
  2. Tap Add a date ahead (or +) and search for "exam". The Exam template tracks the start time of an exam or assessment, counts in hours and reminds you 2 days before. There are also templates for a Certification test, a Driving test, a Dissertation deadline and a Course end.
  3. Set Starts to the date and time the exam begins and Until to when it finishes.
  4. Under More options, use Show as... to pick the unit. Weeks or days suit an exam that is months away; hours feel right in the final week.
  5. Save.

If your timetable is already in your calendar, choose From calendar instead and pick the exam from the list, so the date and time come across as they are.

2. Add the widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  2. Search for Left and add the Ahead widget for one exam, or the Time Ahead list for several.
  3. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose the exam under Select date.

On the Lock Screen, add the Ahead widget in its circular or rectangular size.

A whole exam season at a glance

The Time Ahead list widget shows up to 3 dates in the Medium size and up to 7 in Large, each in its own unit. Put a whole exam season in it and you see every paper in order. In the app, sort Ahead by Closest so the next exam is always first, and archive each one once it is done.

Reminders and the morning of the exam

Each exam can have a reminder a month, two weeks, a week, two days or a day before, at the time you choose. On the day, turn on Get ready for the start and Left shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island for the 4 hours before the exam begins, so you know exactly how long you have for breakfast, the commute and a last look at your notes.

Turn the countdown into a revision plan

A countdown tells you how long you have; the rest of Left helps you use it. In Since you can build a revision Habit, such as one hour a day on weekdays, with up to 4 reminders. With the Habit widget set to its Text layout, you can log a session straight from the Home Screen. An Aim counts up towards a target, such as 20 past papers before the exam, with an optional deadline.

Questions

How do I add an exam countdown to my iPhone Home Screen?

Add the exam in Left’s Ahead section, using the Exam template or From calendar. Then touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, choose Add Widget, search for Left and add the Ahead widget. Touch and hold it, tap Edit Widget and pick the exam under Select date.

Can I see several exams at once?

Yes. The Time Ahead list widget shows up to 3 dates in Medium and up to 7 in Large, each in its own unit. The free plan includes 2 countdowns; Left Premium removes the limit.

Can the countdown show hours instead of days?

Yes. Each date has a Show as... setting for hours, days, weeks, months or years. The Exam template counts in hours by default.

Can I import my exam timetable from my calendar?

Yes. When you add a date, choose From calendar and pick the exam event. Its date and time are imported.

Will it remind me before the exam?

Yes. Turn on Get a reminder and choose 1 month, 2 weeks, 1 week, 2 days or 1 day before. The Exam template sets a reminder 2 days before. On the day, Get ready for the start shows a Live Activity for the last 4 hours.

Can I put the exam countdown on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Add Left’s Ahead widget to the Lock Screen in its circular or rectangular size and choose the exam.
