Best Relationship Counter Apps for iPhone in 2026: Days Together, Compared

Updated November 7, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public search data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

A relationship counter shows how long you've been together: days, months, years, sometimes down to the second. Most also count down to the next anniversary and mark milestones such as day 100 or day 1,000. Some are simple counters for one phone; others are full couples apps that both partners install. Here are seven worth comparing on iPhone.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best for days together and the next anniversary on your Lock Screen, with no ads: Left.
  • Best simple counter with a single Pro upgrade: LoveCounter - My Love.
  • Best for milestones, quotes and a Face ID lock: My Love: Relationship Counter.
  • Best for D+100 milestones and a private shared diary: The Couple (Days in Love).
  • Best full couples app that you both install: Couple Joy.
  • Best for many anniversaries and automatic milestones: Anniversary Tracker.
  • Best photo widget with a shared diary: Couple Widget: Love Countdown.

1. Left: days together and the next anniversary, at a glance

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. It isn't a couples app; it's an app for the dates and counts that matter to you, which makes it a clean relationship counter. Two pieces do the job:

  • Days together, as a streak: in Since, create a streak and set Started on... to the day you got together; any past date works, and you can add a time later by editing it. It counts up by itself, and the Streak widget can show it in hours, days, weeks, months or years.
  • The next anniversary, as an Ahead date: in Ahead, add the date, set Repeat to Yearly and it rolls on to the next year by itself. Add a photo from your library or Unsplash, and a reminder 1 month, 2 weeks, 1 week, 2 days or 1 day before.
  • Widgets: the Streak widget comes in small, medium and large with a photo and the start date, and as a rectangular Lock Screen widget. The Ahead widget counts down on the Home Screen and as a circular or rectangular Lock Screen widget. Both work in StandBy, and both are free.
  • Reminders: a streak can remind you on the days you choose how long it has been, and a countdown can switch to Get the final countdown, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Left Premium extras: Joint Ahead dates, shared with your partner so the anniversary appears in their Left too, plus unlimited items.

Best for: one person who wants days together on the Lock Screen and the next anniversary counting down, without ads or a social feed.

Price: free to download, with no ads. The free plan includes 2 streaks and 2 countdowns (plus 2 habits and 2 Aims), which covers a days-together counter and an anniversary. The count includes every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited items and Joint Ahead dates. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: free Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; no ads; counts in any unit from hours to years; anniversary Live Activities; the same app shows the rest of your countdowns and year progress.

Cons: no automatic milestone list such as day 100 or day 1,000; no shared diary or couple chat; only 2 free streaks and 2 free countdowns; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later; no Apple Watch app.

2. LoveCounter - My Love

By Dennis Wassenberg. Its App Store page describes a counter from years down to seconds, "a live countdown to your next anniversary", milestones "like your 1,000th or 5,000th day together", your names and a photo of the two of you, and shareable cards. Version 2.0 in August 2026 was "rebuilt from the ground up", and 2.1 added an Apple Watch app.

Best for: a clean counter plus anniversary countdown, with one upgrade for widgets and the Watch.

Price: free with ads. One LoveCounter Pro purchase ($1.99 in the US) adds multiple special dates, Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, reminders, colors, the Apple Watch app and no ads.

Pros: recently rebuilt; Watch app and complications; the only competitor listing here that names StandBy; a single low-cost upgrade.

Cons: widgets and reminders need Pro; no iPad in its compatibility list; its privacy label lists usage data used to track you.

3. My Love: Relationship Counter

By Mst. Jahanara Sultana. Its App Store page describes counting "every year, month, day, hour, minute and second", "Milestones tracked automatically: 100 days, 1 year, 1,000 days and more", monthly and yearly anniversary notifications, a Home Screen and Lock Screen widget, unlimited special dates, a passcode and Face ID lock, iCloud backup and more than 10,000 love quotes.

Best for: automatic milestones and a locked, private counter.

Price: free with ads. "MY LOVE PREMIUM: Unlock unlimited photos, all backgrounds and themes, all quote packs and remove ads." Premium, yearly Premium, a one-time purchase and several smaller unlocks are listed.

Pros: milestones built in; Lock Screen widget; Face ID lock; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs.

Cons: ads in the free version and many separate purchases; Apple Watch isn't listed; its privacy label lists identifiers used to track you.

Not to be confused with My Love - Relationship Counter by Bjoern Bartels; see the note below.

4. The Couple (Days in Love)

By TheDayBefore, Inc. Its App Store page says it can "Auto-track every couple milestone from day one" and "Calculate D+100, D+200, 1-year, 2-year anniversaries and more", with reminders for birthdays and anniversaries, Home and Lock Screen widgets in several sizes, and a Couple Diary where you "Upload photos, add captions, and comment together".

Best for: couples who like D+100 style milestones and want a small private diary for two.

Price: free with ads. Premium, offered monthly, every 6 months, yearly or for life, adds "Ad-free experience", more photos per story, themes, photo backup and lock.

Pros: milestones calculated for you; Home and Lock Screen widgets; Apple Watch in its compatibility list; updated in October 2026.

Cons: iPad isn't listed; ads in the free version; its privacy label lists identifiers and usage data used to track you.

5. Couple Joy

By HEARTBIT S.R.L. Its App Store page describes a relationship app for two: "Celebrate your love by tracking how long you've been together", countdowns to "birthdays, anniversaries, and exciting future plans", couple widgets, daily questions, quizzes, a shared journal, a distance tracker and daily streaks. Its latest version lets you send text and drawings to your partner's Home Screen.

Best for: couples who both want an app together, with the counter as one feature among many.

Price: free to download; Couple Joy Premium and Streak Repair purchases are listed. The listing doesn't say what the free version includes.

Pros: 4.9 stars from 39,379 ratings, the most of the current apps here; frequent updates; lots to do together.

Cons: it's built for both partners to join ("invite your partner today"); iPad and Apple Watch aren't listed; its privacy label lists purchases, identifiers and usage data used to track you, and location, contact info and other data linked to you.

6. Anniversary Tracker

By Pocket Grove Ltd. Its App Store page shows "how many days, months, and years you've been together", lets you choose "whether Day 1 starts on your anniversary date or the day after", and generates milestones automatically: "100 days, 200 days, 500 days, 1,000 days, monthly anniversaries, Valentine's Day, Christmas Eve, and more". You can add unlimited anniversaries, birthdays and special dates, with notifications days in advance.

Best for: keeping many anniversaries and milestones in one list, for a partner, a pet or a big day.

Price: free with ads; one Remove all ads purchase is listed.

Pros: a long list of automatic milestones; unlimited dates; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs; updated in October 2026.

Cons: its description doesn't cover widgets, though its release notes added Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in 2024 and 2025; ads unless you pay; its privacy label lists identifiers used to track you and contact info linked to you.

7. Couple Widget: Love Countdown

By Jae Young Kim. Its App Store page describes "A real-time Days Together counter", a photo widget for your Home Screen, reminders "for 10/100 days, the day before, and other important dates", a shared couple's diary and a couple calendar for anniversaries.

Best for: a couple photo on the Home Screen with days together on top.

Price: free, with ads according to its App Store page; the page lists no in-app purchases.

Pros: photo widget built around the counter; shared diary; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs.

Cons: Lock Screen widgets aren't mentioned; recent release notes don't describe changes; its privacy label lists identifiers used to track you.

What about My Love by Bjoern Bartels?

My Love - Relationship Counter is by far the most-rated relationship counter we found, with 4.8 stars from 1,048,607 US ratings. It "creates new anniversaries like 'the 333rd day', 'the 50th month'", takes a photo of the two of you, and its privacy label says Data Not Collected. A Home Screen widget comes with a $0.99 My Love Pro purchase. We left it out of the list because its last update was in May 2023. If it still works well for you, there's no need to switch.

Questions

What is the best app to count days together?

For a simple counter, LoveCounter - My Love and My Love: Relationship Counter are well rated and current. Left suits you if you want days together and the next anniversary on your Lock Screen with free widgets and no ads. If you both want a full couples app, Couple Joy is the most rated current option.

Is the My Love app still updated?

My Love - Relationship Counter by Bjoern Bartels was last updated in May 2023, according to its App Store page checked on 20 October 2026. My Love: Relationship Counter by Mst. Jahanara Sultana is a different app and was updated in September 2026.

Can I put our days together on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Left's Streak widget shows days together as a rectangular Lock Screen widget for free. LoveCounter - My Love lists Lock Screen widgets with Pro, My Love: Relationship Counter and The Couple list Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and Anniversary Tracker's release notes added Lock Screen widgets in 2025.

Which relationship counter apps count down to our anniversary?

LoveCounter - My Love shows a live countdown to your next anniversary, Couple Joy and The Couple count down to anniversaries, and Anniversary Tracker generates milestones such as 100 and 1,000 days. In Left, an Ahead date set to repeat yearly counts down to every anniversary.

Do we both need to install a relationship counter app?

Not for most. As far as their listings say, LoveCounter, My Love: Relationship Counter and Anniversary Tracker don't need a partner to join, and neither does Left. Couple Joy is built for both partners to join, and The Couple and Couple Widget have shared diaries. In Left, sharing an anniversary with your partner as a Joint Ahead date needs Left Premium.
