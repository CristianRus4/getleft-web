Game Release Countdown on iPhone: How to Track Every Launch Date

Updated December 19, 2026 · 6 min read

A big game launch is one of the few dates people count down to by the hour. You know the day months ahead, the date slips at least once, and on launch night the real question is "what time does it unlock where I live?" A good game release countdown on iPhone handles all three: the long wait, the delays and the final hours.

This guide covers how to set one up properly, why the unlock time matters more than the date, how to keep several upcoming games tidy on one screen, and a worked setup in Left.

Why the release time matters more than the date

Most store pages show a release date, but games rarely unlock at midnight in your own time zone. Publishers tend to use one of two patterns:

  • Simultaneous global launch. The game unlocks at the same moment everywhere, for example 15:00 UTC. That's morning in Los Angeles, evening in Berlin and past midnight in Sydney. Players in some regions get it "a day early" by the calendar.
  • Rolling midnight launch. The game unlocks at midnight local time in each region, so New Zealand and Australia play first and the Americas last.

Pre-load windows, early-access editions and console versus PC launches can each have their own times too. So before you set a countdown, find the official unlock time from the publisher or the platform's store and convert it to your time zone. Our time zone planner makes the conversion quick. Then count down to that exact hour, not to the date.

A countdown that lets you switch units, and shows a live minute-by-minute count on launch day, covers the whole wait.

Set up a game release countdown in Left

  1. Open Left, go to Ahead and tap Add a date ahead.
  2. Choose New date and name it with the game and platform, for example "Game title (PS5)". If the launch is already in your calendar, choose From calendar instead.
  3. Set Starts to the unlock date and time in your time zone, not just the day.
  4. Tap the preview card and choose Add image to use key art you've saved to Photos, or pick a symbol.
  5. Under More options, set Show as… to weeks while launch is far away. Switch to days in the last month.
  6. Still under More options, turn on Get ready for the start. In the hours before the unlock time, a Live Activity counts down on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  7. Save, then add the Ahead widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen and pick the game.

If you set the time wrong, nothing else matters, so double-check the unlock hour once the publisher confirms it, usually in the final weeks.

Tracking several upcoming games

Release calendars get crowded in the autumn. A few ways to keep them readable:

  • One list widget for everything. Left's Time Ahead list widget shows up to 7 dates at once, each in its own unit, so next week's launch in days and next year's in months sit side by side.
  • One headline game. Give the launch you care about most the Lock Screen widget or a medium Home Screen widget with its key art.
  • Sort by closest. In Ahead, sort by closest so the next launch is always on top.
  • Archive after launch. Once you're playing, archive the date so the list only shows what's still coming.
  • Count down with friends. If you're planning a launch-night session, a Joint Ahead date (Left Premium) puts the same countdown in your friends' Ahead too.

When the release date slips

Delays are part of the wait. Treat a countdown as a living thing:

  • Use the season until there's a date. If a game says "spring 2027", set a placeholder at the start of that window and rename it "Spring 2027 (TBC)" so you remember it's not final.
  • Edit, don't delete. When a new date lands, edit the existing countdown. Your image and settings stay.
  • Re-check the time near launch. Unlock times are often announced only a couple of weeks before.

Make launch night easier

A countdown is more satisfying when the boring parts are done early:

  • Set a second date for the pre-load. Pre-loads often open a few days early. A separate countdown reminds you to start the download while you sleep.
  • Clear storage the weekend before. Big games and day-one patches need a lot of space.
  • Book the time. If launch lands on a work night, block the evening in your calendar or ask for the next morning off well before the day.
  • Keep the wait visible, not stressful. A widget you glance at is fun. Refreshing store pages all evening isn't.

Questions

How do I make a countdown for a game release on iPhone?

Use a countdown app with widgets. In Left, add the game in Ahead, set Starts to the unlock date and time in your time zone, add a photo or symbol, and place the Ahead widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

What time do new games usually unlock?

It depends on the publisher. Some launch at the same moment worldwide, which can fall on a different calendar day where you live; others unlock at midnight local time in each region. Check the official announcement and convert the time to your time zone.

Can I count down to the exact hour, not just the day?

Yes. Set a time on the date, and in Left turn on Get ready for the start so a Live Activity counts the final hours and minutes on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

How do I track several upcoming games at once?

Use a list widget. Left's Time Ahead list widget shows up to 7 dates, each in its own unit, and you can sort Ahead by closest so the next launch is always first.

What should I do if a game gets delayed?

Edit the existing countdown with the new date and time rather than deleting it, so your image and settings stay. Until a firm date is announced, use the start of the release window and mark it as not final in the name.
