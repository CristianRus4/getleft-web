What Time Does a Business Day Start and End?

Updated November 9, 2026 · 5 min read

In the US and most of Europe, a business day usually starts at 9:00 AM and ends at 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday, in the local time zone, excluding public holidays. That's the convention most people mean when they say "business hours". But it is a convention, not a law, and the details change what "by end of business day" or "within 3 business days" actually means for you.

Below: typical business hours by country and industry, how banks and shippers count business days, how to read common deadline phrases, and how to count business days quickly.

Typical business hours

These are common patterns, not rules. Individual companies, banks and offices set their own hours.

What counts as a business day

A business day is any day that isn't a weekend or a public holiday where the business is. That last part matters: a holiday in the sender's country may not be one in the recipient's, and some holidays are regional.

  • Banks usually count Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays, and often have a daily cut-off time. A payment sent after the cut-off is treated as sent the next business day.
  • Shipping and delivery estimates in business days usually start counting the day after an order ships, not the day you order.
  • Contracts and legal notices define business days in the document or by law. If a deadline has real consequences, read the definition, or ask.

How to read common deadline phrases

PhraseUsually meansSafe assumption
"End of business day" (EOB, EOD)Close of business, often 5 PM5 PM in the requester's time zone, unless stated
"Close of business" (COB)When the office closesSame as above; banks may use an earlier cut-off
"Within 3 business days"Three weekdays, excluding holidaysStart counting from the next business day
"Next business day"The next weekday that isn't a holidayFriday's "next business day" is Monday, or Tuesday after a holiday
"By start of business"Before the office opensBefore 9 AM local time

Time zones are the most common source of confusion. "End of day Friday" from a colleague in New York is 2 PM in Los Angeles and 10 PM in London. When it matters, ask which time zone, or name one yourself: "by 5 PM Eastern on Friday".

Questions

What time does a business day start?

In the US and much of Europe, usually 9:00 AM local time, Monday to Friday. Some offices start at 8:00 AM, and banks and shops set their own hours.

What time does a business day end?

Typically 5:00 PM local time in the US, sometimes 5:30 or 6:00 PM elsewhere. "End of business day" usually means 5 PM in the time zone of whoever set the deadline, unless they say otherwise.

Is Saturday a business day?

Usually not. Most businesses, banks and shipping estimates count Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Some industries and countries work different weeks.

How do I count 3 business days?

Start from the next business day and count three weekdays, skipping weekends and public holidays. From a Thursday, 3 business days lands on Tuesday. Our business days from a date tool does it for you.

Do business days include holidays?

No. Public holidays are normally excluded, but which holidays count depends on the country, region and business.
