7 Best Color Widgets Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated November 11, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

Color Widgets is one of the most popular ways to make an iPhone Home Screen look good, with 4.6 stars from 526,929 ratings on the US App Store. It combines ready-made widgets, whole themes, icon packs and wallpapers with a simple editor for colors, fonts and photos. People look for an alternative when they want a different style, fewer ads, a different price model, or widgets that do one job well. Here are seven alternatives, including ours, and when each one fits.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best if you mainly used countdown, clock or progress widgets: Left.
  • Best for simple, polished customization: Widgetsmith.
  • Best for photo widgets, with Apple Watch faces: Photo Widget.
  • Best for one-tap matching themes: Themify.
  • Best for Liquid Glass and extra-large widgets: iScreen.
  • Best for a steady stream of designer theme sets: Colorful Widget.
  • Best for icon packs and an icon maker: WidgetClub.

What Color Widgets does well

By Digital Charms, Inc. Its App Store page invites you to "Browse thousands of predesigned widgets or, better yet, design your own", for the Home Screen and Lock Screen. Widgets cover photos, time and date, battery, countdowns, weather, music, calendar, quotes, activity, and sun and moon, and each can be customized "by adding photos, selecting styles, editing fonts, crafting a unique color scheme". It adds more than 15,000 themes and wallpapers and "AESTHETIC ICON PACKS".

  • Price: "Enjoy Color Widgets for free, with an optional upgrade". Pro starts with a free 3-day trial, and the listing describes it as billed yearly.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Vision Pro.
  • Recent changes: version 9.0 in September 2026 added the iOS 27 XL widget size, highlighted quote widgets, framed widgets and Lock Screen lunar widgets. Earlier in 2026 it added progress countdown widgets.
  • Ads and privacy: its age rating notes say it contains advertising, and its privacy label lists identifiers used to track you.

If you love its library, there's little reason to leave. The alternatives below differ on style, price model, ads and privacy.

1. Left: widgets about your time, without themes to manage

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone, not a theme app. If your Color Widgets setup was mostly countdowns, a clock, a calendar or a progress widget, Left does those with a calm, consistent look and keeps them up to date with your own dates.

  • Time Left widgets: Year, Month, Week, Day and Life show how much is left as a grid of symbols. On the Year widget you can pick from 14 symbols, such as dots, stars, hearts, flames or leaves, and a background that follows your device, or is light, dark, black or a color.
  • Countdowns: Ahead widgets with your own photo, a progress bar or circle, or a list of up to 7 dates.
  • Habits and streaks: an interactive Habit widget you tap to log today, and Streak widgets that show how long it's been.
  • Clocks and notes: Digital Clock, Analog Clock, Dynamic Time, Calendar and a Special note widget for up to 500 characters.
  • Lock Screen: most of these come as Lock Screen widgets too, and Left Wallpaper draws your year or life as a grid on a wallpaper that updates itself every day.

Best for: a clean, purposeful Home Screen: the days until something, how much of the year is left, today's habit.

Price: free to download, with no ads. The time, countdown, habit, streak, Aim and clock widgets and Left Wallpaper are free; the Fasting and Focus widgets are part of Left Premium. The free plan includes 2 each of countdowns, habits, streaks and Aims, counted over every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited items. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: dozens of free widgets with no ads; a privacy label with no data used to track you; widgets that update with your own dates and habits; a self-updating wallpaper.

Cons: no themes, icon packs or free-form widget editor; no weather, music or photo-frame widgets; only 2 free countdowns, habits, streaks and Aims; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

2. Widgetsmith

By Cross Forward Consulting, LLC. Its App Store page describes widgets for photos, clocks, activity, countdowns, solar and lunar trackers and weather, each with "various colors, fonts, borders, and themes", plus a wallpaper collection from artists to build matching widget themes and Lock Screen widgets. Recent versions add extra-large widgets, new fonts, accent colors and an Inspo tab.

Best for: polished widgets you style yourself, without a theme store.

Price: free, with "an optional premium subscription" monthly or annually. Weather and tide data need Premium.

Pros: 776,423 ratings, the most here; its privacy label lists no data used to track you.

Cons: icon packs aren't mentioned; its age rating notes say it contains advertising; weather needs Premium.

3. Photo Widget: Theme Wallpapers

By Photo Widget Inc. Its App Store page lets you apply complete themes (wallpaper, widgets and app icons) in one tap, with photo, D-Day countdown, quote, zodiac, battery, calendar, clock, memo and to-do widgets, fonts, borders, colors, an AI wallpaper maker and Apple Watch faces. Its 2026 updates removed the 21-photo limit for free users and made some fonts and clock designs free.

Best for: photo-led widgets and themes, with your Watch face to match.

Price: free with ads. PhotoWidget Premium, monthly or yearly, unlocks all premium content and removes ads; single themes and items can also be bought.

Pros: Apple Watch faces; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs; a free tier that has grown this year.

Cons: ads in the free version; its privacy label lists usage data used to track you.

4. Themify

By AIBY Incorporated. Its App Store page lists "2.5K themes, 4K+ widgets, 2K+ icon packs, 5.8K+ wallpapers, and 1K+ lock screen themes", "3-in-1 themes that apply matching icons, widgets, and wallpapers instantly", Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, and a Theme Maker.

Best for: applying a complete matching look in one go.

Price: free to download; "unlimited access to all Themify features with a weekly or annual subscription".

Pros: a close match to Color Widgets' mix of themes, icons and widgets; StandBy widgets; an iOS 27 collection.

Cons: ads; its privacy label lists several kinds of data used to track you, and health and fitness data linked to you.

5. iScreen - Widgets & Wallpaper

By Xiamen ShenZhuo Information Technology Co., Ltd. Its App Store page describes an "Upgraded 4x6 ExtraLarge Widget" for iOS 27, widgets that "support Liquid Glass dreamy style", themes, photo walls, weather, battery, countdown, calendar and clock widgets, Lock Screen styles, couple widgets, interactive widgets and DIY wallpapers.

Best for: large, glassy widgets and wallpapers in the latest iOS style.

Price: free to download; VIP plans run from weekly to yearly, plus a permanent option.

Pros: 4.7 from 153,086 ratings; frequent updates; iPhone and iPad.

Cons: icon packs aren't mentioned; many VIP tiers; ads; its privacy label lists identifiers used to track you.

6. Colorful Widget

By Guangzhou ZHIFENG Information Technology Co., Ltd. Its App Store page covers "widgets, themes, app icons, wallpapers, Lock Screen designs, and Dynamic Island experiences", with theme sets from "Over 300 professional designers", interactive to-do widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy, photo widgets and icons, and extra-large widgets for iOS 27. Its release notes add themes and live wallpapers in frequent updates.

Best for: a steady stream of new designer themes.

Price: free to download; memberships are listed weekly, monthly, yearly and permanent.

Pros: 4.7 stars; very frequent updates; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs.

Cons: release notes that repeat the same text; ads; its privacy label lists location and usage data used to track you.

7. WidgetClub

By Liume. Its App Store page offers "over 4,500 themes", "over 10,000 app icons" and an "Icon Maker to create your own icons", with clock, calendar, music, GIF, photo, countdown, anniversary, weather and battery widgets, a Lock Screen memo and more than 40,000 wallpapers.

Best for: people who care most about app icons.

Price: free to download; Premium is listed weekly, monthly and yearly, and theme packs can be bought separately.

Pros: a large icon library, plus an icon maker; anniversary and countdown widgets; updated in October 2026.

Cons: theme packs are sold separately from Premium; ads; its privacy label lists purchases, location, search and browsing history and more used to track you.

Questions

What is the best alternative to Color Widgets?

Themify is a close match for themes, icon packs and widgets together. Widgetsmith suits you if you'd rather style widgets yourself, and Left if your widgets were mainly countdowns, clocks or year progress.

Is Color Widgets free?

Yes, with an optional upgrade. Its App Store page says you can enjoy Color Widgets for free, and that Pro starts with a free 3-day trial. Its age rating notes say it contains advertising.

Which widget apps don't track you?

Of the apps we compared, Widgetsmith's and Left's privacy labels list no data used to track you. Color Widgets, Photo Widget, Themify, iScreen, Colorful Widget and WidgetClub list at least one kind of data used to track you.

Which Color Widgets alternatives have icon packs?

Themify, Colorful Widget, WidgetClub and Photo Widget list app icons or icon packs, and WidgetClub adds an icon maker. Widgetsmith, iScreen and Left don't list icon packs.

Can I get a countdown widget like Color Widgets' for free?

Color Widgets includes countdown widgets in its free app. If you want countdowns on the Lock Screen as well, Left's countdown widgets are free on the Home Screen, with your own photo, and on the Lock Screen, and its free plan includes 2 countdowns. See the countdown widget page for examples.
