7 Best Streaks App Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated October 26, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

Streaks by Crunchy Bagel is one of the best-known habit trackers on the iPhone: an Apple Design Award winner, with 4.8 stars from 27,353 ratings on the US App Store. It's a polished app that you pay for once. People look for an alternative when they want to try a habit tracker for free first, track more than 24 things, or have their habits sit next to other parts of their life. Here are six alternatives and when each one is the better choice.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best alternative if you want habits, streaks and countdowns in one app, with free widgets: Left.
  • Best for Apple Watch, Lock Screen widgets and Live Activities, free to start: Awesome Habits.
  • Best for a contribution-style grid per habit: HabitKit.
  • Best for friends, groups and the most ratings: Habit Tracker by Inner Grow.
  • Best for automatic logging from other apps: Habitify.
  • Best for habits plus numeric goals: Strides.
  • Best for readers of Atomic Habits: Atoms.

What Streaks does well

By Crunchy Bagel Pty Ltd. Its App Store page says you can "Track up to 24 tasks you want to complete each day". Tasks linked to the Health app can complete automatically, negative tasks help you break habits, and timed tasks, daily notes, automatic reminders, 78 color themes and over 600 icons are included. You can share tasks with other Streaks users, and iCloud syncs everything.

  • Price: $5.99 to download, with no in-app purchases listed and no subscription.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Vision Pro. The Apple Watch app includes Health integration, complications and rich notifications.
  • Widgets: Home Screen widgets, including one to view and complete tasks.

If you want a paid-once habit tracker with a highly rated Apple Watch app and 24 tasks is enough, Streaks is hard to beat. The alternatives below make sense when your needs are different.

1. Left: habits next to the rest of your time

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. Habits, streaks and Aims live in Since, next to countdowns in Ahead and year and life progress in Time Left, so your habits sit inside a view of how much of the week, month and year is left.

  • Habits: set a goal per day, week or month and how many times. Schedules include every day, weekdays, weekends, specific days or a number of days per week. Each habit can have up to 4 reminders, and Get a persistent reminder shows a Live Activity an hour before a time you choose if the habit still isn't logged.
  • Streaks: for quitting something or keeping a run going. A streak counts up automatically from Started on... until you restart it, and keeps your restarts, average streak and longest streak. It does the job of a negative task in Streaks.
  • Aims: count up or down toward a target, with a unit and an optional deadline.
  • Widgets: the Habit widget is interactive: tap today's symbol to log it from the Home Screen. It also works as a rectangular Lock Screen widget. The Habits list widget shows 4 or 8 habits, and Streak, Streaks list, Aim and Aims list widgets cover the rest. These widgets are free.
  • Left Premium extras: unlimited habits, streaks and Aims; Apple Health tracking, where a habit completes when you meet its goal; and friends, who can follow your habits and streaks for accountability.

Best for: people who want habits, quit streaks and goals alongside countdowns and a sense of time left, with widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Price: free to download. The free plan includes 2 habits, 2 streaks, 2 Aims and 2 countdowns, and the count includes every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium is a subscription or a lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: free to start; an interactive habit widget; Lock Screen widgets and a habit Live Activity; quit streaks with restart history; countdowns and time left views in the same app.

Cons: only 2 free habits and 2 free streaks, far fewer than Streaks' 24 tasks; no Apple Watch app, which Streaks has; Apple Health tracking needs Left Premium; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

2. Awesome Habits: Habit Tracker

By Michal Tuma. Its App Store page lists daily, weekly and monthly goals, timed goals, heatmaps and statistics, Health app integration, reminders and alarms, interactive widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy, interactive Live Activities and the Dynamic Island, Control Center widgets, Apple Watch complications and a Smart Stack widget, Siri Shortcuts, iCloud sync and CSV export.

Best for: people who want Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, Live Activities and Apple Watch complications in a tracker that's free to start.

Price: free to download, with Premium monthly, yearly or lifetime. The listing doesn't say what the free version includes.

Pros: Lock Screen, StandBy and Control Center widgets, Live Activities and Apple Watch complications; privacy label says Data Not Collected.

Cons: free limits aren't stated; fewer ratings (940) than the bigger names.

3. HabitKit

By Sebastian Roehl. Its App Store page describes a grid of tiles per habit, "like a contribution graph", for building or quitting habits, with yes/no, count or unit goals, flexible schedules, streak goals and notes on any day. It needs no account and works offline.

Best for: people who are motivated by watching a grid fill in.

Price: free for up to four habits, including streaks, statistics, notes and reminders. HabitKit Pro, monthly, yearly or lifetime, unlocks unlimited habits, Home Screen widgets, restoring archived habits and more reminders.

Pros: a clear free tier; no account, no ads; 4.8 from 2,492 ratings; daily iCloud backup.

Cons: Home Screen widgets are part of Pro; Apple Watch isn't in its compatibility list. Our HabitKit vs Left comparison goes deeper.

4. Habit Tracker by Inner Grow

By InnerGrow (Inner Grow Limited). Its App Store page lists Apple Health sync for water, steps, sleep, exercise and calories, build and quit habits, unit goals, group habits and friendly competition with friends, reports, mood tracking, a habit diary, a focus timer, time or location reminders, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, cloud sync and Face ID lock.

Best for: a free-to-start tracker with social features and the largest review base here.

Price: free to download, with monthly, yearly and lifetime options in its in-app purchases.

Pros: 4.8 from 148,034 ratings; Apple Watch; group habits with friends; location-based reminders.

Cons: the long feature list doesn't say which parts are free; it does a lot, which can feel busy next to Streaks' simplicity.

5. Habitify: Habit Tracker

By Unstatic Ltd Co. Its App Store page lists automatic logging from Apple Health, Google Health, Fitbit, Strava, Screen Time and RescueTime, an API, MCP and App Intents, an AI assistant, streaks and charts, smart reminders, notes, mood tracking, a timer and friendly challenges, synced across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Best for: people who want habits logged for them from the apps and devices they already use.

Price: free to download. Habitify Plus adds unlimited habits, smart reminders and custom areas; Habitify Pro, with a 7-day free trial, adds the automatic integrations and advanced timers.

Pros: the widest set of integrations; Apple Watch and Mac; first released in 2016 and still updated.

Cons: automatic logging needs Pro; the listing doesn't state how many habits are free, and mentions Home Screen widgets only in its release notes.

6. Strides: Habit Tracker + Goals

By Goals LLC. Its App Store page describes four tracker types (streak, milestone, average and target), 150+ templates, progress reports, charts, unlimited reminders, Apple Health integration and notes.

Best for: people who track numbers and deadlines as well as daily habits.

Price: free to download. Strides Plus, monthly, annual or lifetime, unlocks unlimited trackers, sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac, tags and filters, data export and more.

Pros: flexible tracker types for goals as well as habits; Apple Watch; 4.8 from 19,242 ratings; around since 2013.

Cons: sync between devices needs Plus; widgets appear only in a release note and aren't described.

7. Atoms - from Atomic Habits

By Atomic Development Inc. Its App Store page describes guided habit creation based on James Clear's book, bite-sized lessons, "Five Good Minutes", smart reminders, progress insights and an Apple Watch app.

Best for: people who want lessons and coaching from Atomic Habits alongside the tracking.

Price: free to download, with Atoms Pro monthly or annual.

Pros: 4.8 from 10,749 ratings; guided setup based on the book; Apple Watch.

Cons: last updated in February 2026; Apple Health isn't mentioned and widgets appear only in a release note; its privacy label lists contact info, identifiers, usage data and user content as linked to you.

Questions

Is the Streaks app free?

No. Streaks costs $5.99 to download on the US App Store (checked 20 October 2026), with no in-app purchases listed and no subscription.

How many habits can you track in Streaks?

Up to 24 tasks a day, according to its App Store page. If you need more, HabitKit Pro, Habitify Plus, Strides Plus and Left Premium list unlimited habits or trackers.

What is the best free alternative to Streaks?

HabitKit is free for up to four habits, and Left is free for 2 habits and 2 streaks with free Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Awesome Habits, Habit Tracker by Inner Grow, Habitify and Strides are also free to download with paid upgrades.

Which Streaks alternatives have an Apple Watch app?

Awesome Habits, Habit Tracker by Inner Grow, Habitify, Strides and Atoms all list Apple Watch on their App Store pages. Left doesn't have an Apple Watch app, and HabitKit doesn't list Apple Watch in its compatibility.

Can I quit a bad habit with these apps?

Yes. Streaks has negative tasks, HabitKit and Habit Tracker by Inner Grow have quit habits, and Left uses streaks, which count up from the day you started and keep your restarts, average streak and longest streak.
