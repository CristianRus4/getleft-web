Best Exam Countdown Apps for iPhone in 2026: 7 Compared for Students

Updated November 8, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

An exam countdown keeps the date in front of you: how many days until the first paper, which exam comes next, how much of the term is left. Some apps do only that; others are full student planners where exams sit next to classes, homework and grades. Here are seven worth comparing on iPhone, and what each one is best at.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best for your next exam on the Lock Screen, with free widgets and a Live Activity on the day: Left.
  • Best dedicated exam countdown: Exam Countdown - School & Uni.
  • Best general countdown app with Apple Watch: Countdown by Find Appiness.
  • Best for "how much of the term is left" widgets: Countdown - Pretty Progress.
  • Best all-in-one student planner: My Study Life.
  • Best free planner with no ads: School Assistant.
  • Best for exams next to grades and GPA: Power Planner.

1. Left: your next exam where you'll see it

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. Exams go in Ahead, its countdown list, next to habits in Since and the time left in the week, month and year in Time Left.

  • Add an exam: tap Add a date ahead, type the subject or import it with From calendar, and set Starts to the exam's date and time. For a whole exam season, set Until to the last paper and Left shows how much of it is done.
  • Count it your way: Show as... counts in hours, days, weeks, months or years; tap the preview card to add a symbol, a color and a photo.
  • Reminders: 1 month, 2 weeks, 1 week, 2 days or 1 day before, at a time you choose.
  • On exam day: Get ready for the start shows a live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island in the 4 hours before the exam starts, and Get the final countdown does the same before Until.
  • Widgets: the Ahead widget on the Home Screen and as a circular or rectangular Lock Screen widget, and the Time Ahead list for up to 3 exams (medium) or 7 (large). The Month and Week widgets show how much of the month or week is left.
  • Revision habits: a habit such as "Revise for 30 minutes" with an interactive widget you tap to log, and its completion grid.
  • Left Premium extras: unlimited countdowns and habits, and the Focus timer for study sessions with breaks.

Best for: a few big exams you want to see every time you pick up your phone, without ads.

Price: free to download, with no ads. The free plan includes 2 countdowns (plus 2 habits, 2 streaks and 2 Aims), counted over every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. A full exam timetable needs Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: free Lock Screen and Home Screen countdown widgets; exam-day Live Activities; exams, revision habits and a progress bar for the whole exam season in one app.

Cons: only 2 free countdowns, so a full timetable needs Premium; a general countdown app, not made only for exams; no class timetable, homework or grades; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

2. Exam Countdown - School & Uni

By ROOT38 LIMITED. Its App Store page offers "Unlimited exam countdowns, all in one place", counting in years down to seconds, color-coded exams, 400 icons, notes, notifications before every exam and sharing with friends and family. It lists Next Exam, Upcoming Exams and Lock Screen widgets. Premium adds "the full widget collection, including the second by second Live Countdown", Live Activities, a Calendar View, subject categories, exam results and sync; the listing doesn't say which widgets are free.

Best for: students who want an app built only for exams.

Price: free with ads. Premium is offered yearly, for 3 months or as a lifetime purchase, and is "Ad free".

Pros: made for exams, with unlimited countdowns free; Live Activities and a term calendar with Premium; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs; updated in September 2026.

Cons: the full widget collection, Live Activities and sync need Premium; ads in the free version; its privacy label lists identifiers and usage data used to track you.

3. Countdown by Find Appiness

By Find Appiness LLC. Its App Store page lets you "Create as many events as you'd like", with tags, notes, reminders 1 day or 1 week before, a working-days-only count, iCloud sync and an Apple Watch app. Lock Screen, StandBy and Home Screen widgets are each marked "Premium feature".

Best for: exams alongside every other countdown in your life, with your Watch.

Price: free with ads; widgets come with a Countdown Widget purchase.

Pros: 4.8 from 189,110 ratings; Apple Watch; iCloud sync; updated in October 2026.

Cons: not built for exams, so there are no subjects or exam fields in the listing; widgets need the paid unlock; its privacy label lists identifiers used to track you.

4. Countdown - Pretty Progress

By keinois OU. Its App Store page suggests uses such as "Countdown to that important exam" and "Percentage of the academic year left", with six templates for the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy and Apple Watch complications, Calendar and Reminders import, and iCloud sync.

Best for: seeing the term or the run-up to an exam as a progress bar or percentage.

Price: "The free version of Pretty Progress includes four custom countdown widgets with two designs". Pro is offered weekly, monthly, yearly or as a lifetime purchase.

Pros: widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy and Apple Watch; Live Activities and Dynamic Island support; progress-style designs; its privacy label lists no data linked to you.

Cons: a widget app rather than an exam planner, with no subjects or exam fields listed; Pro is needed for more than four custom widgets.

5. My Study Life

By My Study Life Ltd. Its App Store page describes a school planner with an "EXAM TRACKER & STUDY PLANNER" to "Plan ahead for exams with study schedules and reminders", plus class timetables, including rotating weeks, homework, grades and GPA, a focus timer, an AI study coach and Home Screen widgets for upcoming classes and deadlines.

Best for: students who want exams, classes and homework in one planner.

Price: "Free to download. Optional auto-renewing subscriptions unlock premium features", including family plans for parents. The listing doesn't say which features are paid.

Pros: classes, homework, grades and exams in one app; sync across devices; scan your timetable from a photo.

Cons: no countdown view or Lock Screen widget is described; ads in the free version; its privacy label lists location, user content and identifiers used to track you.

6. School Assistant

By Sun Apps, LLC. Its App Store page covers assignments and tests with due dates, reminders and subtasks, a class schedule with A/B weeks, Home Screen widgets showing how many assignments and tests are coming up, an Apple Watch app and iCloud sync. Its latest version can show due dates as a countdown. It says: "School Assistant is 100% free: no subscriptions, no paywalls, no premium features, and no ads!"

Best for: a free, private planner where tests are part of your schoolwork list.

Price: free, with an optional tip jar.

Pros: everything free with no ads; privacy label says Data Not Collected; Apple Watch, iPad and Mac.

Cons: tests are one kind of schoolwork rather than a dedicated exam list; due dates can show as a countdown in the app, but no countdown widget is described; Lock Screen widgets are mentioned only for iPad.

7. Power Planner

By BareBones Dev, LLC. Its App Store page lists "Assignments & Exams" with automatic reminders, class schedules, a grade and GPA calculator, optional cross-platform sync and widgets for your "home or lock screen". It says it is "fully featured, ad-free, and 100% functional for free".

Best for: students who want exams next to their grades and GPA.

Price: free; a single Premium (Lifetime) purchase adds more than 5 grades per class and unlimited semesters.

Pros: ad-free; Lock Screen widgets; one lifetime upgrade instead of a subscription; updated in October 2026.

Cons: no exam countdown is described; the free version limits grades per class and semesters; its privacy label lists identifiers, usage data and diagnostics linked to you.

How we chose

  • Search first: we searched the US App Store for exam countdown, study planner and school planner apps on 20 October 2026 and read what ranks for "exam countdown app".
  • Kept: apps updated in the last 12 months with at least a thousand ratings that either count down to exams or hold exam dates in a student planner. Left is included because we make it.
  • Also considered: ROOT38's general Countdown: Event Countdown (the same developer as Exam Countdown, with Lock Screen and StandBy widgets in Premium), Smart Timetable (a class timetable; exam dates aren't mentioned), and two new exam apps, Exam Countdown & Study Planner and examday, which had one rating each. myHomework, Egenda, iStudiez and Sigma Planner were left out because they haven't been updated in over a year.
  • Sources: each app's own App Store page, including release notes, privacy labels and age-rating notes, and Apple's lookup data. No paid placements, and no developer reviewed this page.

Questions

What is the best exam countdown app?

Exam Countdown - School & Uni is the most-rated app built only for exams that we found, with unlimited free countdowns. Left suits you if you want your next exam on the Lock Screen with free widgets and a Live Activity on exam day. If you also need classes, homework and grades, a planner such as My Study Life or Power Planner fits better.

How do I put an exam countdown on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Use an app with Lock Screen widgets. In Left, add the exam in Ahead, then touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, tap Add Widgets and choose Left's Ahead widget. Countdown by Find Appiness offers Lock Screen widgets with its paid upgrade; Exam Countdown lists Lock Screen widgets, with the full widget collection in Premium; Pretty Progress and Power Planner list them too.

Are there free exam countdown apps?

Yes. Exam Countdown and Countdown by Find Appiness let you add unlimited countdowns for free, with ads. Left's widgets are free with no ads, but the free plan includes 2 countdowns. School Assistant and Power Planner are free, ad-free planners that hold exam dates.

Can I see a countdown to my exam in the Dynamic Island?

Yes. Left's Get ready for the start and Get the final countdown show a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island in the hours before an exam. Exam Countdown offers Live Activities with Premium, and Pretty Progress's App Store page lists Live Activities and Dynamic Island support.
