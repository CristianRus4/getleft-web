Date Calculator on iPhone: How to Count Days Between Dates, Add Days and Track Dates

Updated November 9, 2026 · 5 min read

iPhone has a calculator, a calendar and a clock, but no built-in date calculator. So the everyday questions, like "how many days until March 3?", "what date is 90 days from today?" or "how many working days are left this month?", end up as finger-counting on a calendar. There are better ways, and which one fits depends on whether you need an answer once or a number you'll check every day.

This guide covers the four date calculations people need most, how to do each on iPhone (with free tools that work in Safari, Siri and Spotlight, and apps), and when it's worth keeping a date counting itself in a widget instead.

Quick answers with Siri and Spotlight

For simple questions, you may not need a tool at all. Ask Siri "How many days until Christmas?" or "What's the date 30 days from today?", or type a similar question into Spotlight search. These work well for common questions. For anything with conditions, such as skipping weekends or counting both the start and end dates, a dedicated calculator is more reliable, because it shows you exactly what it counted.

Watch out for the off-by-one problem

The most common date calculation mistake is counting one day too many or too few. "From Monday to Friday" is 4 days if you count the gaps, and 5 if you count both Monday and Friday. Neither is wrong, but you need to know which one you want:

  • Days until something: usually don't count today. Friday is 4 days from Monday.
  • Length of a stay, course or project: often count both ends. A Monday-to-Friday course is 5 days.
  • Hotel nights: count nights, not days. Monday to Friday is 4 nights.
  • Deadlines in business days: start counting the next business day.

Good date calculators let you choose whether to include the end date. When it matters (a contract, a visa, a medication schedule), check which convention applies.

Questions

Does iPhone have a date calculator?

Not a dedicated one. The Calculator app doesn't do dates, but Siri and Spotlight answer simple questions like "how many days until Christmas", and free web tools such as our days between two dates calculator work in Safari.

How do I calculate the days between two dates on iPhone?

Open a days-between-dates calculator in Safari, enter both dates and choose whether to include the end date. For a quick estimate, you can also ask Siri.

How do I find the date 90 days from today?

Use an add or subtract date calculator: enter today's date and add 90 days. Our add or subtract date calculator also handles weeks, months and years.

Should I include the end date when counting days?

It depends on the question. For "days until" a date, usually not. For the length of a stay, course or project, people often count both the first and last day. Check which convention applies when it matters.

Can I keep a date counting on my iPhone Home Screen?

Yes. A countdown app with widgets updates the number every day without you re-entering anything. In Left, add the date in Ahead and place the Ahead widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
