How Many Weeks Are in an Academic Year? School, College and University

Updated December 29, 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: a US school year is about 36 weeks of classes (around 180 school days), a US college year is about 30 to 32 weeks of teaching across two semesters, and a school year in England is 39 weeks (190 days). The calendar span from the first day to the last is longer, usually 40 weeks or more, because breaks sit in between.

The exact number depends on where you study and what kind of calendar your school uses. Below: typical numbers by system, how to count the weeks in your own academic year, and how to keep the weeks left visible so the year doesn't run away from you.

Weeks in an academic year, by system

These are typical figures. Your school's published calendar is the only exact source.

One more definition you may run into: for US federal student aid, an academic year must include at least 30 weeks of instructional time for most programs. That's a minimum for aid purposes, not how long your school's year actually runs.

Teaching weeks vs calendar weeks

Two numbers get mixed up all the time:

  • Teaching weeks are the weeks with classes. This is the number in the table above.
  • Calendar weeks are the weeks from the first day to the last, breaks included. A US school year that runs from late August to early June spans about 41 weeks, even though only about 36 of them have classes.

For planning, teaching weeks are what matter. A paper due "in 8 weeks" over a two-week break is really a paper due in 6 working weeks.

How to count the weeks in your own academic year

  1. Find your school's academic calendar (usually on its website, often as a PDF).
  2. Note the first day of classes and the last day of exams.
  3. Count the calendar weeks between them. Our weeks until date calculator or days between two dates tool does it in a second.
  4. Subtract each break, rounded to whole weeks: fall break, winter break, spring break, reading weeks.

Worked example: classes start on August 25 and exams end on May 14. That's about 37.5 calendar weeks. Take off a one-week Thanksgiving break, a four-week winter break and a one-week spring break, and you're left with about 31.5 teaching weeks, split into two semesters of roughly 15 to 16 weeks.

For school (K-12) years, our school days calculator counts weekdays between two dates; divide by five for school weeks.

What the number is good for

Knowing you have, say, 15 teaching weeks in a semester changes how you plan:

  • Pacing a big project. A term paper due in week 12 gives you 11 weeks. Split it into research, outline, draft and edit, roughly three weeks each.
  • Seeing the midpoint. Week 8 of 15 is past halfway. If your grades need to turn around, that's the moment to act, not week 14.
  • Planning study blocks. Spaced review across weeks beats cramming in the last one.
  • Budgeting. A meal plan or allowance stretches further when you know exactly how many weeks it has to cover.

Questions

How many weeks are in a school year?

In the US, about 36 weeks of classes, from roughly 180 school days. In England and Wales it's 39 weeks (190 days), and in Australia about 40 weeks over four terms. Check your school's calendar for the exact number.

How many weeks are in a college academic year?

A US college on semesters usually has about 30 to 32 teaching weeks: two semesters of 15 to 16 weeks each. Quarter-system colleges have three 10-week terms, about 30 weeks in total.

How many weeks is a semester?

Most US semesters are 15 to 16 weeks including finals week. Summer sessions are much shorter, often 5 to 8 weeks.

How many weeks are in an academic year for financial aid?

For most US federal student aid programs, an academic year must include at least 30 weeks of instructional time. Your school's own calendar may be longer.

How do I count the weeks left in my semester?

Count the calendar weeks from today to your last exam, then subtract any breaks. Our weeks until date calculator does the first part, or keep a weeks-left countdown on your phone with an app like Left.
