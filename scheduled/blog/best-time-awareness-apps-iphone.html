Best Time Awareness Apps for iPhone in 2026: 7 Compared

Updated November 28, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 21 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

A time awareness app makes the passing of time visible. Instead of a clock or a calendar, you get a grid of dots for the year, a percentage for the month, or a picture of your whole life in weeks, on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. It's a different job from a timer, which counts down one session, and from a habit tracker, which logs what you did. Here are seven apps for the job on iPhone.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

  • Best all-round time awareness app, with free widgets: Left.
  • Best for year progress plus countdowns, with the most ratings: Countdown - Pretty Progress.
  • Best for a calm, private life-in-weeks calendar: Time Left.
  • Best for days lived and quarters, with a one-time Pro purchase: Awareness - Time Visualization.
  • Best free dots grid: Life Dots.
  • Best for year progress in many styles, paid once: Orbit.
  • Best for life-age widgets across iPhone, Watch and Mac: Life Widget.

What counts as a time awareness app

  • It shows time as a shape. Dots, bars, rings or blocks that fill or fade as time passes, so you see how much is left without doing the sums.
  • It works at several scales. The day, the week, the month, the year, and in some apps your whole life.
  • It lives where you look. On the Home Screen, the Lock Screen or the wallpaper, not only inside an app you have to open.
  • It isn't a timer or a tracker. A visual timer such as Time Timer counts one session, and a habit app logs what you did. Some apps below add habits or countdowns as extras.

If you only want one scale, we have deeper lists for year progress apps, life calendar apps and Lock Screen widget apps.

1. Left: every scale of time, on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. Its Time Left section shows your life, the year, the month, the week, today and the current hour as grids of symbols, where the past dims and what remains stays solid. Countdowns live in Ahead, and habits, streaks and Aims in Since.

  • Widgets: the Year widget comes in small, medium and large and as circular, rectangular and inline Lock Screen widgets. It can show time as days, weeks, months, hours or minutes, with 14 symbols to choose from. Month, Week, Day, Life and You widgets follow, and an Everything widget shows the day, week, month and year together. These widgets are free.
  • Life: enter your birthday to see your life in Years, Months or Weeks. The default lifespan is 80 years; a custom lifespan is part of Left Premium, and it's an estimate for reflection, not medical advice.
  • Wallpaper: Left Wallpaper puts your life, year or month on the Lock Screen, and it's free. Updating it every day takes a short Shortcuts automation that the app walks you through.
  • Countdowns and habits: Ahead dates show progress to a date with a photo and reminders, and the final countdown can run as a Live Activity.

Best for: people who want the whole picture, from the hour to the life, in one app, with widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Price: free to download. The free plan includes the Time Left views, the widgets, the wallpaper and 2 countdowns, 2 habits, 2 streaks and 2 Aims, and the count includes every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium is a subscription or a lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: every scale of time in one app; free widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen; a free wallpaper; countdowns, habits and goals alongside.

Cons: a custom lifespan, unlimited items and Apple Health tracking need Left Premium; iPhone only, and it needs iOS 26 or later; no Apple Watch app.

2. Countdown - Pretty Progress

By keinois OU. Its App Store page describes countdown widgets that count up or down, including the default daily, weekly, monthly and yearly progress, and the percentage complete, time left or time since for any date. It lists six widget templates for the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy and Apple Watch complications, recurring events, Apple Calendar and Reminders import, and iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac.

Best for: people who want year progress and countdowns with a big review base and an Apple Watch app.

Price: free to download. The listing says the free version includes four custom countdown widgets with two designs, the default progress widgets and some customization. PRO is sold weekly, monthly, annually and as a lifetime purchase.

Pros: 4.7 from 3,112 ratings; updated in October 2026; Lock Screen, StandBy and Watch support.

Cons: it's a countdown app first, so year progress is one feature among many; it needs iOS 18; the listing doesn't describe a life view.

3. Time Left • Be Present

By Julien Lacroix. Its App Store page says Time Left "shows you, the days, months, and years you have left", with a Life in Weeks calendar and moments you add yourself. It says there are no notifications, ads or sign-up, and that your birthdate stays private.

Best for: people who want a quiet, private weeks calendar and nothing else.

Price: free to download. Time Left Pro unlocks widgets and skins, unlimited moments and Live Photos in the Life Calendar.

Pros: no ads, notifications or sign-up; its privacy label lists only analytics and purchase data, not linked to you; on the App Store since 2020; updated in October 2026; runs on iPhone and Apple Vision.

Cons: widgets are part of Pro; month and week views aren't described; fewer extras than an all-in-one app.

4. Awareness - Time Visualization

The store page also lists Apple Watch, and its release notes mention Lock Screen and Watch widgets.

Best for: people who like the idea of one calm app for birthdays, milestones and the shape of the year, and want a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, as the listing describes.

Price: free to download, with Pro sold as an in-app purchase. The listing describes a one-time purchase with lifetime access and no subscription, but doesn't say what the free version includes.

Pros: a one-time Pro purchase; widgets on iPhone, Mac and Apple Watch; 4.7 from 26 ratings; updated in October 2026.

Cons: few ratings; the listing doesn't say what the free version includes; its in-app purchase list has three Pro options.

5. Life Dots: Visualize Time

6. Orbit: Year Progress & Life

By Rahul Singh. Pros: a one-time purchase; 10 year styles; Data Not Collected on its privacy label.

Cons: only 11 ratings; despite "Life" in its name, the listing describes no life view.

Price: free to download, with in-app purchases that include a monthly option and lifetime options. The listing doesn't say what the free version includes.

Questions

What is a time awareness app?

A time awareness app shows how much time has passed and how much is left, as a grid, a bar or a percentage, so you can see it at a glance. Examples include year progress widgets, life-in-weeks calendars and countdowns. It is different from a timer, which counts a single session, and from a habit tracker, which logs what you did.

What is the best free time awareness app for iPhone?

Left, Life Dots and Orbit are free to download. Left's Time Left views, widgets and wallpaper are free, with 2 countdowns, 2 habits, 2 streaks and 2 Aims. Life Dots lists no in-app purchases. Orbit's free version includes year progress in the Ring style and basic widgets.

Which apps put time progress on the Lock Screen?

Left, Countdown - Pretty Progress, Life Dots, Orbit and Life Widget list Lock Screen widgets, and the release notes for Time Left and Awareness mention them too. In Left, the Year, Month, Week, Day, Life and You widgets all come in Lock Screen sizes.

Is a time awareness app the same as a year progress widget?

A year progress widget is one kind of time awareness app. Others show your whole life in weeks or years, count down to dates, or show the day, week and month together. Several apps here do more than one of these.

Can a time awareness app help with time blindness?

These apps make the passing of days, months and years visible, which some people find helpful. They are not a treatment for ADHD or any other condition. For tips on seeing time with an ADHD brain, see our guide to time blindness on iPhone.
