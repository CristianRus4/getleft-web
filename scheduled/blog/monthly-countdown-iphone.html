Monthly Countdown Timer on iPhone: Days Left and Month Progress

Updated November 18, 2026 · 7 min read

A monthly countdown timer can mean two different things. Some people want to see how much of this month is left: the days left in the month, or month progress as a percentage. Others want a countdown that starts over every month, for rent, payday or a bill. This guide covers both on iPhone, with the math, a worked example and setups you can copy.

The math needs only a pencil. Where an app helps, we show it in Left, the time awareness app we make.

How to work out the days left in the month

Start with the month's length:

  • 31 days: January, March, May, July, August, October, December.
  • 30 days: April, June, September, November.
  • 28 days: February, or 29 in a leap year (the next one is 2028).

Then pick how you count, because "days left" has two honest answers:

  • Counting today: days in the month − today's date + 1. Use this when today still counts, as with a daily budget or a daily habit.
  • Not counting today: days in the month − today's date. Use this in the evening, when today is mostly spent.

For month progress, take the days that have fully passed, add the part of today that's gone, and divide by the month's length:

Percent passed = (today's date − 1 + hours so far today ÷ 24) ÷ days in the month × 100. Percent left is 100 minus that.

Worked example: November 9, 2026, at 6 pm

  • November has 30 days.
  • Days left, counting today: 30 − 9 + 1 = 22. Not counting today: 21.
  • Time passed: 8 full days plus 18 hours, or 8.75 days.
  • Month progress: 8.75 ÷ 30 = 29.2% passed, so 70.8% left.

Why "percent of the month" is more honest than "day 15"

Months run from 28 to 31 days, so the same date sits at a different point in each one. Here is how far through the month you are on the morning of the 15th:

Month lengthExamplePassed on the morning of the 15thDays left, counting the 15th
28 daysFebruary 202650.0%14
29 daysFebruary 202848.3%15
30 daysNovember46.7%16
31 daysDecember45.2%17

That's a three-day spread for the "same" date. The 28th ends February but leaves four days in a 31-day month. If you pace anything against the calendar, such as a spending limit, a sales target or a reading goal, a percentage keeps you honest where a date can mislead.

It's the same idea as year progress at a smaller scale. What year progress is and how it's calculated goes deeper, and the time left in the year calculator shows the bigger picture.

How to make a countdown that resets every month

For rent, payday or a bill, you want a date that counts down, reaches zero and starts again. Apple's Reminders and Calendar can repeat an item monthly, but they show a due date, not a count of days.

In Left, this is an Ahead date with a monthly repeat:

  1. Open Ahead and tap Add a date ahead (or +).
  2. Type a name, such as "Rent due", or tap New date. If the date is already in your calendar, From calendar imports it.
  3. Set Starts to the next time it's due, for example December 1.
  4. Set Repeat to Monthly.
  5. Under More options, set Show as… to days.
  6. Add a reminder, anywhere from a month to a day before.
  7. Add an Ahead widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen and pick the date.

If the date falls on the 29th, 30th or 31st, check the next few months after you set it up: apps and calendars handle short months differently, so a date on the 31st may move or skip a month.

Four ways to use a monthly countdown

Budget to payday

Divide what's left by the days left. Say it's November 9, payday is the 25th, and you have $480 for groceries and everything else. Counting today but not payday, that's 16 days, or $30 a day. A week later you have $300 left with 9 days to go: $33 a day, so you're slightly ahead.

A repeating payday countdown keeps that second number in view. If your pay date moves around weekends and holidays, check it each month. This is arithmetic, not financial advice; for a real budget plan, talk to a financial professional.

A monthly goal with an Aim

Turn the goal into a number and compare two percentages. Say you want to run 100 km in November. That's about 3.3 km a day. At 6 pm on November 9 the month is 29% gone, so you're on pace if you've logged about 29 km.

In Left, set it up as an Aim that counts up to 100 km, and set the Month widget to percentage. If the Aim shows 22% done while the month shows 71% left, you're about 7 km behind, with three weeks to catch up.

A month-end work deadline

Count workdays, not days. On Monday, November 9, there are 22 calendar days left but only 16 weekdays, or 15 if your workplace closes for Thanksgiving on the 26th. Planning against 22 is how month-end crunches happen.

Add an Ahead date for the deadline and turn on Get ready for the start: in the hours before it, a Live Activity shows the countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. On the last day, the workday countdown shows the hours and minutes until you stop work.

A monthly review

Use the 1st of each month rather than the last day, so the date never lands on a missing 31st. A repeating Ahead date with a reminder a day before is enough. Ten minutes covers it: what moved, what didn't, and the one number to watch next month.

Questions

How many days are left in this month?

Subtract today's date from the number of days in the month, and add 1 if you count today. On November 9, 2026, that's 22 days counting today, or 21 without it.

How do I calculate what percentage of the month has passed?

Add the full days that have passed to the part of today that's gone, divide by the days in the month and multiply by 100. At 6 pm on November 9, 2026: (8 + 0.75) ÷ 30 = 29.2% passed, so 70.8% is left.

Is there a month progress widget for iPhone?

Yes. Left's Month widget shows the month as a grid with the days or percentage left, on the Home Screen or the Lock Screen, and it's free. Apple's Calendar widget shows the month but doesn't count what's left.

How do I set up a countdown that repeats every month?

Use a countdown app with a monthly repeat. In Left, add a date in Ahead, set Starts to the next due date and set Repeat to Monthly. Reminders and Calendar can repeat monthly too, but show a due date, not a count.

How many workdays are left in the month?

Count the weekdays from today to the end of the month, then subtract the holidays your workplace observes. From Monday, November 9, 2026, that's 16 counting today, or 15 if you're off for Thanksgiving. The business days calculator counts them between any two dates.
