Daily Habit Tracker for iPhone: How to Track Habits Every Day Without It Becoming a Chore

Updated November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

A daily habit tracker asks one question, every day: did you do it? The question is easy. Answering it every day for months is the hard part. Most people don't quit their habits first; they quit tracking them, and the habit slips quietly a few weeks later.

So the real job of a daily habit tracker is to make the daily check-in so light that you keep doing it on a bad day. This guide covers a two-minute daily routine, how to schedule habits that don't happen every day, and how to set up a daily habit tracker on iPhone so logging happens where you already are.

The two-minute daily routine

Tracking works best as three tiny moments spread through the day, not one session where you try to remember everything:

  1. Morning glance (10 seconds). Look at today's habits on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You're not doing anything yet, just seeing the list.
  2. Log in the moment (one tap each). Do the habit, log it right then. Finished your walk? Tap. Took your vitamins? Tap. The habit and the log should be seconds apart.
  3. Evening sweep (one minute). Before bed, check what's left. Do the small ones if you can, log honestly if you can't, and stop there.

Logging "later" is where most data goes missing. If you remember at 11 pm that you did read at lunch, log it, but the aim is to make "later" rare.

Daily doesn't have to mean every day

A habit tracker that only understands "every day" turns your rest days into failures. Most good habits run on a schedule:

HabitHonest schedule
Make the bedEvery day
Plan tomorrowWeekdays
Long walkWeekends
Strength trainingMonday, Wednesday, Friday
RunAny 3 days a week
Drink water6 times a day

Pick the schedule you'll really keep, not the one you wish you'd keep. A habit done four days a week for a year beats one attempted seven days a week for a month.

What a good daily habit tracker does

  • Shows today first. Today's list, with what's done and what isn't, should be the first thing you see.
  • Logs in one tap, outside the app. From a Home Screen widget, the Lock Screen or a watch. Opening an app to log is a step too many on busy days.
  • Handles real schedules. Weekdays, specific days, a number of days per week, several times a day.
  • Reminds you only when it matters. A nudge at the right time, and a backup only if the habit isn't done yet.
  • Forgives a missed day. Shows a consistency score or a grid of the last weeks, not just a streak that drops to zero.

Setting up a daily habit tracker on iPhone with Left

Left is an iPhone app we make for habits, streaks, countdowns and seeing how much time is left. Here's how to set it up for the routine above.

1. Add your daily habits

  1. Open Left, go to Since and tap +.
  2. Type what you want to do ("Make the bed", "Read") to pick a ready-made template, or choose Build from scratch.
  3. Choose the schedule: every day, weekdays, weekends, specific days of the week, or a number of days per week. For habits done several times a day, set how many times.
  4. Save. Start with two or three habits; add more once these run without effort.

2. Put today on your Home Screen

Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Left.

  • Habits list shows several habits and their progress: up to 4 in the small and medium sizes, 8 in the large. This is your morning glance.
  • Habit shows one habit. Set its layout to Text (touch and hold it, tap Edit Widget) and you can tap today's circle to log without opening the app. Habits done several times a day fill up with each tap.

Add a Habits list widget to the Lock Screen too, so today's progress shows before you unlock. The habit tracker page shows each size.

3. Set reminders for the moments, and one backup

Turn on Get reminders and add up to 4 reminders per habit, each with its own days and time. Then turn on Get a persistent reminder and set Before what time? to your evening sweep time: if the habit still isn't logged, a Live Activity appears an hour before. That's the evening sweep, done for you.

4. Log from your wrist

With Left's Apple Watch app, the Habits screen shows what's due today and logs a habit with one tap. Add the Habits Today complication to your watch face and the list is always a glance away.

Reading your progress

Each habit in Left shows the current streak, days completed and missed, your longest streak, a completion grid and habit strength. Use them differently:

  • Daily: only today matters. Done or not done.
  • Weekly: glance at the grid. Mostly filled is good. A whole empty week means the habit, the schedule or the reminder needs changing.
  • Monthly: look at habit strength. It reflects how regular you've been lately, so it's a fairer score than the streak if you sometimes miss a day.

Going on holiday? Archive a habit to pause it: it disappears from Since and widgets, and the archived days count as skipped instead of missed.

Habits that track themselves

Some daily habits don't need a tap at all. With Left Premium, Apple Health automatic tracking completes habits like "8,000 steps" or "30 minutes of exercise" when your iPhone or Apple Watch records them. See Apple Health tracking. And habits you're quitting ("no soda") work better as streaks, which count up on their own until you reset them; streaks vs habits explains when to use which.

When daily tracking starts to feel like a chore

  • Cut the list. Drop the habit you'd be least sad to lose. Fewer habits, more check-ins.
  • Shrink the minimum. "Read 10 pages" becomes "read 1 page". The habit survives the week; the pages come back later.
  • Turn off reminders for automatic habits. If you always brush your teeth, you don't need a notification for it.
  • Don't backfill a week of guesses. Start fresh today instead. A grid with a gap is more useful than a grid of maybes.

Questions

What is a daily habit tracker?

An app or chart where you mark each day whether you did a habit. Over time it shows streaks, gaps and how consistent you've been, which helps you notice when a habit is slipping.

Can a daily habit tracker handle habits I don't do every day?

A good one can. In Left, daily habits can run every day, on weekdays, on weekends, on specific days or on a number of days per week, so rest days don't count as misses.

Can I log habits from my iPhone Home Screen?

Yes. Left's Habit widget, in its Text layout, lets you tap today's circle to log a habit without opening the app. The Habits list widget shows several habits at once on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

How many daily habits should I track?

Start with two or three. Add another only when those run without much effort. A short list keeps the daily check-in quick, which is what keeps you tracking.

What if I miss a day?

Log it honestly and do the habit today. Look at a measure like habit strength or the completion grid rather than the streak alone; one missed day barely moves them.
