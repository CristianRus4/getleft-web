6 Best Days Since App Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated November 27, 2026 · 8 min read

Checked 21 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

Days Since: Quit Habit Tracker is a well-rated quit counter on the US App Store, with 4.8 stars from 19,511 ratings. Its core is simple: you create a counter for a habit you're trying to quit, set the last time you did it, and the app shows how long it has been. People look for an alternative when they want to count more than quit habits, want widgets their listing describes, keep a history of every time, or pay differently. Here are six alternatives and when each one is the better choice.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best alternative if you want days since on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, next to countdowns and habits: Left.
  • Best for countdowns and days since in one list: DayCount.
  • Best "when did I last do this?" log for recurring chores, paid once: Last Time Tracker.
  • Best for history, stats and a full Apple Watch app: Hindsight.
  • Best with one-time unlocks instead of a subscription: Countdown Days Since & Until.
  • Best if you mostly count down and only sometimes count up: Countdown by Find Appiness.

What Days Since does well

By A Couple of Friends OOD. Its App Store page says "Create a counter for each habit that you are trying to get rid of", and gives the example of setting the last time you had a cigarette so the app shows how long it has been. If you want a simple counter for something you're quitting, and its rating and years of updates matter to you, Days Since is a good choice. The alternatives below make sense when your needs are different.

1. Left: days since as streaks, on the Home Screen and Lock Screen

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. A days since counter in Left is a streak, which lives in Since next to habits and Aims. Countdowns live in Ahead, and year and life progress in Time Left.

  • How it works: create a streak and set Started on... to any past date. It counts up automatically. When you slip, or when you do the thing again, tap Restart and choose when it happened, anywhere between the start and now.
  • History: each streak keeps its restarts, average streak, longest streak and reset history, so you can see whether the gaps are getting longer.
  • Units: the Streak widget can show a streak in hours, days, weeks, months or years.
  • Widgets: the Streak widget comes in small, medium and large, with an optional photo and the start date, and as a rectangular Lock Screen widget. The Streaks list widget shows 3 (medium) or 7 (large) on the Home Screen. The Streak and Streaks list widgets are free.
  • Reminders: Get reminders and Get elapsed-time reminders on each streak.
  • Left Premium extras: unlimited streaks, friends who can follow your streak for accountability, and Apple Health tracking that resets a streak when a period ends without meeting its goal.

Best for: people who want "days since" visible on the Home Screen or Lock Screen, and want it next to countdowns, habits and a sense of how much of the year is left.

Price: free to download. The free plan includes 2 streaks (plus 2 habits, 2 Aims and 2 countdowns), and the count includes every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium is a subscription or a lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: the Streak widget is free on the Home Screen and Lock Screen; restart history with average and longest streak; units from hours to years; countdowns, habits and goals in the same app.

Cons: only 2 free streaks, which is tight if you track many things; iPhone only, and it needs iOS 26 or later; no Apple Watch app; a streak is one running count, so it doesn't group chores by how overdue they are the way Last Time Tracker does.

2. DayCount

By Zaminiti Pty Ltd. Its App Store page describes event counters that track time before and after a date, so one list holds countdowns and days since. It also lists custom categories, images from your device or Unsplash, repeating events, notes, daily streaks, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with different filters, fonts, textures and borders, reminders before or after an event, a daily summary notification, calendar import, Face ID or Touch ID lock, and sync across iPhone, iPad, iPod touch and Mac. The latest update added Siri and Shortcuts support.

Best for: people who want countdowns and days since together, with widgets and a few streaks.

Price: free to download. The listing says DayCount offers an auto-renewing yearly subscription with a free 7-day trial, and that DayCount Premium is available as an annual subscription or a lifetime purchase. It doesn't say what the free version includes.

Pros: 4.7 from 26,261 ratings; widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen; sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac; first released in 2016 and updated in October 2026.

Cons: Apple Watch isn't in its compatibility list; the free limits aren't stated; its privacy label includes Contact Info and User Content as linked to you.

3. Last Time Tracker

By Sarun Wongpatcharapakorn. Its App Store page describes tracking how long it has been since you last did something. You log it with one tap, with a note or a chosen date. Events are grouped as Overdue, This Week or Later based on how often you actually do each thing, and each event keeps its history, your average gap and when the next one is due. It also lists a Morning Digest notification, an app lock with Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode, iCloud sync, Siri and Shortcuts, and CSV export.

Best for: recurring things you want to do every so often, such as the dentist, a call to your parents or changing a filter.

Price: $5.99 to download. Its in-app purchases are three tips named Big Fan, Coffee and Beer, which its update notes describe as a way to support the developer.

Pros: privacy label says Data Not Collected; history, averages and "next due" estimates; 4.6 from 636 ratings; updated in October 2026.

Cons: there's no free version; widgets and Apple Watch aren't mentioned in the listing.

4. Hindsight

By Rogue Signal Inc. (Apps). Its App Store page describes swiping to track an event, a detailed timeline of every occurrence with notes, alerts based on time intervals, and stats with a histogram of how often you do something. It lists iCloud sync with no account, CSV export and import, a full-featured Apple Watch app, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and Siri and Shortcuts.

Best for: people who want real history and stats about how often they do something, with an Apple Watch app.

Price: free to download. Its in-app purchases are optional support payments (a monthly and a yearly one, plus tips), and the listing doesn't describe locked features.

Pros: history, stats and a full Apple Watch app; no account; 4.6 from 396 ratings.

Cons: it needs iOS 26 or later, so older iPhones are out; fewer ratings than the big names; it's built for logging events rather than a quit streak.

5. Countdown Days Since & Until

By Joseph Sferrazza. Its App Store page says you can count down to or up from a date in seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years or sleeps, with unlimited events, custom photos and icons, calendar import, custom alerts, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and sync across devices and Apple Watch. The latest update added more Siri actions on iOS 27 and Spotlight search.

Best for: people who want a mature, free app that counts both ways, and prefer one-time purchases to a subscription.

Price: free to download. Its listing says the widgets are "ad-free with in-app purchase". The in-app purchases are one-time unlocks such as Unlock All, Remove Ads and effect packs.

Pros: 4.8 from 2,522 ratings; counts in many units; widgets and Apple Watch; Data Not Linked to You on its privacy label.

Cons: widgets are ad-free only with an in-app purchase; no habit or streak tools are described.

6. Countdown by Find Appiness

By Find Appiness LLC. Its App Store page is mostly about counting down, and says counting up from a past event is "available on each countdown page". It lists repeating events, tags, notes, counting only working days, iCloud sync and an Apple Watch glance. Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, calendar auto import and alarms are Premium.

Best for: people who mostly count down to dates and want the occasional count-up in the same app.

Price: free to download. The in-app purchases are Countdown Widget and Countdown Widget Offer, described as "Unlock widget and remove ads forever".

Pros: 4.8 from 189,243 ratings; updated in October 2026; Apple Watch.

Cons: count-up is a side feature; widgets are Premium; its privacy label includes Data Used to Track You (Identifiers).

How we chose

  • Starting point: the apps most often suggested as Days Since alternatives, plus US App Store searches for "days since", "last time" and "count up" on 20 and 21 October 2026.
  • Kept: counting apps updated in the last 12 months with hundreds or thousands of ratings, and a way to count up from a past date.
  • Left out: apps with only a handful of ratings, apps not updated for a year, and sobriety apps with their own health programs, which suit a different search.
  • Sources: each app's own App Store page and Apple's lookup data. No paid placements, and no developer reviewed this page.
  • Fair cons: where a listing doesn't mention a feature we say so instead of assuming it's missing. Check the App Store page before you buy, because plans change.

Which one should you pick?

  • You want days since on the Lock Screen, next to countdowns and habits: Left.
  • You want countdowns and days since in one list: DayCount.
  • You want a log of recurring things with averages, paid once: Last Time Tracker.
  • You want history, stats and an Apple Watch app: Hindsight.
  • You want one-time purchases instead of a subscription: Countdown Days Since & Until.
  • You mostly count down: Countdown by Find Appiness.
  • You just want a simple counter for something you're quitting: stay with Days Since.

Questions

Is the Days Since app free?

The app is free to download on the US App Store, with in-app purchases named Count Up Club (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Premium (monthly or yearly) (checked 21 October 2026). Its listing doesn't say which features the free version includes.

What is the best free alternative to Days Since?

Left is free to download with 2 streaks, 2 habits, 2 Aims and 2 countdowns, and its Streak widget is free. Countdown Days Since & Until and Hindsight are also free to download, with in-app purchases or optional support payments.

Can I track when I last did something, not just a quit streak?

Yes. Last Time Tracker and Hindsight are built around logging each time you do something, and they keep the history. In Left you can restart a streak from any moment between its start and now, and it keeps your restarts, average streak and longest streak.

Which Days Since alternatives have an Apple Watch app?

Hindsight has a full Apple Watch app, and Countdown Days Since & Until and Countdown by Find Appiness list watchOS in their compatibility. DayCount and Last Time Tracker don't list Apple Watch. Left doesn't have an Apple Watch app.

Is there a days since widget for the iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes. DayCount, Countdown Days Since & Until, Hindsight and Left list Lock Screen widgets, and Countdown by Find Appiness has one as a Premium feature. In Left, the Streak widget comes as a rectangular Lock Screen widget.
