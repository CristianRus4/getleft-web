Best Lock Screen Widget Apps for iPhone in 2026: 7 Compared

Updated November 5, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

You see your Lock Screen every time you pick up your phone, without unlocking it. iOS 26 lets you add widgets there, and Apple's own apps cover the basics: Apple Support gives "the temperature, air quality, battery level, or upcoming calendar events" as examples. Third-party apps add everything else: countdowns, habits, launchers, photos, richer weather and whole themes. Here are seven apps worth comparing, each good at a different job.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best for time on the Lock Screen (year progress, countdowns, habits and streaks), with free widgets: Left.
  • Best for building your own widgets from scratch: Widgy Widgets.
  • Most-rated all-round customizer: Widgetsmith.
  • Best Lock-Screen-first catalog: LockWidget.

  • Best for weather: CARROT Weather.

1. Left: the time you have left, on the Lock Screen

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. It shows how much of the day, week, month, year and your life is left, counts down to dates in Ahead, and tracks habits, streaks and countable goals (Aims) in Since. Most of that can live on the Lock Screen.

  • Time widgets in all three Lock Screen shapes: Year, Month, Week, Day, Life and You come as circular, rectangular and inline widgets. On the Lock Screen, the Year widget counts down in days, weeks, months, hours or minutes, and the circular size can show the time left as an amount or a percentage, or hide it.
  • Countdowns: the Ahead widget comes in circular and rectangular sizes. Pick a date with Select date and it counts down beside the clock.
  • Habits, streaks and goals: rectangular Habit, Streak and Aim widgets, plus a circular or rectangular Habits list.
  • Clocks: Digital Clock and Analog Clock in all three shapes, and an inline Minutes widget for the minutes left in the hour.
  • Live Activities: a countdown can switch to Get the final countdown, a live countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. A habit can show a persistent reminder an hour before the time you choose if it still isn't logged.
  • Left Wallpaper: a Lock Screen wallpaper that draws your year, month or life as a grid and updates itself every day through a Shortcuts automation. It's free.

Best for: people who want the Lock Screen to show time passing: how much of the year is left, the days until a trip, today's habit.

Price: free to download, with no ads. The time, countdown, habit, streak, Aim and clock widgets and Left Wallpaper are free; the Fasting and Focus widgets are part of Left Premium. The free plan includes 2 each of countdowns, habits, streaks and Aims, counted over every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited items. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: free time, countdown and habit widgets in every Lock Screen shape; time, countdowns and habits in one app; Live Activities for countdowns and habits; a self-updating wallpaper.

Cons: not a general customizer, so no weather, photo or launcher widgets; only 2 free countdowns, habits, streaks and Aims; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

2. Widgy Widgets

By Woodsign jdoo. Its App Store page describes a layer-based editor for custom widgets on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and Apple Watch (through "Watchy"), plus Live Activities and Dynamic Island layouts. Its version history adds StandBy support, interactive buttons and inline Lock Screen widgets built "from full layer designs".

Best for: people who want to design every pixel and pull in their own data.

Price: "Try Widgy for free. Optional in-app purchases unlock additional widget slots and features." Lock Screen slots are their own purchase, listed as "Unlock Lock Screen Slots".

Pros: a full layer editor; Lock Screen, Watch, StandBy and Live Activities; in-app purchases with no subscription listed; its privacy label says Data Not Collected.

Cons: a learning curve; Lock Screen slots cost extra; the number of free slots isn't stated.

3. Widgetsmith

By Cross Forward Consulting, LLC. Its App Store page says "Widgetsmith lets you customize not only your home screen but also your lock screen", with song lyrics, a photo, weather "and more without unlocking your device". On the Home Screen it covers photos, clocks, activity, countdowns, solar and lunar trackers, weather and widgets with actions that launch apps or run Shortcuts.

Best for: a broad set of widget styles from the most-rated customizer here.

Price: free to download, with "an optional premium subscription", monthly or annual. "Access to tide and weather data is only available to Widgetsmith Premium subscribers."

Pros: 4.6 stars from 776,423 ratings, the most on this list; time-based switching, so one widget can show different things through the day; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs; its privacy label lists only data not linked to you.

Cons: the listing doesn't say which Lock Screen widget shapes it offers or which are free; weather needs Premium.

4. LockWidget

By SoCloud Technology Co., Limited. Its App Store page lists "a vast collection of lock screen widgets: weather, lock launcher, contact launcher, quotes, health, photos, clock, time, calendar, moon phase, countdown, year process and more", plus battery, couple and animated widgets and Lock Screen wallpapers. Recent release notes add a guide for inline widgets.

Best for: trying many Lock Screen widget types from one app built around the Lock Screen.

Price: free to download. Weekly, monthly, yearly and lifetime plans are listed, and its release notes mention rewarded-ad unlocks.

Pros: a long list of Lock Screen widget types; inline widgets; wallpapers included.

Cons: the listing doesn't say what's free; rewarded ads; its privacy label lists usage data used to track you.

5. Photo Widget: Theme Wallpapers

By Photo Widget Inc. Its App Store page names ten Lock Screen widget types: Animated, Inline, Shortcut, PhotoWidget, Battery, Calendar, Memo, D-Day, Quotes and Zodiac. D-Day countdowns show on both the Home Screen and Lock Screen, with reminders. It also offers themes, icons and Apple Watch faces.

Best for: photos and simple date countdowns on the Lock Screen.

Price: free with ads. "Unlock all premium content and enjoy an ad-free experience with PhotoWidget Premium. Available in monthly and yearly options."

Pros: ten Lock Screen widget types named in the listing; Apple Watch; iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs.

Cons: ads in the free version; its privacy label lists usage data used to track you.

6. Launcher with Multiple Widgets

By Cromulent Labs, Inc. Its App Store page describes "Lock Screen widgets, both circular and rectangular" that launch apps, contacts and shortcuts, plus Live Activities for the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island and an Apple Watch app. Its version history notes rectangular Lock Screen widgets with up to 6 launchers each.

Best for: one-tap shortcuts to the apps, people and Shortcuts you use most.

Price: free to download, and you can "Launch every app on your device". Launcher Premium adds an "Unlimited number of widgets", folders and iCloud backup; a yearly plan and other Premium purchases are listed.

Pros: a different job from the others, done well; circular and rectangular shapes named; Live Activities and Apple Watch; its privacy label lists only data not linked to you.

Cons: 3,377 ratings, fewer than the other third-party apps here; unlimited widgets need Premium.

7. CARROT Weather

By Grailr LLC. Its App Store page lists "Lock & Home Screen widgets", Live Activities and Apple Watch complications in its Premium Club, alongside radar, alerts, several weather sources and its well-known talking personalities. Its version history adds StandBy widgets.

Best for: weather that's richer than the built-in widget.

Price: free to download; Lock Screen widgets come with Premium Club, offered as monthly, annual and higher tiers.

Pros: 4.7 from 87,630 ratings; Live Activities and Watch complications; iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch; its privacy label lists only data not linked to you.

Cons: widgets need Premium; the listing doesn't say what the free version shows.

What iOS 26 already gives you

You may not need an app at all for the basics. Apple Support's widget article describes Lock Screen widgets for "the temperature, air quality, battery level, or upcoming calendar events", and its iPhone User Guide notes that Weather widgets can go on the Lock Screen too. To add any widget, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Add Widgets. Apple notes that "if there's not enough room for a new widget, you can tap the Remove button" to make space. Questions

What is the best Lock Screen widget app for iPhone?

It depends on what you want to see. Left is built for time: year progress, countdowns, habits and streaks, with free Lock Screen widgets. Widgy suits people who want to build their own widgets, Widgetsmith is the most-rated all-rounder, and CARROT Weather is a strong choice for weather.

How do I add widgets to my iPhone Lock Screen?

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap Add Widgets and choose the widgets you want. If there isn't room, tap the Remove button on a widget to make space, then tap Done. Our Lock Screen widget guide walks through it with Left.

Are Lock Screen widgets free?

Apple's own widgets are free. Third-party apps vary: Left's time, countdown, habit and streak widgets are free, Widgy sells Lock Screen slots as a separate purchase, CARROT Weather includes them in Premium Club, and Photo Widget and LockWidget are free with ads or rewarded-ad unlocks.

Can I put a countdown on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Yes. Left's Ahead widget shows a countdown in a circular or rectangular Lock Screen widget, and a Live Activity can show the final countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Photo Widget's D-Day widget and LockWidget's countdown widget are other options.

Which Lock Screen widget apps have Live Activities?

Left (countdowns and habits), Widgy, Launcher with Multiple Widgets and CARROT Weather, which includes them in Premium Club, list Live Activities.
