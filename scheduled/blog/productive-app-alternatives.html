7 Best Productive App Alternatives for iPhone in 2026

Updated November 12, 2026 · 10 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

Productive is a long-running habit tracker with 4.6 stars from 91,063 ratings on the US App Store. It schedules habits and reminders by part of the day, adds guided programs and challenges, and tracks chains of completed habits. People look for an alternative when they want widgets or Apple Health, which its listing doesn't mention, a one-time price, or a different take on routines. Here are seven alternatives, including ours, and when each one fits.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best for habits on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, next to countdowns and year progress: Left.
  • Most-rated dedicated habit tracker here, with widgets, Health and Watch: Habit Tracker by Inner Grow.
  • Best for guided routines and coaching: Fabulous.
  • Best for step-by-step routines with timers: Routinery.
  • Best for planning your day on a timeline: Structured.
  • Best for morning, noon and night routines with Health auto-logging: Habitify.
  • Best one-time purchase: Streaks.

What Productive does well, and what to know

By Mosaic S.r.l. Its App Store page lets you "Create, complete, stop, or pause habits", get notifications for "what's scheduled for each part of the day", follow programs with "daily habit tips, reflection check-ins, and motivation prompts", and join challenges with "guided tasks and competing with other users". Stats track chains of completed habits and perfect days.

  • Price: free to download. Productive Premium adds an "Unlimited number of habits", challenges, location-based reminders, stats per habit and trends; monthly, yearly and weekly options are listed. The listing doesn't say how many habits are free.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Vision Pro.
  • Recent changes: its last update was in March 2026, and its recent release notes describe bug fixes.
  • Privacy: its privacy label lists purchases, contact info and identifiers used to track you.

If you like Productive's programs and challenges, it's still a good choice. The alternatives below differ on widgets, Apple Health, routines and price.

1. Left: habits you see without opening an app

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. Habits live in Since, next to streaks and countable goals (Aims), with countdowns in Ahead and the time left in the day, week, month and year in Time Left.

  • Habits on your schedule: daily, weekly or monthly goals; every day, weekdays, weekends, specific days, or a number of days per week.
  • Reminders: up to 4 reminders per habit, each with its own days and time. Get a persistent reminder shows a Live Activity an hour before your chosen time if the habit still isn't logged.
  • Progress: days completed, days missed, longest streak, a completion grid and habit strength.
  • Widgets: an interactive Habit widget you tap to log today, a Habits list widget for 4 or 8 habits, and a rectangular Habit widget on the Lock Screen.
  • Left Premium extras: unlimited habits, Apple Health tracking that completes a habit when you meet the goal, friends who can hold you accountable, the Focus timer, and the Planner, a day view with your habits, events and reminders.

Best for: a few habits you want in front of you all day, alongside countdowns and a sense of how much of the day is left.

Price: free to download, with no ads. The free plan includes 2 habits (plus 2 streaks, 2 Aims and 2 countdowns), counted over every habit you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited habits. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Left's privacy label lists only purchases, linked to you.

Pros: a free interactive Home Screen Habit widget and a Lock Screen Habit widget; a Live Activity when a habit is still open; habits, streaks, goals and countdowns in one app.

Cons: only 2 free habits, so most people tracking a routine will need Premium; no guided programs, challenges or location reminders; no Apple Watch app; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

2. Habit Tracker by Inner Grow

By Inner Grow Limited. Its App Store page lists Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets "to log habits instantly", time- or location-based reminders, multiple reminders, Apple Health sync, group habits and friendly competition with friends, and an Apple Watch app.

Best for: Productive users who want location reminders too, plus widgets, Health and challenges with friends.

Price: free to download; monthly, yearly and lifetime Premium options are listed. The listing doesn't say what's free.

Pros: 4.8 from 148,058 ratings; Lock Screen widgets; Apple Health; its privacy label lists only diagnostics, not linked to you.

Cons: the listing says only that Premium unlocks "all features", without saying what's free; challenges are with friends rather than guided programs like Productive's.

3. Fabulous

By Fabulous. Its App Store page describes morning and evening routines, coaching series, journeys, live community challenges, a gratitude journal, short workouts and meditation. It says the app has "evolved into a self-improvement, coaching, and mental health platform". Its release notes add a Motivation widget and Dynamic Island support for routines.

Best for: people who liked Productive's programs and want more coaching.

Price: free to download; Fabulous Premium, including "Unlimited habits for your morning and evening routines", is offered as several subscriptions.

Pros: guided content and challenges; Apple Watch; Dynamic Island routines.

Cons: most coaching content, and unlimited habits for your routines, need Premium; its privacy label lists contact info used to track you.

4. Routinery

By Routinery Corp. Its App Store page, under the name Routine Planner, Habit Tracker, runs routines step by step with timers and a spoken voice, groups them into morning, afternoon and evening, grows a plant for each day you keep a streak, and has a Home Screen widget. Its release notes add Lock Screen widgets.

Best for: following a routine in order, one timed step at a time.

Price: "2 routines free, no account", and the listing says the free plan shows ads. Premium lifts the cap and removes ads; weekly, monthly, 6-month, yearly and family options are listed.

Pros: timed steps; Lock Screen widgets; Apple Watch, iPad and Mac.

Cons: ads in the free version; its privacy label lists other data used to track you.

5. Structured

By unorderly GmbH. Its App Store page describes a visual day planner on a timeline for morning and evening routines and habit tracking. Its free version includes a Pomodoro focus timer, Live Activities, interactive widgets and sync across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac.

Best for: Productive users who mainly used the part-of-the-day view and want a full day plan.

Price: free to download; Structured Pro, monthly, yearly or lifetime, adds calendar and Reminders connections, recurring tasks and routines, and AI planning.

Pros: 4.8 from 167,754 ratings; a generous free version; every Apple device.

Cons: recurring tasks and routines need Pro; its privacy label lists contact info and user content linked to you.

6. Habitify

By Unstatic Ltd Co. Its App Store page offers "Smart reminders for morning, noon, or night", habits you can mark "done, skipped, or missed", friendly challenges, auto-logging from Apple Health and other apps, and sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Best for: Productive users who want parts of the day plus automatic logging.

Price: "Habitify is available for free on all platforms." Habitify Plus adds unlimited habits and smart reminders; Habitify Pro, with a 7-day free trial, adds Apple Health and app integrations.

Pros: skips as well as done and missed; every Apple device; its privacy label lists only usage data and diagnostics, not linked to you.

Cons: the free habit limit isn't clear, as its description and release notes differ; the priciest lifetime option listed here.

7. Streaks

By Crunchy Bagel Pty Ltd. Its App Store page lets you "Track up to 24 tasks you want to complete each day", including negative tasks to break bad habits, timed tasks and tasks linked to Apple Health, with iCloud sync, Home Screen widgets, an Apple Watch app with complications and Siri Shortcuts.

Best for: paying once instead of subscribing.

Price: a paid download, with no in-app purchases listed.

Pros: one price; Apple Health and Watch; its privacy label lists only diagnostics, not linked to you; updated in October 2026.

Cons: no free version to try; up to 24 tasks; no part-of-the-day routines or challenges are described.

Questions

What is the best alternative to Productive?

Habit Tracker by Inner Grow is the closest match, with time- and location-based reminders, widgets, Apple Health and Apple Watch. Fabulous suits you if you liked Productive's programs, Structured if you want a timeline for your whole day, Habitify if you plan by part of the day, and Left if you want a few habits on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Is Productive still updated?

Productive's last update was in March 2026, according to its App Store page checked on 20 October 2026, and its recent release notes describe bug fixes.

Which habit trackers don't need a subscription?

Streaks is a paid download with no in-app purchases listed. Habit Tracker by Inner Grow, Structured and Habitify list lifetime options alongside subscriptions, and Left Premium is a subscription or a lifetime purchase.

Which Productive alternatives have Lock Screen widgets?

Left, Habit Tracker by Inner Grow and Routinery list Lock Screen widgets. Left's rectangular Habit widget shows a habit you pick, with its streak and this week's progress, on the Lock Screen for free.

Which habit trackers work with Apple Health?

Habit Tracker by Inner Grow and Streaks list Apple Health, Habitify includes it in Pro, and Left's automatic Apple Health tracking is part of Left Premium.
