Best Day Counter Apps for iPhone in 2026: 7 Compared

Updated November 9, 2026 · 9 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

A day counter does two jobs: it counts down the days until something and counts up the days since something. The best ones put both on a widget, so the number is there without opening an app. Here are seven day counter apps for iPhone that count in both directions, and what each one is best at.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

Quick verdict

  • Best for days until and days since on the Lock Screen, with free widgets and no ads: Left.
  • Best for widget styling, with streaks in the same app: DayCount.
  • Best with an Apple Watch app and a small privacy label: Countdown Days Since & Until.
  • Best with no purchases at all: Countdown & Count Up Timer.
  • Best literal "day counter" with two modes: Day Counter by BITSENS.
  • Best low-cost lifetime unlock with no data collected: Day Counter . by Inner Grow.
  • Best photo-led countdowns with StandBy widgets: Big Days.

1. Left: days until and days since, both on a widget

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone. It counts in both directions with two features that sit side by side.

  • Days until, in Ahead: add a date with Add a date ahead or import one From calendar. Show as... counts it in hours, days, weeks, months or years, and Repeat (weekly, fortnightly, monthly or yearly) rolls birthdays and anniversaries on by themselves. Reminders go out 1 month to 1 day before.
  • Days since, in Since: create a streak and set Started on... to any past date. It counts up by itself, and Restart resets it when you start again, keeping your average and longest run.
  • Widgets: the Ahead widget on the Home Screen and as a circular or rectangular Lock Screen widget; the Streak widget in small, medium and large and as a rectangular Lock Screen widget; and list widgets for several countdowns or streaks at once. They work in StandBy too.
  • Live Activities: Get the final countdown shows the last stretch on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Beyond days: Time Left shows how much of the week, month, year and your life is left, as grids and widgets.

Best for: a handful of counters that matter, on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, without ads.

Price: free to download, with no ads. The free plan includes 2 countdowns and 2 streaks (plus 2 habits and 2 Aims), counted over every item you've ever created, so deleting one doesn't free a slot. Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited items. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: free Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets for both directions; units from hours to years; countdown Live Activities; year and life progress in the same app.

Cons: only 2 free countdowns and 2 free streaks, while two apps here promise unlimited events; no Apple Watch app; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

Get the day counter widgets

2. DayCount

By Zaminiti Pty Ltd. Its App Store page describes "event counters that track time before and after your date", with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets built from "different filters, fonts, textures, and borders", daily streaks, repeats, notes, calendar import, Face ID lock and sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Best for: people who want to style every widget and keep streaks in the same app.

Price: free to download; "DayCount Premium is available as an annual subscription or a lifetime purchase", with a free 7-day trial on the yearly plan.

Pros: 4.7 from 26,262 ratings, the most here; a detailed widget builder; sync across devices; Siri and Shortcuts.

Cons: the listing doesn't say what's free; Apple Watch isn't listed; its privacy label lists contact info and user content linked to you.

3. Countdown Days Since & Until

By Joseph Sferrazza. Its App Store page describes "counting down (or up)" in seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years "or even sleeps", "Unlimited Events", Home and Lock Screen widgets, calendar import and sync to your devices and Apple Watch. Recent versions add Watch complications, shared countdowns and Siri on iOS 27.

Best for: counters on your Watch face as well as your phone.

Price: free with ads. The listing promises "Unlimited Events" and only marks ad-free widgets as paid ("ad-free with in-app purchase"); Unlock All, Remove Ads and effect packs are listed.

Pros: Apple Watch app and complications; unlimited events; its privacy label lists only diagnostics, not linked to you.

Cons: ads in the free version, including on widgets until you pay; Unlock All is listed without saying what it unlocks.

4. Countdown & Count Up Timer

By Yoji Shimobayashi. Its App Store page says: "Count down to something you're looking forward to, or see how much time has passed since a meaningful day." You can "Create as many events as you like" in years, months, weeks, days or hours, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, recurring events, tags, photo backgrounds and iCloud backup.

Best for: a simple two-way counter with nothing to buy.

Price: free with ads; the page lists no in-app purchases.

Pros: unlimited events; Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets; nothing to unlock; updated in October 2026.

Cons: ads, with no listed way to remove them; its privacy label lists location, identifiers and usage data used to track you.

5. Day Counter by BITSENS

By BITSENS, UAB. Its App Store page calls it "a free, customizable counting app with two powerful modes: count up and countdown", counting days down to seconds, with widgets, reminders and a look back at past events by week, month or year. Its release notes add a free widget for everyone, swiping between days, weeks, months and years, and holiday suggestions by country.

Best for: people searching for exactly a "day counter", with sober days, birthdays and events in one list.

Price: free with ads. The listing names separate purchases for widgets, notes, dark mode, unlimited counters and removing ads, plus All In One bundles. Its description also promises "Unlimited Counters", so what's free isn't clear.

Pros: both modes front and center; a free widget; regular updates.

Cons: many separate unlocks; iPad isn't in its compatibility list; ads; its privacy label lists usage data used to track you.

6. Day Counter . by Inner Grow

By Inner Grow Limited. Its App Store page describes "A cute day counter and reminder app" that counts down to events, counts up days since, lists upcoming holidays, tracks birthdays and even food and item expiry dates.

Best for: a cute, simple counter with a low-cost lifetime unlock.

Price: free to download; Lifetime Premium and a Cute Countdown Meow subscription are listed. The listing doesn't say what's free.

Pros: 4.8 from 1,606 ratings; privacy label says Data Not Collected; updated for iOS 27 in October 2026.

Cons: a very short description that doesn't mention widgets; iPad isn't in its compatibility list.

7. Big Days - Event Countdown

By Vittorio Sorbera. Its App Store page covers "Days remaining & days since", photo backgrounds from Pixabay, custom fonts and colors, reminders and saving a countdown as a wallpaper. Version 10 in August 2026 added Home Screen widgets and "Full support for iOS StandBy mode".

Best for: photo-led countdowns, now on the Home Screen and in StandBy.

Price: free with ads; Big Days Pro and a Widget Pack are listed.

Pros: the only listing here that names StandBy; save any countdown as a wallpaper; running since 2014.

Cons: widgets are new; Lock Screen widgets aren't mentioned; ads; its privacy label lists identifiers, usage data and diagnostics used to track you.

Questions

What is the best day counter app for iPhone?

DayCount is the most rated of the apps we compared and has a detailed widget builder. Left suits you if you want a few counters on the Lock Screen with free widgets and no ads. Countdown Days Since & Until is the pick if you want counters on your Apple Watch.

Is there a free day counter app?

Yes. Countdown & Count Up Timer lists no purchases at all, and Countdown Days Since & Until lists unlimited events without marking them as paid; both show ads. Left is free with no ads, but its free plan includes 2 countdowns and 2 streaks.

Can a day counter app show on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Left, DayCount, Countdown Days Since & Until and Countdown & Count Up Timer all list Lock Screen widgets. In Left, the Ahead widget shows days until and the Streak widget shows days since.

What's the difference between a day counter and a days since app?

A day counter usually counts both ways: days until a date and days since a date. A days since app only counts up, often with a reset button for habits or chores. See our best days since apps for count-up-only apps.

How do I count the days since a date?

Use our days since a date calculator for a one-off answer. To keep counting, add it to a day counter app with a widget, such as a Left streak started on that date.
