A month progress widget for iPhone

A month goes by faster than it looks on a calendar. Left’s Month widget draws the current month as one symbol per day, dims the days that have gone, and shows how many are left. It is a small, honest reminder of how much room is left for this month’s plans, budget or goals.

Why watch the month go by

Many things run on a monthly clock: rent and bills, a budget until the next payday, a sales target, a reading goal, a 30-day challenge. A month progress widget answers the question behind all of them: how much of this month is left? Seeing "11 days left" on October 20 makes it easier to decide whether to push, slow down or plan for next month.

It also helps with the end-of-month surprise. When the last week of the month is visibly running out, things like invoices, reports and renewals are less likely to slip into the next one.

When you edit the Month widget you can pick the Symbol (dots, squares, stars, hearts, flames and more), the Background and Theme, whether to show the Current month name, and whether Time left shows as time, as a percentage, or hidden.

How to add the Month widget

  1. Download Left. The Month widget needs no setup in the app.
  2. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Left, swipe to the Month widget, choose a size and tap Add Widget, then Done.
  4. Touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget to change its look.

On the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen and add the Month widget. The inline size shows the days left in the line above the clock; the circular and rectangular sizes go below it.

The month as your wallpaper

If a widget is too small, Left Wallpaper can draw the month across your whole Lock Screen. Its Month mode shows the days left this month as a grid, and its Calendar mode draws the month as a calendar with day numbers, or as Month Blocks, with the days gone dimmed. The wallpaper updates when a daily Shortcuts automation you create runs. See the days left wallpaper page for the setup.

Pair it with a monthly goal

The days left mean more next to something you are working on. In Left’s Since section, a Habit can run on a monthly period, such as going to the gym 12 times a month, and an Aim counts up or down towards a target with an optional deadline, such as saving a set amount by the last day of the month. Put the Aim widget next to the Month widget and you can compare your progress with how much of the month has gone.

Questions

Is there a widget that shows the days left in the month?

Yes. Left’s Month widget shows the days left in the current month, or the percentage left, under a grid with one symbol per day. It works on the Home Screen in three sizes and on the Lock Screen in circular, rectangular and inline sizes.

Can I show the month progress on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Add the Month widget to the Lock Screen. The inline size fits in the line above the clock, and the circular and rectangular sizes go below it.

Can the widget show a percentage instead of days?

Yes, on the Home Screen and in the circular Lock Screen size. Edit the widget and set Time left to show as a percentage, or hide it and keep only the grid. The inline and rectangular Lock Screen sizes always show the days left.

Is there a widget that shows the months of the year?

Yes. Left’s Months widget shows the twelve months by name, with the months gone faded, on the Home Screen in Small, Medium and Large.

Can I see the day, week, month and year together?

Yes. The Everything widget, in the Medium size, shows the progress of all four at once.

Is the Month widget free?

Yes. The Month widget and the other Time Left widgets are free in Left.
