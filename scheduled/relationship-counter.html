What a relationship counter shows

A relationship counter, or love counter, does one simple thing: it counts the time since a date that matters to the two of you. Usually that is the day you got together, but it can just as well be your first date, the day you moved in together or your wedding day.

Seeing "1,247 days" is different from knowing it has been "about three and a half years". Small milestones become visible: day 100, day 500, day 1,000. Plenty of couples like to mark those, and a counter on the Home Screen means you will not miss them.

How to set up a days together counter in Left

1. Create the counter

  1. Open Left and go to Since.
  2. Tap +, then under Build from scratch choose Streak (it "increases automatically unless you restart it").
  3. Name it, for example "Us" or "Together".
  4. Set Started on... to the date it all began. Past dates are fine; that is the point.
  5. Add a photo of the two of you and pick a color, then save.

The streak keeps counting by itself. It only resets if you tap Restart, so there is nothing to log and nothing to remember.

2. Put it on your Home Screen or Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  2. Search for Left and add the Streak widget in Small, Medium or Large.
  3. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, choose your counter under Select streak, then pick Show streak in... hours, days, weeks, months or years. You can also show the Background photo and the Start date.

On the Lock Screen, add the Streak widget in its rectangular size, under the clock.

Add your anniversary as a countdown

The counter looks back. For the next celebration, add a countdown in Ahead: tap Add a date ahead and search for "anniversary". The Wedding anniversary, Engagement anniversary and Anniversary dinner templates repeat every year, count in days and remind you a week before. Once the date has passed, the countdown rolls over to next year.

Some couples also celebrate every month. Set Repeat to Monthly and the countdown rolls over each month instead. Add the Ahead widget next to the counter, or use the Ahead widget on the Lock Screen, and you can see both numbers at a glance.

Sharing it with your partner

With Left Premium you can add your partner as a friend in Left. A shared anniversary becomes a Joint Ahead date, so the same countdown appears in their Left as well. You can also invite them to watch your streak, which lets them see it and its progress (how sharing works). Your partner can also simply create the same counter on their own phone with the same start date.

Questions

How do I count the days since we got together on iPhone?

In Left, go to Since, tap +, choose Streak under Build from scratch, and set Started on... to the day you got together. Then add Left’s Streak widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen and select that streak.

Can it show months or years instead of days?

Yes. When you edit the Streak widget, Show streak in... lets you choose hours, days, weeks, months or years.

Can I put a photo of us on the widget?

Yes. Add a photo to the streak in Left, then turn on Background photo when you edit the Streak widget.

Can I see it on my Lock Screen?

Yes. The Streak widget has a rectangular Lock Screen size. The Ahead widget for your anniversary countdown comes in circular and rectangular Lock Screen sizes.

Can I count down to our anniversary too?

Yes. Add the date in Ahead with Repeat set to Yearly, or use the Wedding anniversary or Engagement anniversary template, which repeat every year and remind you a week before.

Can my partner see the same counter?

With Left Premium you can add your partner as a friend, invite them to watch the streak and share the anniversary as a Joint Ahead date. Without Premium, your partner can create the same counter with the same start date on their own iPhone.
