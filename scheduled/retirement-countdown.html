Why count down to retirement

Retirement is the kind of date that feels abstract until it suddenly isn’t. Seeing it as a number makes it real enough to plan for: "6 years" is a savings plan, "18 months" is a conversation with your employer, "40 working days" is a handover. And for many people, watching the number fall is simply motivating.

The unit matters. Twenty years in days is a big, slow number; in years it is a clear one. Left lets you change the unit as the date gets closer, so the countdown always reads in the scale that feels right.

How to set up a retirement countdown in Left

1. Add your retirement date

  1. Open Left and go to Ahead.
  2. Tap Add a date ahead (or +) and choose New date. If you are counting down to your final day at work, the Last day at current job template is a good start. For the celebration, there is a Retirement party template too.
  3. Name it, then set Starts to your retirement date and time.
  4. Under More options, use Show as... to pick years, months, weeks, days or hours.
  5. Tap the preview card at the top and choose Add image to add a background photo from My photos or Unsplash: a beach, a garden, the boat.
  6. Save.

2. Put it on your Home Screen or Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  2. Search for Left and add the Ahead widget, or Ahead Bars or Ahead Circle if you prefer to see progress.
  3. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose your retirement date under Select date. In the Ahead widget’s Text layout you can pick the unit and show the date or a percentage; the Grid layout draws the time left as a grid of symbols.

On the Lock Screen, add the Ahead widget in its circular or rectangular size.

Track your savings goal next to it

A countdown says when; an Aim says whether you are on track. In Left’s Since section, create an Aim that counts up towards a target, such as your retirement savings figure, with a unit and an optional deadline. Each time you log progress, the Aim shows how far you have come as a percentage. Add the Aim widget beside the countdown and both numbers are on your Home Screen. See how Aims work.

Left is a countdown and tracking app, not financial advice. Use it alongside the plan you have made with your pension provider or adviser.

The last day, and the years after

For the final stretch, turn on Get ready for the start on the date. Left then shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island for the last 4 hours before your retirement time: the end of the last shift, counted to the second.

Retirement is also a good moment to look at the bigger picture. Left’s life calendar shows your life in weeks, months or years, a reminder that the time after the countdown is the part that matters.

Questions

How do I put a retirement countdown on my iPhone?

Add your retirement date in Left’s Ahead section. Then touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, choose Add Widget, search for Left and add the Ahead widget. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose the date under Select date.

Can the countdown show years and months instead of days?

Yes. Each date has a Show as... setting for years, months, weeks, days or hours, and the Ahead widget’s Text layout lets you pick the unit it displays.

Can I see my retirement countdown on the Lock Screen?

Yes. Add Left’s Ahead widget to the Lock Screen in its circular or rectangular size and choose your retirement date.

Can I track my retirement savings in Left?

You can track a savings figure you enter yourself with an Aim, which counts up towards a target with an optional deadline. Left does not connect to bank or pension accounts.

Can my partner and I share the countdown?

Yes, with Left Premium. Add your partner as a friend and share the date as a Joint Ahead date, so the same countdown appears in their Left too.

Is the retirement countdown free?

Yes. Left’s widgets are free and the free plan includes 2 countdowns and 2 Aims. Deleting one does not free a slot; Left Premium removes the limits.
