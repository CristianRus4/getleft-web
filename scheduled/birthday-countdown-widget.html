Why put a birthday countdown on your screen

Birthdays have a habit of arriving all at once. You know the date, but it is "ages away" until suddenly it is next Tuesday and there is no gift, no booking and no plan. A countdown you see every day fixes the "suddenly" part: 40 days left is a gentle nudge, 7 days left is a clear one.

It works the other way too. Counting down to your own birthday, a child’s first birthday or a milestone like a 30th or a 50th is part of the fun, and a number that shrinks a little every morning makes the wait feel shorter.

How to make a birthday countdown in Left

1. Add the birthday

  1. Open Left and go to Ahead.
  2. Tap Add a date ahead (or +) and search for "birthday". The Birthday party template is already set to repeat every year, count in days and remind you a week before. You can also choose New date and start from scratch.
  3. Name it, for example "Maya’s birthday", and set Starts to the next time the birthday comes round. Set Until to the end of that day, so the countdown moves on once the birthday is over.
  4. Check that Repeat is set to Yearly. This is what makes the countdown roll over to next year once the day has passed.
  5. Under More options, pick how to Show as...: days suits most birthdays; weeks or months work well when it is far off.
  6. Tap the preview card at the top to pick a symbol, or choose Add image for a background photo from My photos or Unsplash.
  7. Save. The birthday now sits in Ahead with the others.

2. Put it on your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  2. Search for Left and add the Ahead widget in the size you like.
  3. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and choose the birthday under Select date. Turn on the background photo if you want their face on your Home Screen.

The Lock Screen works the same way: customize your Lock Screen, tap the widget area and add Left’s Ahead widget in its circular or rectangular size.

Keeping track of several birthdays

If you want more than one birthday in view, the Time Ahead list widget shows several dates at once: up to 3 in the Medium size and up to 7 in Large, each in its own unit. It is a simple way to see whose birthday is next across family and friends.

In the app, sort Ahead by Closest so the next birthday is always on top. Each date can also have a reminder: turn on Get a reminder and choose a month, two weeks, a week, two days or a day before its Starts time, at the time you choose. A month ahead is about right for anything that has to be ordered or posted.

The final hours

The Birthday party template turns on Get the final countdown, a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island for the last 4 hours before the date’s Until time. With Until at the end of the day, that is the evening of the birthday. To count down to the party itself, set Starts to the time it begins and turn on Get ready for the start: the Live Activity then covers the 4 hours before it starts. Either way it stays up to 4 hours unless you dismiss it.

Planning a party together? With Left Premium you can share the date with friends as a Joint Ahead date, so everyone sees the same countdown in their own Left.

Questions

How do I put a birthday countdown on my iPhone Home Screen?

Add the birthday in Left’s Ahead section with Repeat set to Yearly. Then touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, choose Add Widget, search for Left and add the Ahead widget. Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and pick the birthday under Select date.

Will the countdown reset after the birthday?

Yes, if Repeat is set to Yearly. Once the birthday is over (at its Until time), Left moves it to the same day next year and the widget starts counting down again. The Birthday party template has Yearly turned on already.

Can I count down to more than one birthday?

Yes. Each birthday is its own Ahead date, and the Time Ahead list widget shows up to 7 of them at once in the Large size. The free plan includes 2 countdowns in total; Left Premium removes the limit.

Can I get a reminder before a birthday?

Yes. Turn on Get a reminder for the date and choose 1 month, 2 weeks, 1 week, 2 days or 1 day before it starts, at a time that suits you. The Birthday party template sets a reminder 1 week before.

Can the widget show a photo of the person?

Yes. Add a background photo to the date from your library or Unsplash, then turn on the background photo option when you edit the Ahead widget.

Can I see the birthday countdown on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Add Left’s Ahead widget to the Lock Screen in its circular or rectangular size and pick the birthday. A Live Activity can also show the final hours on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
