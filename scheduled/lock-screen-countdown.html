How to put a countdown on your iPhone Lock Screen

You look at your Lock Screen far more often than you open any app, which makes it the best place for a countdown. Left can put one there in three ways: a small Lock Screen widget that is always there, a Live Activity that ticks down the final hours, and a wallpaper that draws the whole countdown and refreshes daily through Shortcuts. You can use one, or all three for the same date.

All three start the same way: open Left, go to Ahead, tap Add a date ahead, and add the date with its Starts and Until times. Under More options, Show as... sets the unit: hours, days, weeks, months or years.

1. Add a countdown widget to the Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen until Customize appears, tap it, then choose Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area below the clock and find Left in the list.
  3. Add the Ahead widget in its circular or rectangular size.
  4. Tap the widget you just added and choose your date under Select date, then tap Done.

The single line above the clock only takes inline widgets. Left’s countdowns don’t come in that size, but its Year, Month and Day widgets do, if you want the days left in the year or month up there. iOS decides when Lock Screen widgets refresh; for a countdown to the second, use the Live Activity below or open the date in Left.

2. Use a Live Activity for the final hours

Open the date in Left, tap More options, and look below Get a reminder for two toggles:

  • Get ready for the start counts down to Starts, for the 4 hours before it.
  • Get the final countdown counts down to Until, the end of the date, for the 4 hours before it.

The Live Activity appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and it stays for up to 4 hours unless you dismiss it. It is the closest thing to a ticking clock on your Lock Screen: ideal for a flight, an exam, a race start or a product launch.

3. Make the countdown your wallpaper

Left’s Wallpaper tool has a Special Date mode that draws one of your Ahead dates as a grid of symbols, with an optional line such as "45 days left". You choose the symbol, colors, light or dark appearance, and where the grid sits so it does not clash with the clock. Save the configuration, then add a Shortcuts automation that runs every day (12:01 AM works well) to set a fresh wallpaper, so the countdown is never out of date.

The full walkthrough is on the days left wallpaper page, and step by step in set up the wallpaper shortcut.

Questions

How do I put a countdown on my iPhone Lock Screen?

Add the date in Left’s Ahead section. Then touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen, tap the widget area below the clock and add Left’s Ahead widget. Tap the widget and pick your date under Select date.

Can a countdown show above the clock on the Lock Screen?

Only inline widgets fit in the line above the clock, and Left’s countdown widgets come in circular and rectangular sizes, which go below the clock. Left’s Year, Month and Day widgets do have an inline size.

How do I get a live countdown in the Dynamic Island?

Open the date in Left and turn on Get ready for the start or Get the final countdown. Left then shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island for the 4 hours before the start or the end of the date.

Can my Lock Screen wallpaper show a countdown?

Yes. In Left’s Wallpaper tool, choose the Special Date mode and pick one of your Ahead dates. Then add a Shortcuts automation that runs every day to set the updated wallpaper.

Why does my Lock Screen countdown not update every second?

iOS decides when widgets refresh, so they update in steps. A Live Activity is the way to see a countdown to the second on the Lock Screen.

Is the Lock Screen countdown free?

Yes. Left’s widgets and Left Wallpaper are free, and the free plan includes 2 countdowns. Deleting a countdown does not free a slot; Left Premium removes the limit.
