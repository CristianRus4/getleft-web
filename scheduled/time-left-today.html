A time left today widget for iPhone

A clock tells you what time it is. It does not tell you how much of the day you still have. Left’s Day widget does: it draws today as 24 symbols, one per hour, dims each hour as it goes, and shows the hours left underneath. Glance at it at 3 PM and "8 hours left" is a very different feeling from "3:00".

Why seeing the time left helps

Most of us are optimistic about how much a day can hold. Seeing what is left, rather than what time it is, makes the remaining hours concrete: there is room for one more big task, or there isn’t. It is a gentle way to decide what to drop before the evening, not after it.

For people who find clocks abstract, including many people with ADHD, seeing time as a grid can make it feel more concrete. See ADHD time blindness on iPhone for more on that.

When you edit the Day widget you can choose the Symbol (dots, squares, stars, hearts, hexagons and more), the Background and Theme, whether to show the Current day, and whether Time left shows as time, as a percentage or not at all.

How to add the Day widget

  1. Download Left. The Day widget needs no setup in the app.
  2. Touch and hold an empty part of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Left, swipe to the Day widget, choose a size and tap Add Widget, then Done.
  4. To change its look, touch and hold the widget and tap Edit Widget.

For the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, tap Customize, choose Lock Screen and add the Day widget. The inline size sits in the line above the clock; the circular and rectangular sizes go below it. iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so the number moves in steps rather than every second.

Count only your waking hours

A full 24-hour day includes the hours you sleep. If you would rather see how much of your waking day is left, use the Everything widget. Set Start of the day and End of the day to the hours you are usually up, for example 7 AM to 11 PM, and its day bar shows progress through those hours only, next to the week, the month and the year.

Today and Now in the app

Open Left and the same idea fills the screen. The Today view draws the day as a grid with the hours left at the top, and Now zooms in on the current hour. The other views take you out to the Week, Month, Year and Your life.

If you want the rest of the day laid out, not just measured, Planner (part of Left Premium) puts your calendar events, reminders, habits and streaks in one day view, with its own widget.

Questions

Is there a widget that shows how many hours are left in the day?

Yes. Left’s Day widget shows the hours left today, or the percentage left, under a grid of 24 symbols, one per hour. It works on the Home Screen in three sizes and on the Lock Screen in circular, rectangular and inline sizes.

Can the widget count only my waking hours?

Yes, with the Everything widget. Set Start of the day and End of the day to your waking hours and its day bar tracks progress through those hours only. The Day widget always uses the full 24 hours.

Can I see the time left today on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Add Left’s Day widget to the Lock Screen. The inline size fits in the line above the clock, and the circular and rectangular sizes go below it.

Is there a widget for the minutes left in the hour?

Yes. Left’s Minutes widget shows the minutes left in the current hour, on the Home Screen or as an inline Lock Screen widget.

Why does the widget not update every minute?

iOS decides when widgets refresh to save battery, so the number moves in steps. Open the Today or Now view in Left for the exact time left.

Is the Day widget free?

Yes. The Day widget and the other Time Left widgets are free in Left.
