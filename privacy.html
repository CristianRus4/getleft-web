Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Left is built and maintained by Cristian Rus ("we", "I"). This policy explains what data Left collects, why, and what your choices are. Plain English first; defined terms after.

What stays on your device

Your habits, streaks, countdowns, custom dates, Planner settings, lifespan inputs, and widget configurations live in a local SQLite store inside Left's App Group container. None of it is transmitted unless you explicitly enable a feature that requires it (sharing with a friend, joint countdowns, iCloud sync).

iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, Left uses CloudKit to sync your habits, streaks, and countdowns between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac under your Apple ID. We never see this data — it lives in your private iCloud database. You can turn iCloud sync off for Left in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

Left account & shared features

If you create a Left account (to send friend requests, start a Joint Ahead, or share a streak), Left stores a minimal profile — username, display name, optional avatar — and the shared items themselves in our backend (Firebase). Only friends you've accepted can see your shared items. Delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account; this removes your profile and all shared data we hold.

Analytics & crash reports

Left collects anonymous usage analytics and crash reports through TelemetryDeck (privacy-first analytics, no IDFA, no personal identifiers) to understand which features are used and where the app is breaking. Aggregated only — never sold, never combined with personal data. You can disable this in Settings → Privacy.

HealthKit & permissions

The Planner reads steps, heart rate, sleep, and similar summaries from HealthKit only after you grant permission, and only on-device. Nothing leaves your device. Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders integration is read-only and stays on-device. Weather summaries come from Apple's WeatherKit using your current location, never logged.

Purchases

Left+ is sold by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment information — only an anonymous receipt indicating that you purchased.

Third parties

Left uses Apple (iCloud, StoreKit, HealthKit, WeatherKit), Firebase (for shared features and the friend graph), and TelemetryDeck (anonymous analytics). No advertising networks. No data brokers.

Children

Left is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will update and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
