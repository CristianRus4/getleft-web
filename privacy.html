Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Left collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app and its related services. Left only accesses data needed to provide app features, purchases, analytics, crash reporting, account sync, and optional integrations you choose to enable. If a feature requires access to Apple services or device permissions, Left only uses that data after you grant permission.

Shared data

Left uses RevenueCat to collect anonymous device and purchase data so the app can determine whether a user has previously bought products inside the app and provide the correct access.

Left also collects analytics and crash reports to understand which features are used and where the app is breaking. Crash reporting is handled through Sentry. This data is aggregated only, never sold, and never shared with third parties for advertising or data brokerage.

By using our app and making in-app purchases, you consent to our sharing of data regarding your usage and consumption of purchased content with Apple, as part of our efforts to resolve refund requests. This information may include details about how you have accessed and interacted with the purchased content. The purpose of sharing this data is to help Apple make an informed decision regarding refund requests. We ensure that such data sharing is done in compliance with Apple's policies and only as necessary to process your requests.

Optional shared data

Left may access Location to show Apple Weather forecasts, HealthKit to show sleep and workout information in the Planner section, Apple Reminders and Apple Calendars to show and manage reminders and calendar events in Planner, and minimal account data so you can share with friends and sync your content between devices.

Each of these features requires your permission first. They are optional and are not required to use the app.

This website

This section covers getleft.app, the website you are reading. The app is covered above.

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure which pages are visited and which download buttons are clicked, so we can tell which pages help people find Left.

Google Analytics collects the pages you view, the page that referred you, an approximate location that Google derives from your IP address, your device and browser type, and a download_click event when you tap a button that leads to the App Store. Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, and none of this data is used for advertising.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, no analytics cookies are stored on your device until you allow analytics in the banner. Elsewhere, the analytics cookies _ga and _ga_<id> are set by default. Wherever you are, you can turn analytics on or off at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

The website also stores two values in your browser’s local storage: left.lang remembers your language, and left.consent remembers your analytics choice. Invite codes are never sent to analytics, and invitation and Todoist connection pages load no analytics at all.
