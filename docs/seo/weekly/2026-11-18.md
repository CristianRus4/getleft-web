## What happened to search

- **The redirect moved the users but not the rankings yet.** Before 26 Oct, left-time.app got about 14 clicks and 380 impressions a day. Since the 301 it gets almost nothing, and getleft.app gets about 3 clicks and 75 impressions a day. Together the two domains had 174 clicks in the window, against about 420 a month on left-time.app alone before the move.
- **Brand search held up partly.** "left app" on getleft.app: 26 clicks, 311 impressions, position 4.2. On left-time.app over 3 months it ranked 2.3 with a 20% CTR. The long tail on the old home page ("left time" 562 impressions, "days left widget", "time left app") has not moved across.
- **Almost every click is brand.** The home page has 84 of 85 clicks. Non-brand clicks: about 2. Blog posts: 0 clicks.
- **One clear opening:** "pretty progress alternatives" had 424 impressions at position 7.4 and no clicks.
- Search Console confirms that Change of Address from left-time.app is active. Domain moves usually take a few weeks to a few months to consolidate.
