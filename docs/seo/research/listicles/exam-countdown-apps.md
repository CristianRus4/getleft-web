## Competitive angle for Left

- The dedicated exam apps gate their best features: in Exam Countdown (ROOT38), widgets beyond the basics, Live Activities, Calendar View and sync are Premium and the free tier shows ads; in Countdown (Find Appiness), the Lock Screen, StandBy and Home Screen widgets are each labelled "Premium feature".
- The student planners (My Study Life, School Assistant, Power Planner) list exams as one item among classes, homework and grades; none of their listings describes a countdown to an exam date, and only Power Planner mentions Lock Screen widgets.
- Newer exam-specific apps (examday, Exam Countdown & Study Planner) have one rating each, so established names with thousands of ratings are the practical comparison set. Exam Countdown's description claims "over 30,000 ratings" while the US page shows 1,662.
