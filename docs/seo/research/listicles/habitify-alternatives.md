# Research: Habitify alternatives (target keyword "Habitify alternatives")

Checked 2026-10-20. Primary sources: the US App Store page (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`, curl) and the iTunes lookup API (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`), re-fetched today (nothing copied from `streaks-alternatives.md`). Developer sites are labelled "dev site" (secondary). "Not stated" means the source does not say.

Method notes:
- The App Store page shows only the top 10 in-app purchases (IAPs); product names repeat at different prices. I list them as shown and flag period labels.
- Only the current version's release notes were in the fetched HTML and API; older version-history entries were not, so widget checks cover description plus current release notes plus dev site.

## Recommendation

Strongest 6 for an iPhone "Habitify alternatives" article:

1. **Streaks** - $5.99 one-off, no IAPs, Apple Watch, widgets; hard cap of 24 tasks (listing).
2. **HabitKit** - grid-style tracker; free up to four habits; cheap Pro with lifetime; iPhone only (no iPad or Watch in compatibility).
3. **Awesome Habits** - widgets incl. Lock Screen, StandBy, Live Activities; iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch; Data Not Collected; $22.99 lifetime listed.
4. **Strides** - trackers, goals and targets, Apple Watch, Mac; free tier plus Plus; Android app exists (dev site).
5. **Habit Tracker (Inner Grow)** - 148K ratings, Apple Health, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Watch; cheapest subscriptions ($7.99 yearly listed).
6. **Way of Life** - simple red/green tracker since 2010; Premium "simply unlocks unlimited habits"; iPhone and Mac only.

Drop and why:
- **Productive** - last updated 2026-03-19; privacy label includes "Data Used to Track You" (Purchases, Contact Info, Identifiers); yearly IAPs up to $79.99.
- **Habitica** - gamified RPG task manager, not a like-for-like tracker; no Apple Watch or Mac in compatibility; 4.3 rating. Use only if the article wants a "gamified" slot (fetched, facts below).
- **TickTick** - task manager that includes habits; see `structured-alternatives.md`.
- **Loop Habit Tracker** - Android-only (alternativeto.net via WebFetch); no block. Also Android-only per the same page: YA Habit Tracker, GroveResurrect.

What people search this for (WebSearch "Habitify alternatives", 2026-10-20):
- Free-plan limit: habi.app (a competitor) says Habitify's free tier is capped at 3 habits. Confirmed today on Habitify's own pricing page (https://habitify.me/pricing: Free "3 habits"). Source for the claim: https://habi.app/insights/habitify-alternatives/ (search summary only; page returned 403).
- Platform/ecosystem and preference: Toolradar ranks Streaks first for Apple devices; AlternativeTo's top picks are Habitica and the Android-only Loop Habit Tracker. Sources: https://toolradar.com/alternatives/habitify , https://alternativeto.net/software/habitify/ (list fetched via WebFetch). Toolradar's list includes non-habit apps (e.g. Productive, Google Drive/Workspace), so treat it with care.

Surprises:
- The App Store listing and the dev pricing page disagree. App Store: tiers named Plus and Pro (Plus Yearly $29.99; Pro Monthly $8.99, Yearly $49.99, Lifetime $119.99; one "PRO Lifetime" at $59.99). Dev pricing page: only Free and Premium (annual "$2.49 per month, billed annually at [Price] per year" with an unfilled placeholder, and Lifetime $59.99), page footer "(c) 2025 Habitify". Tell readers to confirm pricing in the app.
