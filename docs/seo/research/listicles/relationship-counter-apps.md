Every fact below comes from the US App Store page (description, in-app purchases, Compatibility, privacy label, version history) fetched today, plus the iTunes search endpoint (https://itunes.apple.com/search?term=...&country=us&entity=software) fetched today for rating average and count, first release date, current version date and minimum iOS. The iTunes lookup endpoint returned 403 for every call today, so the lookup API was not used and the search endpoint supplied those fields instead. "Not stated" means the listing does not say. It does not mean the app lacks the feature. Developer websites were not used.

Notes:
- The App Store page shows at most 10 in-app purchase rows; repeated names are different price points. Periods are only known when the product name says so.
- Ratings are US averages and counts from the search endpoint (the page rating, rounded, matched in every case).
- None of the listings mention Live Activities. StandBy is mentioned only by LoveCounter - My Love. Apple Watch is listed in Compatibility only for LoveCounter - My Love and The Couple (Days in Love).
- No listing states iCloud sync between two partners' phones. "Shared" in this sheet means the listing says the partner joins in the app (account/invite based). Single-device means the listing never mentions a partner joining.
- The page's privacy label is the developer's self-reported label.

## Competitive angle for Left

- Every app here frames time as one relationship: a count-up since a date, with anniversary and milestone reminders (100 days, 1,000 days, monthly, yearly). The couple-sharing apps bolt on diaries, chat, quizzes and pets, and several carry heavy privacy labels ("Data Linked to You" includes Location, Contact Info, Purchases in Couple Joy).
- Only LoveCounter - My Love states StandBy and an Apple Watch app in its listing; no listing mentions Live Activities. Lock Screen widgets are stated by LoveCounter, My Love (Jahanara) and The Couple, and added mid-2026 by Love Days.
- Ads or ad removal appear in nearly every free tier (ad-removal IAPs from $0.99 to $9.99). Only the 2023-stale My Love (Bartels) shows "Data Not Collected".
