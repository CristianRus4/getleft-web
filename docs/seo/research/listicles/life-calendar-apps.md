# Life-calendar apps: research sheet

Target queries: "life calendar app" / "life in weeks app" / "memento mori app". All facts come from sources fetched on **2026-10-20**: the US App Store page (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`) and the iTunes lookup API (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`). "Not stated" means the listing does not say. It is not a claim the feature is absent.

## Recommendation

Honest caveat first: this category is tiny. Only **Time Left** has more than 100 ratings (240) among the life-in-weeks candidates; the rest have 5 to 48. The search for "life in weeks" turned up about 20 more apps with 0 to 5 ratings; none beat the list below.

**Include (6):**
1. **Time Left • Be Present** (240 ratings, 4.55, updated 2026-10-12). Clear leader. Weeks calendar, privacy-first. Widgets and skins are Pro.
2. **Life in Weeks - WeeksAlive** (5 ratings, updated 2026-10-19). The only candidate that states Home Screen, Lock Screen and an automatic wallpaper, plus a journal. Subscription after a trial. Flag: privacy label lists tracking.
3. **Memento Mori: Death Clock** (21 ratings, 4.90, updated 2026-06-14). Life blocks grid, widgets, adjustable life expectancy, journal. Best match for "memento mori app".
4. **Life Dots: Visualize Time** (33 ratings, 4.97, updated 2026-09-29). Added from search. Free, no IAP, Data Not Collected, widgets; shows an expected-lifespan dots grid.
5. **Life Time Left: 4K Weeks** (18 ratings, updated 2026-02-11). 4,000-weeks grid with a journal; core features gated by subscription.
6. **Life Calendar: Visual Timeline** (12 ratings, 3.67, updated 2026-05-05). One-time $9.99, notes and photos per week. Weakest ratings; keep only if a one-time-purchase option is wanted.

**Optional (only if the article has a Stoic/memento-mori section):** **Stoic Journal & Mindset: Mori** (48 ratings, 4.42, updated 2026-10-17): Death Clock is one feature in a broad Stoic app; no weeks grid described.

**Drop:**
- **Lifetime: Life Progress**: last update 2025-06-09 (over 12 months), subscription at $49.99/month or $99.99/year, broad Data Linked to You label, 15 ratings.
- **Weeks - Memento Mori**: 5 ratings, version 1.0.2, no widgets stated, minimal listing (not abandoned: updated 2025-12-26). Fine as a footnote only.
- **Death Clock: The Life Lab** (17,595 ratings): a health/longevity subscription (up to $99.99) with tracking label, not a life calendar. Mention only as context.
- **When Will I Die? - Death Clock** (6,048 ratings): entertainment quiz, tracking label.

**Availability:** all eleven IDs resolved on the US store. None missing.

**Which show a weeks grid (per listing):** Time Left (Life in Weeks calendar), WeeksAlive (every dot a week), Life Calendar: Visual Timeline (square per week), Lifetime (dots, each a week), Life Time Left 4K (4K weeks), Weeks - Memento Mori (weeks). Grid of blocks or dots but weeks not named: Memento Mori: Death Clock, Life Dots. No grid described: Mori, Death Clock: Life Lab, When Will I Die.
**Widgets stated:** Time Left (Pro), WeeksAlive (Home, Lock), Lifetime (Pro, Home), Memento Mori: Death Clock (Home), Life Dots (Home, Lock), Life Calendar: Visual Timeline (mentioned in release notes only), Mori (widgets, kind unspecified).
**Wallpaper stated:** WeeksAlive only (automatic wallpaper).
**How lifespan is set (per listing):** Weeks - Memento Mori: birthdate plus life expectancy, both editable. Lifetime: country average, customizable. Memento Mori: Death Clock: adjustable life expectancy. Time Left: birthdate (listing), "chosen life expectancy" (developer site). Life Dots: "expected lifespan" (how set Not stated). Others: Not stated.
