- **Where Lock Screen widgets go / how to add:** Apple's page does not say where on the screen they sit. Steps from the Create-a-Lock-Screen page: "Press the side button on your iPhone two times. - **How many fit:** A number is not stated on any Apple page fetched. Sizes (circular, rectangular, inline): not named by Apple on these pages.
- **Which built-in widgets Apple names:** No list of built-in Lock Screen widgets. Stocks and News have their own widget pages in the guide (not read for Lock Screen content).

## Competitive angle for Left

- Every featured app is a general widget builder, theme pack, launcher or weather app; the listings sell customization and information, and the two biggest (Widgetsmith, Color Widgets) name countdowns only as one widget type among many.
- Lock Screen widgets are often paywalled or sold separately: Widgy sells "Unlock Lock Screen Slots" at $7.99, CARROT lists "Lock & Home Screen widgets" under Premium Club, and LockWidget and Photo Widget use ads or rewarded-ad unlocks.
- Apple's own pages say nothing about a slot count or the exact built-in Lock Screen widget names, so listing-based claims about "how many widgets fit" cannot be sourced from Apple's guide today.
