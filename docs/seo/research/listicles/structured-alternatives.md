# Research: Structured app alternatives (target keyword "Structured app alternatives")

Checked 2026-10-20. Primary sources: the US App Store page (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`, curl) and the iTunes lookup API (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`), both fetched today. Developer sites are labelled "dev site" (secondary). "Not stated" means the source does not say; nothing here claims an app lacks a feature unless its listing says so.

Method notes:
- The App Store page shows only the 10 top in-app purchases (IAPs), and the same product name often repeats at different prices (these are separate listed IAP entries; the page does not say why). I list them as shown. Only some carry a period label (monthly/yearly/lifetime); that is flagged per app.
- Only the current version's release notes are in the fetched page and API. Older version-history entries were not in the HTML I fetched, so widget/Live Activity checks cover the description plus current release notes only.

## Recommendation

Write the article about Structured's visual-timeline day planning. Strongest 6 for an iPhone-focused "Structured alternatives" article:

1. **Tiimo** - closest like-for-like: visual daily timeline, AI co-planner, Apple Watch, widgets. (Its listing also uses neurodivergent/ADHD positioning; keep claims modest.)
2. **Planny** - timeline of Reminders plus Calendar on one day view, Live Activity focus timer, no account (needs iOS 26+, which limits reach).
3. **TickTick** - cheapest big task-plus-calendar app ($4.99 monthly listed), habits and Pomodoro, 4.9 rating from 46K ratings.
4. **Things 3** - paid one-off app ($9.99), Today/Upcoming with calendar events, Apple-only.
5. **Akiflow** - calendar-plus-tasks time blocking with Apple Watch app; no free plan (dev site), $4.99 weekly IAP is listed.
6. **Sorted³** - unified timeline of events and tasks, "NO Subscription" one-time PRO. Caveat: last App Store update 2024-10-15 (maintenance note only). Keep only if the article is happy to flag that.

Drop and why:
- **Sunsama** - iPhone app is a "Companion app to the desktop app"; you "can use the Sunsama mobile app once you've planned your first day from the Sunsama desktop app". No Apple Watch/Mac in compatibility; 511 ratings.
- **Motion** - $44.99 monthly / $294.99 annual IAPs; listing says "this mobile app is a companion to our web/desktop app and is not a standalone app"; last updated 2025-11-27.
- **Amie** - 3.4 rating (128 ratings), last updated 2025-08-06, iPhone/iPod only.
- **Todoist, Fantastical, Timepage** - fetched and fine, but they are task lists or calendars, not visual day planners (see "Also checked"). Todoist could be a 7th if a task-manager angle is wanted.
- **Llama Life, Routinery** - better suited to Tiimo/ADHD article (timer/routine tools); see `tiimo-alternatives.md`.

What people search this for (WebSearch "Structured app alternatives", 2026-10-20):
- Lifestack and Saner.ai guides frame alternatives around AI auto-scheduling, cross-platform task managers and free options (Google Calendar, Microsoft To Do); no source fetched states a single reason such as price. Sources: https://www.saner.ai/blogs/best-structured-alternatives , https://lifestack.ai/blog/structured-alternative (both vendor-adjacent blogs; search-result summaries only, pages not fetched).
