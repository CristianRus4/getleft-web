# Research: HabitKit alternatives (target "HabitKit alternatives")

Checked 2026-10-20. Facts come from the US App Store page and iTunes lookup API fetched today (all candidates re-fetched; nothing carried over from streaks-alternatives.md). "Not stated" means the listing is silent; it does not mean the app lacks the feature.

Notes:
- The page shows at most 10 IAP rows. Ratings are US values from the lookup API.
- WebSearch "HabitKit alternatives" returned only AlternativeTo, which lists Loop Habit Tracker, Habitica, YA Habit Tracker, Checker, Duse, Ritus, QuestLog, Habit Radar and LaHabit (https://www.alternativeto.net/software/habitkit/?p=5). None of these is verified here; the App Store is the source for all facts below. One result quotes a reviewer: "You can set 4 habits to track before being prompted to subscribe."

## Recommendation

Strongest 6 to feature (against HabitKit: free to 4 habits, widgets Pro, no Watch listed, Lock Screen/StandBy not stated):
1. **Habit & Goal Tracker GridKit** (6661028276) - nearest look (color-coded grids), Lock Screen, Control Center widgets, iCloud; but 1 free habit, min iOS 26.0, no Watch.
2. **Awesome Habits** (1514915737) - heatmaps; interactive Home, Lock Screen, StandBy widgets; Live Activities; Watch; iCloud; Lifetime $22.99; "Data Not Collected". 940 ratings.
3. **Habit Tracker by Inner Grow** (1438388363) - 148k ratings, Watch, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Health sync, group habits.
4. **Streaks** (963034692) - $5.99 once, 24-task cap, Watch.
5. **Habitify** (1111447047) - Watch, Live Activities, auto-logging, AI; priciest.
6. **Way of Life** (393159800) - free cloud backup, chain/red-green journal, widgets; no Watch.

Drop or mention only:
- **Grit** (6446997766): min iOS 26.0 on iPhone, privacy label includes tracking; widgets and Watch fine. Optional.
- **(Not Boring) Habits** (1593891243): "No streaks. No guilt."; opposite proposition, no Watch.
- **HabitGrid by Edvard Adam Eros** (6746483721): free tile grid, only 50 ratings; mention as a free lookalike at most.
- **everyday** (1394150432), **Strides** (672401817), **Evoday** (1403517519), **Goal Streak** (1630626343), **Ripples** (6502667826): fetched but not documented here; other names from the "contribution graph" search were not fetched.
- Other grid-named apps in search with 0 to 6 ratings were ignored.
