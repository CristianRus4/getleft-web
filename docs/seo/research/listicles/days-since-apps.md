# Days-since apps: research sheet

Target query: "best days since app" / "last time I did it tracker". All facts below come from sources fetched on **2026-10-20**: the US App Store page (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`) and the iTunes lookup API (`https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`). "Not stated" means the listing does not say. This is not a claim the feature is absent.

## Recommendation

**Include (6):**
1. **Days Since: Quit Habit Tracker** (19,499 ratings, 4.82, updated 2026-10-13). The category leader by ratings among dedicated days-since apps. Habit-quitting angle.
2. **DayCount** (26,262 ratings, 4.66, updated 2026-10-11). Counts before and after dates, Home and Lock Screen widgets, streaks. Yearly sub or lifetime.
3. **Last Time Tracker** by Sarun Wongpatcharapakorn (636 ratings, 4.61, updated 2026-10-18). Closest to the "last time I did it" query. Paid ($5.99), Data Not Collected.
4. **Hindsight - Time Tracker** (395 ratings, 4.55, updated 2026-10-13). History, stats, full Watch app, widgets. Requires iOS 26.
5. **Countdown Days Since & Until** (2,522 ratings, 4.76, updated 2026-10-11). Free, Watch sync, one-time purchases.
6. **Countdown** by Find Appiness (189,110 ratings, 4.81, updated 2026-10-19). Added from search: huge audience, supports counting up from past events, but widgets are Premium.

**Drop or mention briefly:**
- **Day Counter & Tracker - Since**: works, but ad-supported, last update 2026-01-02, minimum iOS 18.6, widgets/Watch not stated, messy IAP list.
- **Countdown Buddy** (26,190 ratings): widget-only, free tier is one basic widget; countdown-first.
- **Countdown & Count Up Timer** (2,317 ratings): tracking label (Location, Identifiers, Usage Data); no IAP; fetched for completeness.
- Seen in search, not fetched (names and counts from today's search API only): **I Am Sober** (189,228 ratings) is a sobriety app, off-topic for a general days-since list. Many other "Days Since" or "Last Time" apps have 0 to 60 ratings and were ignored (for example Quitly 59, Days Since - Track Memories 155 with last update 2026-04-09).

**Availability:** all candidates resolved on the US store. None missing.

Notes for the writer: the two extra apps I added are Countdown (1403367428) and Countdown Buddy (1534850579). No dedicated days-since app other than the original six has both many ratings and a 2026 update.
