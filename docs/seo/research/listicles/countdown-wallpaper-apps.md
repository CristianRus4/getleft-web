Notes for every block:
- Fetched for every app: `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>` and `https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`.
- The App Store page shows at most 10 in-app purchase rows; regional price-tier duplicates repeat product names at several prices, and the listing does not label durations unless the name does.
- Platform note: iOS lets only the Shortcuts app change the wallpaper automatically. "Today: Year Calendar Wallpaper" says this in its own listing ("iOS lets only the Shortcuts app change a wallpaper"). Apps that update the wallpaper by themselves therefore rely on a Shortcuts automation that the user adds once.
