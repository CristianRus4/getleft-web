# Countdown Star alternatives: research sheet

Target keywords: "Countdown Star alternatives", "countdown app alternatives".
Checked 2026-10-20. Every fact below was read on 2026-10-20 from the US App Store page (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id<ID>`, fetched with curl; description, What's New, version history, In-App Purchases, compatibility, privacy labels, ratings) and `https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<ID>&country=us`. "Not stated" means the sources fetched do not say it. Developer sites were not used as sources. The earlier sheets `countdown-wallpaper-apps.md` and `birthday-countdown-apps.md` were not reused; everything was re-fetched.

Caveats on the data:
- The App Store page version history lists at most 25 versions per app, so "not in release notes" means "not in the versions shown" (25 shown for every app checked, including Countdown Star).
- In-App Purchase lists on the pages repeat items at different prices (regional or offer variants). They are copied as listed. The page does not say which price is current for a given user.
- "First release year" is the `releaseDate` from the iTunes lookup (first App Store release of that listing).
- Ratings are US storefront figures.

## Recommendation

Countdown Star is a free-with-ads app (Remove Ads $1.99, or a separate $1.99 "Ad-Free" app) from a solo developer. It has the biggest rating base of the group (216,279) but is in the middle of a 3.0 rewrite with several release-note-stated problems (see section below). The good alternatives are the ones that update often, have large rating counts, and have clear Lock Screen / StandBy / Watch stories.

Use these 6 (strongest current, relevant):
1. **Countdown by Find Appiness** (1403367428): 189,110 ratings, 4.81, updated 2026-10-19. Lock Screen, StandBy and Home Screen widgets and Apple Watch, but the widgets are labelled "Premium feature". Best direct rival on scale.
2. **Countdown: Event Countdown by ROOT38** (983258067): 23,902 ratings, 4.75, Live Activities, Lock Screen, StandBy, Calendar View. Widgets/Live Activities are Premium; free tier is unlimited countdowns. No Apple Watch in the compatibility section.
3. **Countdown@** (1456997268): 40,427 ratings, 4.70, one premium purchase ($4.99), Apple Watch, extra-large widgets for iOS 27 (v3.62, 2026-10-06). Has ads.
4. **Days • Countdown & Widgets** (939368917): 12,675 ratings, 4.76, Lock Screen widgets, Apple Watch, no ads label, cheap Lifetime Pro ($12.99/$14.99 listed).
5. **DayCount** (1121088244): 26,262 ratings, 4.66, widget builder, Lock Screen widgets, and a separate "Streaks and Habits" mode (relevant for the streak article). No Apple Watch listed.
6. **Countdown - Pretty Progress** (1597616326): 3,109 ratings, 4.71, widgets for Home, Lock Screen, StandBy, Watch complications, Dynamic Island (v2.2.0). Needs iOS 18.0, so the smallest audience of the six, but the best widget/Watch coverage. Free tier is limited to four custom widgets.

Drop, and why:
- **TimeCount** (1497946231): 24,583 ratings and decent, but compatibility lists no Apple Watch or iPad, the IAP list runs to $19.99/yr subscription and $44.99 "Premium Forever", and the description gives no Lock Screen / StandBy detail. Keep as a 7th fallback only.
- **Big Days** (940109775): only 592 ratings; widgets and StandBy only arrived in 10.0.0 on 2026-08-07 ("Widgets are finally here!"); shows "Contains Advertising"; no Lock Screen widget mentioned.
- **Countdown Widget & Photos** (6450153688): only 7 ratings (4.0), requires iOS 26.0, description says "No ads" but the age-rating box shows "Contains Advertising". Interesting feature set (Live Activities on Dynamic Island, one-time $2.99) but too new and too small to recommend.
- Also seen, not researched in full: Countdown by Sevenlogics (424216726, 11,427 ratings but 3.78 average), Countdown Buddy (1534850579, 26,190 ratings, last update 2026-05-11, needs iOS 18.0), Bears Countdown (1536711520, $3.99, last update 2024-02-18), Outside: Countdown for Couples (1611434405, 2,760 ratings, shared countdowns for couples, subscription). Details in "Names from WebSearch" below.
