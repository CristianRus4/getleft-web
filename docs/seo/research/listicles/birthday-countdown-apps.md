# Research: birthday countdown apps (targets "birthday countdown app" / "days until my birthday app")

Checked 2026-10-20. Every fact below comes from the US App Store page and the iTunes lookup API fetched today. "Not stated" means the listing (description, release notes, version history, compatibility, privacy label) does not say. It does not mean the app lacks the feature. Developer websites were not used.

Notes:
- The App Store page shows at most 10 in-app purchase rows; repeated names are different price points. Periods are only known when the product name says so.
- Ratings are US averages and counts from the lookup API.
- No listing in this set mentions Live Activities. Only one mentions StandBy (Countdown by Find Appiness, a generic countdown app).

## Recommendation

Strongest 6 to feature (current, relevant, rated):
1. **Birthday Countdown, Find Appiness** (1452049360) - 4.80 from 7,994, updated 2026-10-01. Imports from Contacts, Calendar and Facebook; Lock and Home Screen widgets (Premium) and Watch app; reminders on the day, 1 day or 1 week before; birthday notes for gift ideas; sharing. Closest feature match for "days until my birthday".
2. **Birthday Reminder & Countdown, New Marketing Lab** (1453656360) - 4.69 from 10,189. Contacts import, shows age turning and days left, 6 widgets (Lock and Home), gift shopping, iCloud sync in Pro. No Watch. Last updated 2025-12-01.
3. **hip: Birthday Reminder App** (401949944) - 4.68 from 17,790, updated 2026-10-08. Contacts and calendar sync, Apple Watch, gift ideas (US only), cards, notes. Needs iOS 18.6; widget details not stated.
4. **Birthday Calendar and Reminder, Christopher Wolf** (1508329346) - 4.95 from 1,382. Watch app, widget, several reminders, imports, "Data Not Collected". Smallest developer; good "privacy" angle.
5. **Birthday Countdown, Sevenlogics** (417047728) - 4.43 from 2,136. Visual countdown with age mode, Lock Screen widgets, photos and music; Home Screen widgets Premium. No Contacts import or Watch stated.
6. **Countdown Star** (576177593) - 4.77 from 216,279. Not birthday-only but the biggest audience; annual repeat, Watch, Small/Medium/Large widgets, iCloud. Only $1.99 to remove ads.

Documented but dropped:
- **Birthday Countdown, Zaffles** (6462927847): 267 ratings, widgets only, no import or Watch stated, release notes often just repeat the description.
- **Countdown, Find Appiness** (1403367428): excellent widgets (StandBy, Lock Screen) and Watch, but a generic events app by the same developer as #1; use as a "general countdown" mention if needed.
- **BirthdaysPro** (390055439): widgets and calendar sync paid, no Watch, privacy label shows linked data and tracking.
- **Birthday Countdown • Reminder, HAPPYPIXEL** (1550516996): generic release notes, 428 ratings, Watch not listed.
- Not fetched in full (tiny rating counts or poor fit from the search results): Birthday Reminder Pro+ (427341961, $0.99), Birthday Calendar + (459270366, 3.77), Widget | Countdown to birthday (1545098525, 3.93), Countdown (Sevenlogics, 424216726, 3.78).

Competitive angle for Left: all of these count down to a date or list birthdays. None frames time awareness or "how much of the year is left", and none mentions Live Activities.
