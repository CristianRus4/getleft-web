## Competitive angle for Left

Way of Life's listing sells a green/red journal with charts and notes, free backups and a "private by design" stance, but lists no iPad or Watch and does not state its free habit cap. The alternatives differ on a few axes: price (Habit List $4.99 once, Streaks $5.99 once), structure (Strides goals, Daylio mood) and platforms (everyday adds Web). None of the listings frames time left in a day, year or life as the way to see progress, and none of these listings carries "Advertising".
