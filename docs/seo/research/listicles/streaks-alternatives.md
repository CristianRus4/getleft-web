# Research: Streaks app alternatives (target keyword "Streaks app alternatives")

Checked 2026-10-20. App facts come from the US App Store page and the iTunes lookup API fetched today. "Not stated" means the listing does not say. Nothing here says an app lacks a feature unless its listing says so.

Notes for every block:

## Recommendation

Streaks' two pain points, per its own listing, are paid-upfront ($5.99) and a hard cap of 24 tasks. Alternatives worth featuring are the ones that are free to start, have Apple Watch apps and currently update.

Strongest 4 to 6 to feature:
1. **Awesome Habits: Habit Tracker** (1514915737) - the closest feature match: interactive Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, Live Activities and Dynamic Island, Control Center widgets, Apple Watch complications and Smart Stack widget, iCloud sync, Apple Health, Siri Shortcuts; "Data Not Collected". Lifetime Premium $22.99 visible. Min iOS 18.0. 4.80 from 940.
2. **HabitKit** (6443918070) - the visual rival (a contribution-style tile grid per habit). Free up to four habits; Pro is monthly, yearly or lifetime. 4.84 from 2,492. No Apple Watch app listed (iPhone, iPod touch, Mac, Vision).
3. **Habit Tracker by Inner Grow** (1438388363) - the volume leader: 148,034 ratings (4.79), Apple Watch, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Apple Health, group habits with friends, location-based reminders. Lifetime Member $19.99 visible.
4. **Habitify** (1111447047) - Apple Watch, Health and third-party auto-logging (Fitbit, Strava, Screen Time), AI/MCP/App Intents. Priciest: Pro Monthly $8.99, Yearly $49.99, Lifetime $119.99 visible. 4.59 from 7,142.
5. **Strides** (672401817) - habits plus goals with four tracker types, Apple Watch, Health. Free tier plus Strides Plus (subscription or lifetime; prices range from $4.99 to $149.99 in the visible rows). 4.79 from 19,242. Sync needs Plus.
6. **Atoms - from Atomic Habits** (6474421906) - the brand-name pick (James Clear), Apple Watch app. Updated 2026-02-16 (8 months ago), widgets and iCloud not stated in its listing, Pro $4.99 per month or $39.99 per year. 4.83 from 10,749. Include if you want a book-brand option. **Grit** (6446997766) is the alternative sixth pick, but it requires iOS 26.0 or later (stated in its compatibility), which narrows the audience.

Drop or demote:
- **Productive** (983826477): last updated 2026-03-19, premium visible up to $79.99 per year and $10.99 per month, privacy label includes tracking (Contact Info, Identifiers, Purchases). Still within 12 months, but a weak fit for a "fair alternative" list.
- **Done: Habit Tracker** (6757984661, Remi Herbiet): the only app with this name in the US search; 3 ratings at 2.67, first release 2026-01-20. Drop. (If the intended "Done" is a different app, it did not appear in US search results for "Done habit tracker".)
- **(Not Boring) Habits** (1593891243): positions itself as "No streaks. No guilt." Interesting as a counter-position, not a Streaks alternative per se. Min iOS 18.0, no Watch listed.
- **Way of Life** (393159800): current and well rated (4.82 from 5,317), but no Apple Watch app listed, and a premium price list dominated by $29.99. Keep as optional.

Competitive angle for Left: every listing here is about daily habit chains. None of them mentions time awareness.
