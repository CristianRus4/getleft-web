## Recommendation

Strongest 4 to 6 to feature (all updated within the last 4 months, all with a custom-widget builder as the core idea):

1. **Widgy** (1524540481) - the closest "build it yourself" rival. Layer-based editor, Lock Screen and Apple Watch ("Watchy"), Live Activities, community designs, no data collected. Free with paid slot unlocks.
2. **Color Widgets** (1531594277) - the biggest direct competitor by audience (527k ratings). Widgets plus themes, icon packs, wallpapers. Pro has a 3-day trial.
3. **Photo Widget: Theme Wallpapers** (1530149106) - the only mainstream theme app that lists an Apple Watch app (watchOS 9+), plus D-Day countdown widgets with reminders.
4. **iScreen** (1534704608) - 4.74 stars, 153k ratings, Dynamic Island, interactive widgets, iOS 27 extra-large widget. Many VIP price points.
5. **Themify** (1534126062) - the only one that explicitly lists StandBy widgets and 3-in-1 themes. Heavy privacy label (see below), so mention it only with that caveat.
6. **Launcher with Multiple Widgets** (905099592) OR **Brass** (1533158013). Launcher is the best "different job" pick (app launching widgets, Apple Watch, Live Activities, one-time-style Pro prices). Brass has 188k ratings but its listing copy is dated (iOS 14 era) and the last update was 2026-07-14.

Drop or demote:
- **Widgetable** (1641107226): a social widget app for couples and friends (shared pets, distance, mood). iPhone only. It is not a Widgetsmith-style customizer. Highest rating of the group (4.92) but wrong job.
- **ThemePack** (1616629991): listing mentions only Time, Date, Calendar and Photo widgets; weekly VIP at $3.99 and $9.99, monthly $14.99 in the visible IAP list. Poor fit and aggressive pricing for a fair comparison article.
- **Widget Box** (1527561558): last updated 2024-06-24 (over 12 months ago). Drop.
- **Top Widgets+** (6446477593) and **WidgetClub** (1580284904): current and decent (4.61 / 4.70) but small review counts and heavy theme-shop pricing. Keep as "also considered" only.
- OneWidget, Widget Wizard, Widgeridoo: tiny rating counts or not found in the US store (see "Other names").

Competitive angle for Left: none of these is a time-awareness app. Their clock, countdown and calendar widgets are decoration modules inside a general-purpose widget builder.

## What Apple's built-in iOS widgets cover (one line)

Apple's own widgets come from first-party apps (the support page names Mail, Reminders, Podcasts, Home and News), can be placed on the Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy, are tappable (for example checking off a Reminders item), come in size and style options, and can be grouped in a Smart Stack. Source: https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-widgets-iphb8f1bf206/ios (fetched 2026-10-20). The page does not list a time-awareness or countdown widget among those named.
