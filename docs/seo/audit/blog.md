## Summary

- **Strong:** the blog is accurate against the claims doc almost everywhere. No Mood, Time Between, prices, trial lengths, Control Center, user counts or testimonials; free-tier wording (2 each, lifetime) and Premium attribution are right; iOS 26 stated. Best pages: `what-is-year-progress` (36, maths checked: day 292 = 80.0%), `best-countdown-widgets-iphone` (36, dated App Store sources), `how-to-add-widgets-iphone` and `how-to-build-a-streak` (34). Average 30.0/40.
- **One hard-rule breach:** `digital-clock-date-widget-iphone` FAQ answers "Does Left work on iPad and Mac? No." (blog-02, 1 unit). `left-apple-watch-app` still describes an unreleased watch app but is noindexed by design (state noted, no restore recommended).
- **No search traction yet:** getleft.app GSC shows 0 impressions for every blog URL in 16 months (the page export lists no /blog/ URL; the indexing export has no /blog/ rows, so indexing is unverified). The only blog URL with impressions is `left-time.app/blog/how-to-add-widgets-iphone.html` (433 impr, 1 click, pos 8.3). Demand figures below are therefore topic-level left-time.app queries, and most rows are 0.

Three systemic issues:
1. **Health and research claims without sources (E-E-A-T):** 13 ADHD pages cite nothing external; unsupported statements include "ADHD-tested", "science-informed", body doubling "well-documented" (Medical News Today: no research), "60-90 days" habit formation (Lally 2010: median 66, range 18-254) (blog-04..07). Every post shows the same bulk "Updated October 20, 2026" with an Organization author and no published date (blog-08).
2. **Translation maintenance:** all 18 catalogs are ~99% complete per page, but 44 posts have one stale unit (the inline download button, re-keyed when its href gained tracking parameters), so live de/es/es-419 pages show the English button label (blog-01, no English change). Also the "Updated" line is a translation unit, so every date bump costs 1 unit per page per locale.
3. **Thin internal linking beyond `/countdown-widget`:** 43 of 47 posts link to no /tools page, 19 link to no /support article, `/life-calendar` has 0 blog links, 5 posts have no inbound blog link (blog-15..17). Plus 3 accuracy slips that need app facts (Year widget cannot take custom dates, wallpaper has no free-text label, "with screenshots" pointers) (blog-03, 10, 11).

Translation rule for every fix: blog English is frozen while translations run, so each fix in the JSON lists exact unit ids and live locales (de, es, es-419; all others are unreleased). Fixes that add more than about 10% new units (blog-19, blog-20) would push the live locale copies under the 90% publishing threshold and unpublish them until retranslated.
