# Fix list: ranked by demand x gap (CNTXT-885)

Built 2026-10-21 from the four scorecards (`blog.md`, `tools.md`, `support.md`, `site.md`), the locale sample (`translations.md`) and an independent Opus check of the first 20. **No live page was edited.** Lane 0 and the owner decide what to apply and when.

## How the ranking works

- **Demand** = getleft.app Search Console impressions (16 months) + matching left-time.app query impressions (16 months, from `docs/seo/gsc/2026-10-19/left-time.app/query-16m.tsv`; only queries judged relevant to the page) + the best App Store keyword popularity from `docs/seo/research/aso-keyword-seed.md` (0-100). It is capped at 1,500 per fix, because several fixes cover whole clusters whose numbers overlap.
- **Gap** = how far the page is from right, 1-5 (5 = a hard-rule breach or a page that fails its query). The Opus check changed the gap on 7 of the first 20 and rejected 1.
- **Priority** = (capped demand + 25) x gap. The +25 floor stops a real error on a quiet page from scoring zero.
- **Tier A first.** Fixes with gap 4-5 on accuracy or trust (wrong output, banned claim, health content without a caveat) are listed before everything else, in priority order. They carry little search demand because getleft.app is new (13 clicks, 1,120 impressions in 16 months), but they are the ones that can mislead a reader.
- **Translation impact** says what the fix does to the 18-language site. Changing English text on a live page drops the translations of that text until redone. Live today: blog de, es, es-419; tools ja; landing pages (`pages`) de, ja, zh-Hant (the rest are scheduled); home, contact, support in all 18. Labels: "no translated text", "translated: <n> units" or "new page". A fix that adds more than about 10% new units to a page unpublishes its live locale copies until they are retranslated: those are marked **90% risk**.
- **Verified** = checked by the Opus helper against the HTML, the tool scripts, the app source and the catalogs. The remaining fixes are the Sonnet helpers' findings, spot-read but not independently verified.
- Full text of every fix, its evidence and the demand inputs: `data/ranked-fixes.json`.
