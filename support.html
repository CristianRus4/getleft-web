Support

Having trouble with Left? Common questions, troubleshooting, and how to get more help.

Common issues

Left doesn't appear in my widget list
  1. Open the Left app at least once after installing.
  2. Update to the latest iOS/iPadOS and the latest version of Left.
  3. Restart your device and try adding the widget again.
Why don't widgets refresh more often?

iOS manages widget refreshes to save battery. Apps request updates, but the system decides when they actually run. Opening Left refreshes widgets immediately; otherwise the system schedules updates within its own budget. Habit completions reload affected widgets right away.

How do I add a widget to my Home Screen?
  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen until apps jiggle.
  2. Tap + (top-left) and search for Left.
  3. Pick a style and size (small, medium, large).
  4. Tap Add Widget, then long-press and choose Edit Widget to configure it.
How do I add a Lock Screen or StandBy widget?
  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap Customize.
  2. Tap the widget area and choose Left.
  3. Select the widget type and finish customization.

StandBy widgets are added the same way from the StandBy customization flow.

What's in Left+? What's free?

Most of Left is free: habits, streaks, countdowns, the Planner, Left Time views, and most widgets. Left+ unlocks special dates and a custom lifespan. It's a one-time, pay-what-you-want purchase — three prices, same full app.

How do I restore my Left+ purchase?

Open Left → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used originally.

How is life expectancy calculated?

Left uses an estimate based on age, gender, country, and optional health and activity factors. You can override it with a custom lifespan in Settings (Left+).

Does it work offline? Is my data private?

Widgets work fully offline. Your habits, dates, and settings live on your device and sync via iCloud if enabled. Shared habits and joint countdowns sync through your Left account. See the privacy policy for details.

My habit isn't syncing to my iPad / other device
  1. Make sure both devices are signed into the same iCloud account.
  2. Make sure iCloud Drive is on for Left in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.
  3. Open Left on the device that's missing data — sync runs on launch and in the background.
  4. Wait a minute and pull-to-refresh on the Since list.
I can't see my friend in the Friends list

Both of you need to be signed into a Left account. Send a friend request by username from the Friends section, and have them accept it. Joint countdowns and Shared Since become available once you're friends.

Left Wallpaper isn't updating

Left Wallpaper updates through Apple Shortcuts. Open the Shortcuts app and confirm the Update Left Wallpaper automation is enabled. iOS may delay automations when the device is hot or in Low Power Mode.
