World Mood globe

World Mood is an interactive globe showing anonymous moods shared around the world during the last six hours.

On this page

  • Explore recent moods
  • Viewing and sharing
  • How location privacy works
  • Turn sharing off
  • Related guides

Explore recent moods

Open World Mood from the Mood tracker. Drag to rotate the globe, pinch to zoom, and select a glowing location to see the mood shared there. The globe refreshes as entries move beyond the six-hour window.

Viewing and sharing

Anyone with access to World Mood can explore the globe. Sharing your own mood is optional and requires Premium. Only moods you choose to share are added to the globe.

How location privacy works

Left never sends your precise location to World Mood. Your position is reduced to a broad one-degree location cell, and extra random jitter is added before the mood appears on the globe.

A shared entry does not include your name, account details, Apple Health history, or other mood history. People viewing the globe cannot identify who shared a mood.

Turn sharing off

You can disable World Mood sharing at any time in Mood settings. Left stops sharing future entries and removes your current shared pin when possible. Mood logging in Left and Apple Health continues privately.
