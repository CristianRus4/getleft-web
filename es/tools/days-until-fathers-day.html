When is Father's Day?

In the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, and many other countries, Father's Day is celebrated on the third Sunday of June every year. The date shifts between June 15th and June 21st depending on the year.

Australia and New Zealand observe Father's Day on the first Sunday of September instead. If you're in the Southern Hemisphere, use the countdown to any date tool to calculate yours.

Planning ahead

  • 3–4 weeks out — book a sporting event, golf tee time, or experience
  • 2 weeks out — order engraved gifts, gear, or a custom item
  • 1 week out — plan a BBQ, dinner, or day trip
  • Day before — pick up a card, confirm any plans
