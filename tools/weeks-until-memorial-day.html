Memorial Day, the last Monday of May

Memorial Day is a US federal holiday to honor and remember the men and women who died while serving in the US armed forces. Many Americans visit cemeteries and memorials, place flags on graves, and attend services and parades. At 3 p.m. local time, the National Moment of Remembrance asks everyone to pause for a minute of silence.

The long weekend is also seen as the unofficial start of summer: many pools and beaches open for the season, and it is one of the busiest travel weekends of the year.

How the date is set

Since 1971 Memorial Day has been observed on the last Monday of May; before that it was held on May 30. Because it is tied to a weekday, the date moves between May 25 and May 31. In 2027 it is May 31, in 2028 May 29, in 2029 May 28 and in 2030 May 27.

How Memorial Day is observed

By custom, the US flag flies at half-staff from sunrise until noon on Memorial Day, then is raised to full staff for the rest of the day. Volunteers place small flags on the graves of service members at national cemeteries, and many towns hold a parade or a wreath-laying ceremony. Red poppies, worn or placed at memorials, are a common symbol of remembrance.

For many families the weekend is also a time to be together, at a cookout or on a first trip of the season, while taking a moment to remember those who gave their lives.

Questions

How many weeks until Memorial Day?

The countdown at the top of this page shows the whole weeks left, with the exact weeks and days under it, in your time zone. Memorial Day 2027 is Monday, May 31, 2027.

When is Memorial Day 2028?

Memorial Day 2028 is Monday, May 29, 2028. It is always the last Monday of May, so it falls between May 25 and May 31.

What is the difference between Memorial Day and Veterans Day?

Memorial Day, in May, honors the military personnel who died while serving. Veterans Day, on November 11, honors everyone who has served in the US armed forces.

Why is Memorial Day called the start of summer?

It is the first long weekend of the warm season, when many pools, beaches and parks open. Astronomical summer starts later, at the June solstice around June 20 or 21; the days until summer page counts down to it.
