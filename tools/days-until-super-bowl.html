Super Bowl LXI: February 14, 2027

The Super Bowl is the championship game of the National Football League (NFL) and one of the most-watched television events in the United States. Super Bowl LXI, the 61st, is scheduled for Sunday, February 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, near Los Angeles. The stadium also hosted Super Bowl LVI in 2022. Super Bowls are numbered in Roman numerals from the first game, played in January 1967, so LXI is the 61st.

In 2027 the game falls on Valentine’s Day, so plans for dinner and plans for the game land on the same Sunday. The Valentine’s Day countdown counts to the same date.

How the date is set

The NFL sets the date and the host stadium: team owners vote on host cities, and the stadium is usually announced several years before the game. Since the regular season grew to 17 games in 2021, the Super Bowl has usually been played on the second Sunday of February; before that it was usually the first Sunday.

This page only counts down to games the NFL has confirmed. Once Super Bowl LXI has been played, it says the next date isn’t confirmed yet rather than guessing, until the next date is added here.

How the countdown works

The countdown runs to midnight at the start of February 14, 2027 in your device’s time zone. It counts to the date rather than the kickoff, because the exact kickoff time is set closer to the game; check the broadcast listings in the week of the game. Choose a unit under the counter to see the time left in weeks, days only, hours, minutes or seconds.

On game day the page shows the day instead of a counter. For a countdown to a kickoff time you choose yourself, use the date countdown calculator.

Super Bowl Sunday

For many Americans the game is only part of the day. Friends and families gather for watch parties, the halftime show draws viewers who don’t follow football, and the commercials are a talking point of their own. If you are hosting, a countdown on your phone is an easy way to keep the shopping, the food and the guest list on schedule.

Questions

When is the next Super Bowl?

Super Bowl LXI is on Sunday, February 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The countdown at the top of this page shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds left in your time zone.

What time does the Super Bowl start?

The kickoff time for Super Bowl LXI isn’t confirmed on this page, so the countdown runs to the start of the day. Recent Super Bowls have kicked off in the early evening, Eastern Time; check the broadcast listings in the week of the game.

Is the Super Bowl on Valentine’s Day in 2027?

Yes. February 14, 2027 is both Valentine’s Day and the date of Super Bowl LXI.

Why is the Super Bowl in mid-February now?

Since the NFL regular season grew to 17 games in 2021, the season ends a week later, and the Super Bowl has usually moved from the first to the second Sunday of February.
