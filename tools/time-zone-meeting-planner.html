Time zone meeting planner

Compare a meeting time across multiple time zones. Plan calls for New York, London, Los Angeles, Tokyo, Sydney, and more.

Plan across time zones

Choose a meeting time in your local time, then compare how it appears in other cities and time zones. This helps avoid calls that are too early or too late for someone else.

Good meeting windows

For global teams, look for overlap that is within normal waking or work hours for most participants. The best time is often a compromise, not the default calendar slot.

Make the meeting visible

Once the meeting time is set, add a countdown in Left so the call stays visible before it starts.
