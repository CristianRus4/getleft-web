How the countdown works

The calculator measures the difference between right now and your chosen date and time, then converts that gap into whichever unit you select. It updates every second, so you can watch the minutes tick away in real time.

When you pick a date without setting a specific time, the countdown runs to midnight at the start of that day. If you want to count down to an event at a specific hour - a flight departure, a meeting, a birthday party - tap the time field and set it precisely.

Choosing the right unit

The default "all units" view shows days, hours, minutes, and seconds together - the most complete picture. But sometimes one unit is more useful:

  • Months - useful for long-range planning (a holiday six months away).
  • Weeks - good for project milestones or pregnancy tracking.
  • Days only - the clearest single number for most events.
  • Hours - great for same-day events like a flight or concert.
  • Minutes / Seconds - useful for live events and launches.

A worked example

Say it is 12:00 on Tuesday, 10 November 2026, and you set the target to 00:00 on Friday, 25 December 2026. The gap is 44 days and 12 hours. Switch units and the same gap reads 1,068 hours, 6 weeks or 1 month.

Every single-unit view shows whole units only, rounded down. "6 weeks" means at least 6 full weeks are left, not exactly 6. Months use an average month of 30.4375 days (a 365.25-day year divided by 12), because real months run from 28 to 31 days. For long countdowns, days or weeks are the more exact figure.

Midnight or a set time?

The target is a moment, not a whole day. 00:00 on 25 December is the very start of that day, so the countdown reaches zero as Christmas Day begins. If your event is a 15:00 wedding on that date, set 15:00 and you get 15 more hours.

To count to the end of a day, for example a form due "by 31 March", set 23:59 on that date. Once the target has passed, the tool says so instead of counting into negative numbers.

Time zones and daylight saving

The date and time you enter are read in your device's time zone. If the event happens somewhere else, such as a launch at 09:00 in New York while you are in London, convert it to your own time first, or the countdown will be off by the difference between the two zones. The time zone meeting planner does the conversion.

The countdown measures real elapsed time. When the clocks change in between, a gap that looks like three days on the calendar can be an hour shorter or longer. In London, 12:00 on Friday, 26 March 2027 to 12:00 on Monday, 29 March 2027 is 2 days and 23 hours, because the clocks go forward on Sunday, 28 March.

Counting business days instead

This tool counts every day, weekends included. For a work deadline you may care only about working days. The business days calculator counts Monday to Friday between two dates, and the business days from a date calculator finds the date a number of working days ahead.

Questions

How many days until a date?

Set the target date and time above and choose Days only. The number is the whole days left from this moment in your time zone, rounded down.

Does the countdown include today?

It counts from this moment, so what is left of today is included as hours, not as a full day. With a target of 00:00, the target day itself is not included, because the countdown ends as that day begins.

Should I count to midnight or to a set time?

Use 00:00 when the whole day matters, such as a holiday or a birthday. Use the start time for a flight, an exam or a wedding, so the countdown ends when the event begins.

Does it handle time zones and daylight saving?

It uses your device's time zone and counts real elapsed time, so a gap that crosses a clock change is one hour shorter or longer than the calendar suggests. For an event in another time zone, convert its time to yours first.

Can it count business days only?

No, it counts every day, weekends included. Use the business days calculator to count Monday to Friday only.

Can I keep this countdown on my iPhone?

Yes. Add the date to Ahead in Left, then show it in an Ahead widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
