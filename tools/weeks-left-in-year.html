How the weeks left are counted

The tool counts the days from tomorrow to December 31 in your time zone and divides them by 7. On November 10, 2026, for example, 51 days are left: 7 full weeks and 2 days, or 7.3 weeks.

Working days are Monday to Friday. Public holidays are not taken off, because they differ by country; the business days calculator counts working days between any two dates. A weekend counts as left while at least one of its two days is still to come.

The grid shows the weeks of the year by their week number: the weeks gone are faded, this week is highlighted and the ones still ahead stay solid. It is the same picture Left’s Year widget draws when you show the year in weeks.

Week numbers, and why some years have 53

The tool uses ISO 8601 week numbers, the standard in Europe and much of Asia. Weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week that contains the first Thursday of January. That makes most years 52 weeks long, but a year that starts on a Thursday, or a leap year that starts on a Wednesday, has 53.

In the United States and some other countries, calendars often start weeks on Sunday and count the week of January 1 as week 1, so their week number can differ by one. The ISO week number tool finds the number for any date.

Questions

How many weeks are left in the year?

Divide the days left until December 31 by 7. On November 10, 2026, 51 days are left, which is 7.3 weeks. The tool above shows the figure for today.

What week of the year is it?

The tool shows this week’s ISO 8601 number, where weeks start on Monday and week 1 contains the first Thursday of January. Calendars that start weeks on Sunday can show a number one higher or lower.

Does a year have 52 or 53 weeks?

A calendar year is 52 weeks and 1 day, or 52 weeks and 2 days in a leap year. In ISO week numbering most years have 52 weeks and some have 53, such as 2026, 2032 and 2037.

How many working days are left in the year?

The tool counts the Mondays to Fridays from tomorrow to December 31. Public holidays are not removed, so take off the ones that apply where you work.

Can I see the weeks left in the year on my iPhone?

Yes. Add Left’s free Year widget and set Show time as to weeks. It shows one symbol per week and the weeks left, on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
