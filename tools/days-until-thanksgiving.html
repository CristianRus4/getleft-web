How Thanksgiving’s date is worked out

US Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November, so it always lands between November 22 and November 28. To find it, take the first Thursday of November and add 21 days. That is exactly what the counter above does each year, and it counts to the start of Thanksgiving Day, 00:00 in your local time zone. Once the day begins, it moves on to next year’s Thanksgiving.

The rule is set by law. Abraham Lincoln proclaimed a national Thanksgiving on the last Thursday of November in 1863. In 1941 Congress fixed it to the fourth Thursday, which took effect in 1942. Canadian Thanksgiving is different: it falls on the second Monday of October. This page counts to the US date.

Planning the meal

  • Four weeks out: confirm who is coming, book travel, and order a fresh turkey if you want one.
  • Two weeks out: plan the menu and make a shopping list.
  • One week out: buy pantry staples, drinks and anything that keeps.
  • Several days out: start thawing a frozen turkey in the fridge. The USDA advises allowing about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds, so a 16-pound bird needs about four days.
  • The day before: bake pies, prep sides and set the table.

Black Friday is the next day. If you are planning for both, track the shopping deadline separately with the countdown to any date tool.

Questions

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

US Thanksgiving 2026 is on Thursday, November 26. In 2027 it is on Thursday, November 25.

Why does Thanksgiving’s date change every year?

It is defined as the fourth Thursday of November rather than a fixed date, so it moves between November 22 and November 28 depending on which weekday November 1 falls on.

Is Canadian Thanksgiving on the same day?

No. Canadian Thanksgiving is the second Monday of October, about six weeks earlier. In 2026 it is on October 12.

How many days are there between Thanksgiving and Christmas?

Between 27 and 33 days. It is 27 days when Thanksgiving falls on November 28 and 33 days when it falls on November 22. In 2026 the gap is 29 days.
