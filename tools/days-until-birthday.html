Days until your birthday

Enter your birthdate and get a live countdown to your next birthday — down to the second.

How the birthday countdown works

Enter your date of birth and the tool calculates the next anniversary of that date in the current or next calendar year. If today is your birthday, the countdown resets to 365 (or 366) days for next year automatically.

The countdown counts to midnight at the start of your birthday — so when it hits zero, it's officially your birthday in your local time zone.

Track someone else's birthday too

This tool works for any birthdate — not just yours. Enter a friend or family member's birthday to see how long until their next celebration. In Left, you can add multiple birthday countdowns and see them all side by side on your Home Screen.

Make it a tradition

Pin a birthday countdown widget to your Lock Screen and you'll naturally think about upcoming celebrations weeks in advance — giving you time to plan, book, and make it special rather than scrambling the day before.
