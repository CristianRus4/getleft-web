How this Valentine’s Day countdown works

Valentine’s Day is always February 14. The counter above runs to 00:00 on February 14 using your device’s clock and time zone, then moves on to next year’s date as soon as the day begins. What changes from year to year is the day of the week, and that is what makes planning harder or easier.

Why the weekday shifts

A normal year has 365 days, which is 52 weeks and one day, so a fixed date moves one weekday later each year. When a February 29 falls in between, it moves two. February 14 comes before the leap day, so the jump happens the year after a leap year: Valentine’s Day 2028 is a Monday, but 2029 skips Tuesday and lands on a Wednesday.

When it falls on a Friday or a weekend, more people go out on the day itself, so book earlier. On a weeknight, many couples celebrate the weekend before or after instead.

Planning timeline

  • Four or more weeks out: book a restaurant or trip, and order anything custom or personalized.
  • Two to three weeks out: order flowers for delivery and book experiences.
  • One week out: pick up cards, chocolates and local gifts.
  • The day before: confirm your reservation and wrap gifts.

Questions

When is Valentine’s Day 2027?

Valentine’s Day 2027 is on Sunday, February 14. In 2028 it falls on a Monday.

Is Valentine’s Day a public holiday?

No, not in the US or the UK. Shops, offices and schools keep their normal hours, which is why many people celebrate on the nearest weekend.

Why is Valentine’s Day on a different weekday each year?

A year is 52 weeks plus one day, so February 14 moves one weekday later each year, or two when a leap day falls in between.

Can I get a reminder before Valentine’s Day?

Yes. In Left, add February 14 as an Ahead date that repeats yearly and turn on a reminder, for example 2 weeks before, to leave time for bookings and deliveries.
