What 120 days is used for

A hundred and twenty days is about four months, or a third of a year. It is long enough for a real project: a training plan for a first marathon, a season of study before an exam, or saving towards a trip. Some notice periods, payment terms and visa rules also use 120 days. It is 17 weeks and 1 day.

Four calendar months add up to 120 to 123 days, so a 120-day period ends a few days before the same date four months on. Count days when a rule says 120 days, and months when it says four months.

How the date is counted

Today is day 0, so 1 day from today is tomorrow. The tool adds the days to today’s date in your time zone and shows the weekday of the result. Weeks are 7 days each. Months follow the calendar and stop at the end of a shorter month: one month after January 31 is the last day of February.

Choose business days to skip Saturdays and Sundays. Public holidays are not skipped, because they differ by country; the business days from a date tool counts from any start date. Switch to before today to count backwards, or use what was the date for dates in the past.

Questions

What is the date 120 days from today?

The tool above shows it, with the weekday, for your time zone. Today counts as day 0, so the date is 120 days after today.

How many months is 120 days?

About four. Four calendar months add up to 120 to 123 days, so 120 days usually ends up to three days before the same date four months on.

How many weeks is 120 days?

120 days is 17 weeks and 1 day, or about 17.1 weeks.

How many business days are in the next 120 days?

Usually 85 or 86, depending on the weekday you start from. The Weekdays figure above counts them for today, without public holidays.

Can I keep a countdown to the date on my iPhone?

Yes. Add the date in Left as an Ahead date and put the Ahead widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. It counts down every day.
