When is Chinese New Year?

Chinese New Year, also called Lunar New Year or the Spring Festival, is the first day of the first month of the Chinese lunisolar calendar. In 2027 it falls on Saturday, February 6, 2027 and begins the Year of the Goat.

The celebrations start the evening before, on New Year’s Eve, when families gather for the reunion dinner. The festival then runs for 15 days, to the Lantern Festival on the first full moon. It is celebrated across East and Southeast Asia and by communities around the world, and in Korea and Vietnam the same new year is known as Seollal and Tết.

How the date is set

Each month of the Chinese calendar begins on the day of a new moon, and the new year usually starts on the second new moon after the winter solstice. In the Gregorian calendar that always lands between January 21 and February 20, so the date moves from year to year.

The calendar’s days are reckoned at China’s time, so a new moon close to midnight can put the new year on a different day in calendars made for another time zone. That is why the dates on this page come from a published calendar rather than a formula.

The zodiac animals

Each year belongs to one of twelve animals, in a fixed cycle: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. 2027 is the Year of the Goat, also called the Sheep or Ram, followed by the Monkey in 2028 and the Rooster in 2029.

Because the zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on January 1, someone born in January 2027 belongs to the Year of the Horse, the animal of the year that began in February 2026.

Questions

When is Chinese New Year 2027?

Chinese New Year 2027 is on Saturday, February 6, 2027, the start of the Year of the Goat. New Year’s Eve, with the reunion dinner, is Friday, February 5, 2027, and the Lantern Festival is Saturday, February 20, 2027.

Why does the date of Chinese New Year change every year?

The Chinese calendar is lunisolar: its months start at the new moon, and a leap month is added about every three years to keep it in step with the seasons. Lunar New Year therefore falls on a different Gregorian date each year, always between January 21 and February 20.

What is the zodiac animal for 2027?

The Goat, also called the Sheep or Ram. Its year runs from February 6, 2027 to January 25, 2028. Then come the Monkey in 2028, the Rooster in 2029, the Dog in 2030, the Pig in 2031 and the Rat in 2032.

How long does Chinese New Year last?

The festival lasts 15 days, from New Year’s Day to the Lantern Festival, with the reunion dinner on the evening before. Public holidays are shorter and vary by country, from a few days to about a week.
