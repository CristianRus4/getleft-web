A private day counter

This sobriety calculator counts the days since your sobriety date and shows the next common milestone. It is a simple date calculator, not medical or recovery advice.

Why milestones help

Milestones like 7, 30, 90, 180, and 365 days make long-term progress easier to see. The next milestone gives the streak a near-term target.

Keep progress visible

Many people use a private widget or streak tracker for recovery milestones. Left can show the day count without needing to open a separate app.
