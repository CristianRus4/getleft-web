Labor Day, the first Monday of September

Labor Day is a US federal holiday that honors American workers and the labor movement. It falls on the first Monday of September, so it always comes with a three-day weekend, and for many people it marks the unofficial end of summer: the last long weekend before school and work routines take over again.

Canada marks Labour Day on the same Monday. Most other countries mark Labour Day, often called May Day or International Workers’ Day, on May 1 instead.

How the date is set

Labor Day became a federal holiday in 1894, set on the first Monday of September. Because it is tied to a weekday rather than a date, it moves around: it can fall on any day from September 1 to September 7. In 2027 it is September 6, in 2028 September 4 and in 2030 September 2.

The long weekend runs from the Saturday before to Monday itself. In years when Labor Day is September 1 or 2, that Saturday is still in August.

Labour Day around the world

The United States and Canada are the exceptions in holding Labor Day in September. In much of Europe, Latin America, Africa and Asia, workers are honored on May 1, a public holiday in many of those countries. A few places choose other dates: New Zealand marks Labour Day on the fourth Monday of October, and Australia’s states each set their own day.

Labor Day also arrives a few weeks before the autumn equinox, which is the astronomical end of summer in the Northern Hemisphere.

Questions

When is Labor Day 2027?

Labor Day 2027 is Monday, September 6, 2027. It is always the first Monday of September, so in 2028 it is Monday, September 4.

Is Labour Day in Canada on the same day?

Yes. Canada’s Labour Day is also the first Monday of September, so the United States and Canada mark it on the same day every year.

Why do other countries celebrate Labour Day on May 1?

May 1 is International Workers’ Day, a public holiday in many countries in Europe, Latin America, Africa and Asia. The United States and Canada hold their Labor Day in September instead.

Is Labor Day the end of summer?

Unofficially, yes: it is the last long weekend of the summer for many Americans. Astronomical summer in the Northern Hemisphere ends later, at the September equinox around September 22 or 23.
