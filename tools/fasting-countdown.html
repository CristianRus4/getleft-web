How to use this countdown

Enter the time your fast is scheduled to end. If you're doing a 16:8 fast that started at 8 PM last night, your fast ends at 12 PM today - enter that. The countdown updates live, to the second.

If you don't know your end time, use the end of fasting calculator to find it from your start time and duration, then come back here.

A worked example

Say you finished eating at 8:00 PM on Tuesday, November 10, 2026 and you follow 16:8. Your fast ends 16 hours later, at 12:00 PM on Wednesday, November 11, so enter that date and time. At 9:20 AM on Wednesday you have been fasting for 13 hours 20 minutes, and the countdown shows 2 hours 40 minutes left.

From the same 8:00 PM start, other common fasts end at these times:

  • 16:8 - 16 hours of fasting and an 8-hour eating window: ends at 12:00 PM the next day
  • 18:6 - 18 hours fasting, 6 hours eating: ends at 2:00 PM
  • 20:4 - 20 hours fasting, 4 hours eating: ends at 4:00 PM
  • OMAD (one meal a day) - usually about 23 hours of fasting: ends around 7:00 PM
  • 24-hour fast - from one meal to the same meal the next day: ends at 8:00 PM

Local time, midnight and clock changes

The countdown uses your device's clock and time zone. Most fasts cross midnight, which is why the end time includes a date: if you finish eating tonight, pick tomorrow's date for the end.

The countdown always counts real time to the clock time you entered. That matters on the two nights a year when the clocks change. In the US, clocks go forward on Sunday, March 14, 2027, so a fast from 8:00 PM on the Saturday to 12:00 PM on the Sunday lasts only 15 hours; for a full 16, enter 1:00 PM. When the clocks go back in the autumn, the same fast lasts 17 hours. If you travel across time zones during a fast, re-enter the end time in your new local time.

What counts as breaking a fast differs between approaches, and this page does not decide it. The countdown only measures time; follow the plan you have chosen.

Staying the course during a fast

Fasting becomes easier with concrete time awareness. Hunger comes in waves - knowing that you're 83% through your fast and have only 2 hours 40 minutes left is far more manageable than vaguely knowing "a few more hours."

  • Drink water - thirst and hunger feel similar; staying hydrated reduces false hunger signals
  • Stay busy - boredom is one of the biggest fasting challenges, not actual hunger
  • Black coffee or tea - both are generally considered fast-compatible and suppress appetite
  • Check the countdown, not the fridge - knowing the exact time remaining is more useful than willpower alone

Is fasting right for you?

Fasting is not suitable for everyone. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have diabetes or another medical condition, have had an eating disorder, or take regular medication, talk to a doctor before you fast. Mayo Clinic has an overview of intermittent fasting. This calculator only works out times; it does not give medical advice.

Questions

How long is left in my 16:8 fast?

Enter the time your fast ends, 16 hours after your last meal, and the counter shows the hours, minutes and seconds left. If you finished eating at 8:00 PM, a 16-hour fast ends at 12:00 PM the next day.

What do 16:8, 18:6 and OMAD mean?

The first number is the hours of fasting and the second is the eating window in a 24-hour day, so 16:8 is 16 hours fasting and 8 hours eating. OMAD means one meal a day, which usually works out at about 23 hours of fasting.

Does the countdown work across midnight?

Yes. The end time includes the date, so a fast that starts in the evening and ends the next day counts down correctly. It uses your device's local time.

What happens if the clocks change during my fast?

The countdown counts real time to the clock time you entered. On the night the clocks go forward, a fast from 8:00 PM to 12:00 PM lasts 15 hours; on the night they go back, it lasts 17.

Does black coffee break a fast?

Approaches differ, and this tool does not decide what breaks a fast. It only counts time, so follow the plan you have chosen or your doctor's advice.

Can I see my fast on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Add the end time as an Ahead date in Left and show it in a Lock Screen widget. Left's Fasting timer, part of Left Premium, also shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch.
