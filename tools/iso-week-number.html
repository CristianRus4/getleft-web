What is an ISO week number?

ISO 8601 defines a standard way to number weeks in a year. Under this standard:

  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday
  • Week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of the year (equivalently, the week with January 4th)
  • A year has either 52 or 53 ISO weeks
  • The ISO year can differ from the calendar year - days at the very start or end of January may belong to the previous or next ISO year

Why does week 1 sometimes start in December?

If January 1 falls on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday, it belongs to the last week of the previous ISO year, and week 1 starts on the first Monday after it (January 2 to 4). If January 1 falls on a Monday to Thursday, week 1 is the week that contains it, which can start in late December.

Who uses ISO week numbers?

ISO week numbers are common in European business, manufacturing, and logistics. Sprint planning in software teams often references week numbers. Financial reporting, academic calendars, and broadcast schedules in many countries are structured around ISO weeks.

Weeks in a year

Most years have 52 ISO weeks. A year has 53 weeks when January 1st is a Thursday, or (in a leap year) when it's a Wednesday or Thursday. Upcoming 53-week years include 2032 and 2037.

2026 is itself a 53-week year, because January 1, 2026 was a Thursday. Recent and upcoming 53-week years are 2015, 2020, 2026, 2032, 2037 and 2043. Over the full 400-year cycle of the Gregorian calendar, 71 years have 53 weeks, so it happens roughly every five or six years.

How the week number is worked out

The calculator finds the Monday-to-Sunday week your date belongs to and looks at the Thursday of that week. The year that Thursday falls in is the ISO year. The week number is how many Thursdays that year has had so far, including this one.

A worked example: Tuesday, November 10, 2026. Its week runs from Monday, November 9 to Sunday, November 15. The Thursday of that week is November 12, which is day 316 of 2026. Days 1 to 7 hold the first Thursday, days 8 to 14 the second, and so on, so day 316 holds the 46th Thursday. The date is in week 46 of 2026, written 2026-W46-2 in ISO 8601 notation, where the final 2 means Tuesday (Monday is 1, Sunday is 7).

Weeks that cross New Year

Because a week always runs Monday to Sunday, the first and last weeks of a year often spill into the neighbouring year. Two real examples:

  • Tuesday, December 31, 2024 is in week 1 of 2025. That week ran from Monday, December 30, 2024 to Sunday, January 5, 2025, and its Thursday was January 2, 2025.
  • Friday, January 1, 2027 to Sunday, January 3, 2027 are in week 53 of 2026. Week 1 of 2027 starts on Monday, January 4, 2027.

This is why the calculator shows the ISO year next to the week number. For dates in the last days of December or the first days of January, check both.

ISO weeks vs US week numbers

Many US calendars and apps use a different system: weeks start on Sunday, and week 1 is whichever week contains January 1, even if it is only one day long. Under that system a year can touch 54 weeks; 2028 does, because it is a leap year that starts on a Saturday and ends on a Sunday.

The two systems often disagree by one week. Sunday, November 15, 2026 is the last day of ISO week 46 but the first day of week 47 on a Sunday-start calendar. Around New Year they can disagree on the year too: January 1, 2027 is week 1 of 2027 in the US system and week 53 of 2026 in ISO. When you share a week number with people in other countries, say which system you mean, or give the dates.

In Excel and Google Sheets, =ISOWEEKNUM(A1) returns the ISO week. Plain =WEEKNUM(A1) starts weeks on Sunday, so it can give a different number.

Time zones

The calculator uses the date on your device. Late on a Sunday in one time zone it may already be Monday in another, so two people can be in different weeks for a few hours.

Questions

What week number is it today?

The calculator opens on today's date and shows its ISO week number, ISO year and the Monday-to-Sunday dates of the week. For example, Tuesday, November 10, 2026 is in week 46 of 2026.

Is it 52 or 53 weeks this year?

2026 has 53 ISO weeks, because January 1, 2026 was a Thursday. 2027 has 52, and the next 53-week years are 2032 and 2037.

How is week 1 defined?

Week 1 is the Monday-to-Sunday week that contains the year's first Thursday. That is always the same week as the one containing January 4.

Why is December 31 in week 1?

If December 31 falls on a Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday, its week contains the next year's first Thursday, so it counts as week 1 of the next year. December 31, 2024 is in week 1 of 2025.

Are US week numbers the same as ISO week numbers?

Not always. US calendars usually start weeks on Sunday and count the week containing January 1 as week 1, so the two systems can differ by a week, and in early January by a year.

How do I get the ISO week number in Excel or Google Sheets?

Use =ISOWEEKNUM(A1) in either app. =WEEKNUM(A1) without a second argument starts weeks on Sunday, so it can give a different number.
