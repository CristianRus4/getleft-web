How to set it as your wallpaper

  1. Pick a style, a colour and your iPhone model, then tap Save image. On an iPhone, choose Save Image in the share sheet to put it in Photos.
  2. Open Settings, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper, and choose Photos.
  3. Pick the image, check that the clock sits above the grid, tap Add and choose Set as Wallpaper Pair, or customise the Home Screen separately.

The image is made at your iPhone’s exact screen size, so iOS does not need to zoom or crop it. The grid starts below the clock and stops above the torch and camera buttons. Everything is drawn in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

What the wallpaper shows

Dots draws one dot per day of the year, 365 or 366, with the days that have gone faded and today in your colour. Months draws a bar for each month, filled with the days still ahead. Ring draws the part of the year that is left as a ring around the year. With text on, the wallpaper also shows the days left, or the percentage left for a year that has not started yet.

Why a saved image goes out of date

A wallpaper is a picture, and iOS does not let a website or an app change it on its own. An image saved today still shows today tomorrow. You can come back and make a new one every few days, or let your iPhone do it.

Left Wallpaper draws a fresh image every day: the year as a grid with the days left, your life in weeks, this month, or a countdown to a date. A Shortcuts automation sets it as your Lock Screen each night, so it is always current. It is free, and the setup takes a few minutes once. Questions

How do I set a year progress wallpaper on my iPhone?

Save the image above, then open Settings, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper, choose Photos and pick the image. Check that the clock sits above the grid and tap Add.

Why does my wallpaper not update?

A wallpaper is a fixed image, and iOS does not let websites or apps change it by themselves. A saved image keeps showing the day you made it until you replace it.

Is there a wallpaper that updates automatically?

Yes. Left Wallpaper draws a new image every day and a Shortcuts automation sets it as your Lock Screen each night. It is free in Left.

What size should a wallpaper be for my iPhone?

The same size as the screen in pixels, for example 1206 × 2622 for an iPhone 17 or 1320 × 2868 for an iPhone 17 Pro Max. Pick your model above and the image is made at that size.

Is anything uploaded when I make the wallpaper?

No. The image is drawn in your browser and saved straight to your device.
