Common past date lookups

Some of the most frequent uses for this calculator:

  • 30 days ago - common return window, subscription trial period
  • 60 days ago - extended return policies, billing cycle lookups
  • 90 days ago - quarterly review periods, tax filing references
  • 6 months ago - credit report lookups, half-year reviews
  • 1 year ago - anniversary checks, annual contract renewals

How the calculation works

The calculator subtracts the specified duration from today's date. For days and weeks, this is an exact number of calendar days. For months, the calculation uses calendar months - so "3 months ago" from March 31st gives December 31st, which is 90 days back in most years and 91 when February has 29 days.

Years follow the same calendar-month logic, which handles leap years correctly.

Worked examples: 30, 60 and 90 days ago

The answer depends on today's date, so the tool always starts from today. To count back by hand, go back through the days of the current month first, then through each earlier month in turn. From November 10, 2026:

  • 30 days ago - 10 days back reaches October 31, and 20 more reach October 11, 2026.
  • 60 days ago - October has 31 days, so 41 days back is September 30; 19 more reach September 11, 2026.
  • 90 days ago - September has 30 days, so 71 days back is August 31; 19 more reach August 12, 2026.

Tomorrow, each answer moves forward by one day. Today counts as day 0, so 1 day ago is yesterday and 7 days ago is the same weekday last week.

Weeks ago or months ago?

Weeks are exact: 4 weeks ago is always 28 days ago, and on November 10, 2026 that is October 13. One month ago means the same day number in the previous month, October 10, which is 31 days back. The two only match when the month in between is a 28-day February.

Months also stop at the end of a shorter month: 1 month before March 31, 2027 is February 28, 2027, the last day of February.

Questions

What was the date 30 days ago?

It depends on today. Count back through the days of this month, then into last month: from November 10, 2026, 30 days ago was October 11, 2026.

What was the date 90 days ago?

From November 10, 2026, it was August 12, 2026. Tap 90 days ago above to get the answer for today.

Is 4 weeks ago the same as 1 month ago?

Usually not. 4 weeks is always 28 days, while a month back is 28 to 31 days, depending on the month.

Does it count today?

No. Today is day 0, so 1 day ago is yesterday and 7 days ago is the same weekday last week.

What is 1 month before March 31?

February 28, or February 29 in a leap year. When the earlier month is shorter, the tool uses its last day.
