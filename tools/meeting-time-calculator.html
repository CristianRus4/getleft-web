Meeting time calculator

Calculate meeting duration, elapsed time, and time remaining from a start and end time. Free meeting-time calculator for back-to-back calls.

How the meeting time calculator works

Enter when the meeting starts and ends. The tool shows three numbers in minutes: the meeting length, how many minutes have passed since it started, and how many remain. Before the meeting starts, the elapsed time is 0 and the minutes left equal the full length. After it ends, minutes left show 0. The numbers refresh every minute. If the end is before the start, the length shows 0.

Keeping a meeting to time

  • Start the agenda with the clock. Share the end time so everyone sees the limit.
  • Check at the halfway mark. If half the minutes are gone and half the agenda is left, you are on track.
  • Protect the last five minutes for decisions and next steps.

To count hours between two times, use hours between two dates, or time between two times for clock times. If you are scheduling across time zones, use the time zone meeting planner.

Questions

How long is my meeting?

Enter the start and end times above. The first number shows the length in minutes.

How many minutes are left in my meeting?

The third number shows the minutes remaining and updates every minute while the meeting is on.

What if the meeting crosses midnight?

Enter the full end date and time, not just the clock time, so the tool can measure across the date change.

Does it use my time zone?

Yes, the time zone of your device.
