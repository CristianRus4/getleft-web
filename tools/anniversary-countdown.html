Anniversary countdown

Count down to your next anniversary from any original date. See days remaining, years completed, and the exact next anniversary date.

Find the next anniversary

Enter the original date and the calculator finds the next yearly anniversary from today. It also shows which anniversary year is coming up.

Useful anniversary types

Use it for weddings, relationships, sobriety dates, launches, work anniversaries, birthdays, memorials, and personal milestones.

Since and ahead together

Anniversaries combine elapsed time and future countdowns. Left supports both patterns: Since for how long it has been, Ahead for the next anniversary.
