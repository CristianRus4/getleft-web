Time until the weekend

Live countdown to Saturday morning. See exactly how much work week is left - to the second.

When does the weekend start?

This countdown treats Saturday at midnight (00:00 local time) as the start of the weekend. If today is already Saturday or Sunday, the timer shows zero and a message confirming it's already the weekend.

The countdown resets automatically each week - every Monday at midnight it starts a fresh five-day countdown to the next Saturday.

The psychology of countdowns

Seeing a concrete number - "39 hours left" - is more motivating than a vague sense of "just a few more days." Countdowns break time into manageable chunks. A 40-hour work week looks very different when you can watch the hours tick down.

Make every day count

Rather than just counting down to the weekend, use Left to also track what you want to accomplish before it arrives. A deadline widget alongside your weekend countdown gives you both the motivating goal and the urgent constraint - a powerful combination for getting things done.
