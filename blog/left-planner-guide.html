Left Planner: Your Entire Day in One View

May 2026 · 5 min read

The hardest part of a productive day isn't doing the things. It's deciding which thing is next. The Planner in Left tries to make that decision boring — one timeline, everything you need, in order.

What the Planner aggregates

  • Your local Left habits (Since)
  • Your local Left countdowns (Ahead)
  • Apple Calendar events (read-only)
  • Apple Reminders due today (read-only)
  • HealthKit summaries (steps, sleep, etc.)
  • WeatherKit summary for your current location

All in one scrollable timeline that the Planner owns. The Planner widget shows the next few items right on the Home Screen.

Permissions

The Planner asks for the permissions it needs only when you tap a section. Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders are visible by default and show a "tap to allow access" card if you haven't granted EventKit permission. Once you grant it, the Planner seeds the selected calendars/lists for you.

Setup

  1. Open Left → Planner.
  2. Tap the access cards for Calendar, Reminders, HealthKit, and Location/Weather. Approve each.
  3. Add the Planner widget to your Home Screen (search Left → PlannerWidget).

Tips

  • Use Apple Reminders for to-dos and Apple Calendar for time-bound events — the Planner is much better at displaying than capturing.
  • If a calendar is too noisy (work meetings on a personal phone), hide it in iOS Calendar settings — the Planner respects that selection.
  • HealthKit summaries fall back gracefully if your watch is dead.
