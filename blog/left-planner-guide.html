Left Planner: Your Day in One View

May 2026 · 1 min read

Planner brings the moving parts of your day into one view: Apple Reminders, calendar events, habits, streaks, weather, sleep, workouts, and more. It is built to make the day visible, not to become another task system.

What Planner brings together

  • Apple Reminders due today (read-only)
  • Apple Calendar events (read-only)
  • Habits and streaks from Left
  • Ahead dates and countdowns
  • Weather for the day
  • Sleep, workouts, and Health summaries
  • A weekly agenda forecast

The Planner widget shows what is next on your Home Screen, so the day stays visible without opening another calendar or task app.

Permissions

The Planner asks for the permissions it needs only when you tap a section. Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders are visible by default and show a "tap to allow access" card if you haven't granted EventKit permission. Once you grant it, the Planner seeds the selected calendars/lists for you.

Setup

  1. Open Left → Planner.
  2. Tap the access cards for Calendar, Reminders, HealthKit, and Location/Weather. Approve each.
  3. Add the Planner widget to your Home Screen.

Tips

  • Use Apple Reminders for to-dos and Apple Calendar for time-bound events. Planner keeps them next to your habits, streaks, and dates.
  • If a calendar is too noisy (work meetings on a personal phone), hide it in iOS Calendar settings - the Planner respects that selection.
  • HealthKit summaries fall back gracefully if your watch is dead.
