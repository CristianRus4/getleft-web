Best Countdown Widgets for iPhone Home Screen (2026)

May 2026 · 1 min read

A countdown widget is one of the simplest, most useful things you can put on a Home Screen. The trip you keep checking, the deadline you keep pretending isn't coming, the date you're excited about — turning it into a number you see ten times a day changes how you plan. Here's how to think about countdown widgets, and how Left's Ahead feature handles them.

What to look for in a countdown widget

  • All the surfaces. Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, iPad — not just one.
  • Custom styles. A circle, a bar, a number, an analog ring — the same date in the visual that fits your screen.
  • Live Activities. When the date gets close, the countdown should jump to the Dynamic Island or Lock Screen.
  • Sharing. Some dates only make sense if other people see them too.
  • Calendar import. Pulling from Apple Calendar so you don't re-type every event.

How Left does countdowns: Ahead

In Left, every countdown is an Ahead date. You add a title and a date, pick a color and a symbol, and it shows up as a widget on whatever surface you've added it to. The same Ahead date can be a small Home Screen widget, a large Lock Screen ring, and a Live Activity that takes over when the date is hours away.

Joint Ahead — countdown with a friend

Some dates are better shared. Add a friend by username, invite them to the Ahead date, and the countdown stays synced for both of you. It works for trips, anniversaries, deadlines, holidays, or anything you want to look forward to together.
