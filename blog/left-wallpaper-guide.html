The Left Wallpaper: A Lock Screen That Updates Itself

May 2026 · 5 min read

iOS wallpapers are static. Widgets aren't. Left Wallpaper sits in between — it generates a wallpaper image with your live time data, then uses an Apple Shortcut to update itself.

What it can show

  • Year progress
  • Life in weeks (a dot grid)
  • A countdown to a date you choose
  • A blend of a photo and a time view

How it actually works

Inside the app, Left's WallpaperGenerator writes a PNG into the App Group Wallpapers/ directory. An App Shortcut (UpdateDynamicWallpaperIntent) regenerates it on demand. You wire that Shortcut into an Apple Shortcuts Automation — usually "every hour" or "when I unlock my iPhone" — so the wallpaper stays current without you doing anything.

Setup, step by step

  1. Open Left → Settings → Left Wallpaper.
  2. Pick the view (Year, Life, Ahead, Day) and color.
  3. Tap Save as Wallpaper to write the first version into Photos.
  4. Open Shortcuts → Automation → + → pick a trigger (Time of Day, App Opened, etc).
  5. Add the Update Left Wallpaper action. Save.
  6. On your Lock Screen: Customize → choose the new wallpaper photo.

Tips

  • iOS may delay automations when the phone is hot or in Low Power Mode — that's the OS, not Left.
  • Pair it with a small Lock Screen widget for redundancy.
  • Pre-generation runs on app launch so the wallpaper's ready when you switch to it.
