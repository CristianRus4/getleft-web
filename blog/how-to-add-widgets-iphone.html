How to Add Widgets on iPhone, iPad, Lock Screen, and StandBy

May 2026 · 4 min read

iOS has four places you can put a widget: Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, and iPad. The steps are similar but not identical. Here's the full path for each, using Left as the example.

Home Screen (iPhone)

  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap + in the top-left corner.
  3. Search for Left.
  4. Swipe between widget styles and sizes — pick the one you want.
  5. Tap Add Widget, then tap an empty area to stop jiggle mode.
  6. Long-press the new widget and choose Edit Widget to pick what it shows (which habit, which Ahead date, what color, etc.).

Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Customize at the bottom → choose Lock Screen.
  3. Tap a widget area (above or below the clock).
  4. Pick Left from the list of apps.
  5. Choose the widget you want.

Lock Screen widgets are smaller and monochrome by design. Left's Lock Screen variants are tuned for that — bars, rings, and short text.

StandBy

StandBy mode kicks in when you charge your iPhone on its side. To add Left widgets:

  1. Put your iPhone on a charger horizontally so StandBy activates.
  2. Touch and hold one of the widget panels.
  3. Tap +, search for Left, and add the widget.

iPad

Identical to iPhone Home Screen — touch and hold, tap +, search, add. The widgets render at their larger sizes by default.

Editing or removing

Long-press any widget. You'll see Edit Widget (re-configure it) and Remove Widget (delete it from the screen).

Troubleshooting

If Left doesn't appear in the widget list, open the app once, update iOS and Left to the latest version, and restart the device. More tips on the support page.
