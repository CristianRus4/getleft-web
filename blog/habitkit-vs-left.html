HabitKit vs Left — A Fair Comparison

May 2026 · 1 min read

HabitKit and Left are both well-loved iPhone habit trackers. They aim at different things. If you're choosing between them, here's a clean side-by-side from someone who's used both.

Quick verdict

  • Pick HabitKit if you want one beautifully designed habit-tracking app focused on streak grids and visual consistency.
  • Pick Left if you want habits alongside countdowns, year progress, the Planner, and shared features — all from the same library of widgets.

What HabitKit does well

HabitKit is laser-focused on the GitHub-contributions-style grid. Each habit is a colored cell map, each completion fills a cell. The widgets are gorgeous and the app does this one thing very well.

What Left does that HabitKit doesn't

  • Time-left views. Year progress, life in weeks, day countdown — built in as their own feature (Left Time), with widgets.
  • Countdowns. Ahead dates with Live Activities and Joint Ahead (shared countdowns with friends).
  • The Planner. A timeline that aggregates your habits, countdowns, Apple Calendar, Reminders, HealthKit, and WeatherKit.
  • Left Wallpaper. A Lock Screen wallpaper that updates itself.
  • Shared Since. Sharing a habit or streak with a friend.

Pricing

Both apps have free tiers. HabitKit's Pro is a one-time purchase or subscription depending on platform; Left's Left+ is a one-time pay-what-you-want purchase with three price options.

How to choose

If your phone's job is "show me my habit grid and nothing else," HabitKit is hard to beat. If you want one app that holds your habits, your countdowns, and the time you have left — that's Left.
