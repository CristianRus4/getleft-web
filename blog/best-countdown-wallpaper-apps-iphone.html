Best Days Left and Countdown Wallpaper Apps for iPhone in 2026: 6 Compared

Updated October 24, 2026 · 8 min read

Checked 20 October 2026. Every competitor detail below comes from that app's US App Store page and Apple's public lookup data, fetched that day. Left details come from the app itself.

A days left wallpaper turns your Lock Screen into a countdown: the year as a grid of dots with today highlighted, your life in weeks, or the days until a trip. You see it every time you pick up your phone, without adding a widget. The trick is keeping it current. On iPhone, an app can't swap your wallpaper on its own; the apps below generate the image and then use a Shortcuts automation, which you set up once, to refresh it every day. Here are six worth comparing.

Disclosure: we make Left, so we have put it first. Here is why, and when another app suits you better.

  • Best free self-updating wallpaper for the year, your life or a countdown: Left.
  • Best for habits plus a year dot grid wallpaper: DailyHack.
  • Best for agenda-style Lock Screens with countdowns and days since: Calendarly.
  • Best for a schedule or to-do wallpaper with a life progress style: LockScreen Calendar Maker.
  • Best for many kinds of progress in one wallpaper app: One Dot.
  • Best for a minimal "days left in the year" wallpaper: Today: Year Calendar Wallpaper.

1. Left: Left Wallpaper

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone, and Left Wallpaper draws the same grids you see in the app onto your Lock Screen.

  • Modes: Life (your life in years, months or weeks), Year (in days, weeks or months), Month, Calendar (month, year, month blocks or year blocks) and Special Date, which counts down to any of your Ahead dates.
  • Style: pick a symbol, light or dark appearance, a theme color (including a custom one), the layout position and grid size, and a background photo from your library or Unsplash, with blur. A legend can show text such as "73 days left".
  • Updating: save your setup in Left, then build a shortcut with Left's Wallpaper action and Set Wallpaper Photo, and run it with a daily Time of Day automation at 12:01 AM. Left's own tutorial walks through each step, and so does our wallpaper shortcut guide.
  • Pairs with widgets: the same countdowns, year progress and life views are available as Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Best for: a free days left or countdown wallpaper that matches the rest of your setup, from one app.

Price: free to download, and Left Wallpaper is free. A Special Date wallpaper uses one of your Ahead dates; the free plan includes 2 Ahead dates, counted over every date you've ever created. Left Premium, a subscription or lifetime purchase, unlocks unlimited dates and more. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

Pros: free; life, year, month, calendar and countdown modes in one place; photo backgrounds; the same countdowns and grids as Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Cons: the Shortcuts setup takes a few minutes the first time; no agenda, to-do or calendar-event wallpaper; iPhone only, with iOS 26 or later.

Questions

How do I get a days left wallpaper on my iPhone?

Use an app that draws the wallpaper, then a Shortcuts automation to refresh it daily. In Left, set up Left Wallpaper, add Left's Wallpaper action and Set Wallpaper Photo to a shortcut, and run it with a Time of Day automation at 12:01 AM every day.

Can an iPhone wallpaper update itself every day?

Yes, through the Shortcuts app. An app can't change your wallpaper by itself, but a daily Shortcuts automation can generate a fresh image and set it as your wallpaper, so the count stays current.

Is there a free countdown wallpaper app for iPhone?

Yes. Left Wallpaper is free, including a Special Date mode that counts down to one of your Ahead dates (the free plan includes 2). Today: Year Calendar Wallpaper's Year wallpaper and One Dot's Life and Year modes are also free, per their listings.

Can I put my life in weeks on my Lock Screen?

Yes. Left Wallpaper's Life mode shows your life in years, months or weeks, and DailyHack, Calendarly, LockScreen Calendar Maker and One Dot list life calendar styles too.

Is a wallpaper better than a widget for a countdown?

A wallpaper fills the whole Lock Screen and leaves widget slots free; a widget keeps itself up to date without a Shortcuts automation. Left offers both, so you can use the wallpaper for the year and a widget for a specific date.
