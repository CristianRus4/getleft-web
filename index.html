FAQ

Questions, answered.

The basics on pricing, widgets, sharing, sync, and what Left does with your data.

Is Left free?
Yes. The core of Left is free — habits, streaks, countdowns, the Planner, Left Time views, and most widgets. Left+ unlocks special dates, a custom lifespan, and a few extras.
What's the difference between a habit and a streak?
A streak just measures how long you've kept something going since a start date. A habit asks you to check in on a schedule — daily, weekly, or custom — and counts the days you completed it. Both live inside the Since section.
Can I share countdowns and habits with friends?
Yes. Add a friend by username, then start a Joint Ahead (a shared countdown) or a Shared Since (a habit or streak you both see). Each of you keeps your own widget; updates sync through your Left account.
How do I add a Left widget to my Home Screen?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Left, pick a style and size, tap Add Widget, then long-press the widget and choose Edit Widget to configure what it shows.
What is Left Wallpaper?
Left Wallpaper generates a Lock Screen image that shows your time data — and an Apple Shortcut updates it automatically. You can blend it with a background photo or keep it minimal.
What does the Planner do?
Planner combines your habits, countdowns, Apple Calendar events, Reminders, HealthKit summaries, and WeatherKit into one scrollable timeline — plus its own widget.
How many widget styles does Left have?
Over 3,000 combinations across Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, and iPad. Dots, rings, bars, text, analog, dynamic — most can be customized with color and symbol.
Does Left sync across my devices?
Yes. Left uses iCloud sync between your iPhone and iPad. Shared habits and joint countdowns also sync through your Left account so friends stay in step.
