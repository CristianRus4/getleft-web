# Left Website Content Working Document

This is the working source for the public website copy. It captures the product truth, message hierarchy, tone, page structure, and draft copy to use in `index.html`.

## Core Idea

Left is the place to make every moment count.

Left is built on a powerful idea: your time left is limited, and seeing it changes how you relate to it. Left visualizes that time through widgets and symbol grids that dim as time passes, while helping people make the most of it with dates to look forward to, habits and streaks that build a better version of themselves, and Planner for managing the day-to-day.

Public copy should present these as one combined promise: visualize the time left and make the most of it.

## Product Summary

Left is a time awareness app for iPhone.

It visualises time as grids of symbols. Each unit of time is a dot or symbol. As time passes, elapsed units dim. The user can see the year, month, week, day, hour, minute, and their life as something tangible.

The app brings these surfaces together:

- Time Left: make the year, month, week, day, hour, minute, and life visible on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.
- Habits & Streaks: help users become a better version of themselves through routines, completions, and streaks that stay visible.
- Ahead: gives users dates to look forward to, count down to, and share with friends.
- Planner: brings the day together with habits, streaks, calendar events, reminders, weather, sleep, workouts, and a weekly agenda.
- More to explore: Friends, Left Wallpaper, You, Time Between, Games, Live Activities, templates, alternate icons, and other time-related tools.

## What Left Should Transmit

Left should feel like:

- A serious, well-made time awareness tool.
- A beautiful way to confront limited time without becoming morbid.
- A home-screen-first app that earns its place every day.
- A central hub for seeing time, acting on it, and becoming better.
- Useful, calm, intentional, and worth paying for.

Left should not feel like:

- A gimmick.
- A generic productivity coach.
- A motivational poster.
- A loud habit game.
- A dashboard full of clutter.
- A free utility with endless options and no taste.

The app can be slightly confrontational about time being limited, but it should not shame the user. It should say: your time is finite, here it is, now choose what matters.

## Message Hierarchy

The homepage should prioritise the product in this order:

1. Time Left.
2. Habits & Streaks.
3. Ahead.
4. Planner.
5. Secondary features: Friends, Wallpaper, You, Time Between, Games, Live Activities, templates, customization.

Reasoning:

- Widgets and Time Left are the strongest acquisition hook.
- Habits & Streaks and Ahead are the strongest ongoing value.
- Planner turns Left into a day-to-day time hub.
- Secondary features deepen the product but should not dilute the core story.

## Target User

Primary users:

- People who have become aware that time is limited.
- People in their late 20s to 40s who have had a moment of reckoning with time.
- Younger users attracted to the visual aesthetic of time grids and widgets.
- People who want to be more intentional without being lectured.
- People who value beautiful design and home-screen customization.
- People who want habits, streaks, and dates visible without opening an app constantly.
- People likely to share screenshots of their widgets.

Not the target:

- Users who only want the cheapest free tool.
- Users who want infinite customization without design taste.
- Users who want a loud gamified productivity system.
- Users who want a clinical calendar or task manager.

## Tone Of Voice

Use:

- Minimal.
- Direct.
- Considered.
- Slightly confronting.
- Human.
- Clear.
- Calm but not passive.

Avoid:

- Corporate productivity language.
- Overly soft wellness language.
- Hype.
- Generic app-store phrases.
- Long explanations.

Good language:

- `make every moment count`
- `time left`
- `limited time`
- `make time visible`
- `dates to look forward to`
- `habits that shape who you become`
- `streaks worth keeping`
- `your day, in view`
- `with friends`
- `Home Screen`, `Lock Screen`, `StandBy`

Avoid:

- `supercharge`
- `crush your goals`
- `unlock your potential`
- `ultimate productivity app`
- `never waste time again`
- `life-changing`

## Three-Word Framework

The old "Look. Plan. Do." should become:

> Notice. Act. Become.

Meaning:

- Notice: become aware of the time you have left.
- Act: choose dates, habits, and plans that make the time count.
- Become: use habits, streaks, and accountability to move toward a better version of yourself.

Alternative if needed:

- See. Choose. Become.
- Aware. Active. Better.
- Notice. Choose. Grow.

Recommended: `Notice. Act. Become.` It is clear, active, and has the right arc.

## Core Features

### 1. Time Left

Purpose:

- Main acquisition hook.
- The iconic feature.
- The clearest expression of Left's philosophy.

What to say:

- Left makes time visible.
- It shows the year, month, week, day, hour, minute, and life as dots or symbols.
- Passed time dims.
- Time left stays visible.
- Widgets live on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, and iPad.
- Over 20 widgets and many combinations of color, symbols, layouts, and content.

Draft copy:

> See the year, month, week, day, hour, minute, and your life as grids of symbols. The past dims. What remains stays visible on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### 2. Habits & Streaks

Purpose:

- Main retention feature with Ahead.
- Helps the user become a better version of themselves.
- Turns time awareness into repeated action.

What to say:

- Since contains habits and streaks.
- Streaks track how long something has lasted since a start date or reset.
- Habits ask for completion on a schedule.
- Schedules can be daily, multiple times per day, weekly, monthly, or custom.
- Progress can live in widgets.
- Habits and streaks can be shared with friends for accountability.
- Friends can send kudos and congratulations.

Draft copy:

> Since is where time becomes action. Track streaks you want to keep alive, build habits on your own schedule, and let a friend see your progress when accountability helps.

### 3. Ahead

Purpose:

- Main retention feature with Since.
- Gives users something to look forward to.
- Makes future moments visible.

What to say:

- Add future dates and bounded date ranges.
- Use for trips, birthdays, holidays, weddings, anniversaries, retirement, deadlines, and personal milestones.
- Syncs with Apple Calendar.
- Supports shared countdowns with friends.
- Countdowns can appear as widgets and Live Activities.

Draft copy:

> Add the dates that pull you forward: trips, birthdays, holidays, anniversaries, deadlines, or anything else worth counting down to. Keep them in widgets, import from Calendar, and add friends to joint special dates.

### 4. Planner

Purpose:

- Turns Left into a daily time hub.
- Helps users manage the day-to-day.
- Gives practical utility beyond passive widgets.

What to say:

- Planner combines habits, streaks, Ahead dates, Apple Calendar, Apple Reminders, weather, sleep, workouts, and a weekly agenda forecast.
- It is a time-first day view, not just another calendar.
- It helps users see what the day asks from them.

Draft copy:

> Planner brings your day into one view: Apple Reminders, calendar events, habits, streaks, weather, sleep, workouts, and more.

## Secondary Features

These belong in "More to explore".

### Friends

Public angle:

- Shared countdowns.
- Shared habits and streaks.
- Private accountability.
- Kudos and congratulations.
- No public feed.

Draft copy:

> Share a countdown, habit, or streak with the right person. Keep each other moving without turning it into a feed.

### Left Wallpaper

Public angle:

- Extends the time-left grid to the Lock Screen wallpaper.
- Updates automatically through Apple Shortcuts.

Draft copy:

> Turn your time-left grid into a Lock Screen wallpaper that updates as the days pass.

### You

Public angle:

- Personal profile and charts.
- Life progress, age, habits, countdowns, and personal stats.

Draft copy:

> See your life progress, age, habits, countdowns, and personal time patterns in one profile view.

### Time Between

Public angle:

- Utility for calculating duration between dates or times.

Draft copy:

> Calculate the exact distance between two dates or times.

### Games

Public angle:

- Small extras inside the app.
- Mention lightly so it does not distract from the time-awareness positioning.

Draft copy:

> Small built-in games and extras give Left more to explore without getting in the way.

### Live Activities

Public angle:

- Final countdowns and habit progress on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.

Draft copy:

> Get a persistent four-hour reminder for unfinished habits near the end of the day, or follow a final countdown or habit progress from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

## Homepage Structure

Recommended public structure:

1. Hero: make every moment count by seeing the time left.
2. Three-word framework: Notice. Act. Become.
3. Core features in priority order: Time Left, Habits & Streaks, Ahead, Planner.
4. More to explore: friends, wallpaper, You, Time Between, games, Live Activities, templates, and customization.
5. Manifesto quote: limited time, moments that matter, better self.
6. Templates: starter habits, streaks, and countdowns.
7. Social proof or photo section if the assets are strong.
8. FAQ: pricing, time-left calculation, Ahead, Since, Planner, sharing, sync.
9. Download CTA.
10. Footer.
