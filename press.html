Left — Press

Left is an iPhone app that makes time visible. It brings time-left widgets, countdowns, habits, streaks, planning, wallpapers, and friends into one focused app for seeing what is left, what is ahead, and what is worth keeping on track.

  • Time-left views for life, year, month, week, day, and hour.
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, and iPad widgets.
  • Ahead countdowns for trips, birthdays, deadlines, and shared dates.
  • Since habits and streaks, including shared accountability with friends.
  • Planner, Left Wallpaper, Live Activities, shortcuts, and smaller tools built around time awareness.
