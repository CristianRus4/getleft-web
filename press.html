Press kit

Left is an iPhone app that makes time visible. It brings time-left widgets, countdowns, habits, streaks, planning, wallpapers, and friends into one focused app for seeing what is left, what is ahead, and what is worth keeping on track.

  • Visualise time left in the app or widgets through symbols for life, year, month, week, day, hour, or any date.
  • Use more than 20 different widgets on Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, and more for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
  • Create countdowns for dates ahead, like trips, birthdays, deadlines, or any event from your calendar.
  • Track habits and streaks, including shared accountability with friends.
  • Use other tools like a weekly planner, automatic wallpapers, Live Activities, and more tools built around time awareness.
