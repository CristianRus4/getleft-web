About Left

Left is an iPhone app that turns the time you have into something you can see on your Home Screen. It combines a habit tracker, a countdown app, a year-progress view, and a life-in-weeks visualizer behind a single library of widgets — over 3,000 combinations across Home Screen, Lock Screen, StandBy, and iPad.

The app is built around a simple idea: most apps help you track time you've lost. Left helps you track the time you have left.

Features at a glance

  • Left Time — see how much is left in your life, year, month, week, day, and hour as widgets.
  • Ahead — countdowns to any future date, with Live Activities and Joint Ahead (shared countdowns with friends).
  • Since — habits and streaks in one feature area, with daily/weekly/custom schedules and Shared Since for accountability.
  • Planner — a single timeline that combines habits, countdowns, Apple Calendar, Reminders, HealthKit, and WeatherKit.
  • Left Wallpaper — a Lock Screen wallpaper that shows your time data and updates itself via Apple Shortcuts.
  • Friends — find a user by username, send a request, and unlock joint countdowns and shared streaks.
